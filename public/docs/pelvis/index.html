    Visualizing the Position of Major Blood Vessels

    Each of the vertebral levels in the abdomen is related to the origin of major blood vessels (Fig. 4.167):

    • The celiac trunk originates from the aorta at the upper border of the LI vertebra.
    • The superior mesenteric artery originates at the lower border of the LI vertebra.
    • The renal arteries originate at approximately the LII vertebra.
    • The inferior mesenteric artery originates at the LIII vertebra.
    • The aorta bifurcates into the right and left common iliac arteries at the level of the LIV vertebra.
    • The left and right common iliac veins join to form the inferior vena cava at the LV vertebral level.
    Vertebral Level Structure
    Upper border of LI Aorta
    Lower border of LI Celiac trunk
    LII Superior mesenteric artery / Approximate origin of renal artery
    LIII Inferior mesenteric artery

    臨床重點：了解主要血管與椎體平面的對應關係，對腹部大手術及血管介入手術的術前規劃至關重要。Aorta 在 LIV 處分叉為左右 common iliac arteries，是骨盆與下肢血管造影的重要標誌。

    Surface Anatomy（表面解剖）

    Using Abdominal Quadrants to Locate Major Viscera

    The abdomen can be divided into quadrants by a vertical median plane and a horizontal transumbilical plane, which passes through the umbilicus (Fig. 4.168):

    • The liver and gallbladder are in the right upper quadrant.
    • The stomach and spleen are in the left upper quadrant.
    • The cecum and appendix are in the right lower quadrant.
    • The end of the descending colon and sigmoid colon are in the left lower quadrant.

    臨床重點：

    • 大部分 liver 位於 right dome of the diaphragm 下方，深藏在 lower thoracic wall 之後。囑患者深吸氣時，liver 的下緣可沿 right costal margin 觸及，臨床上稱為 liver edge slipping sign。
    • McBurney’s point 是臨床上闌尾的常見體表投影點，位於 right anterior superior iliac spine 至 umbilicus 連線的上三分之一處。

    Most of the liver is under the right dome of the diaphragm and is deep to the lower thoracic wall. The inferior margin of the liver can be palpated descending below the right costal margin when a patient is asked to inhale deeply.

    On deep inspiration, the edge of the liver can be felt “slipping” under the palpating fingers placed under the costal margin.

    A common surface projection of the appendix is McBurney’s point, which is one-third of the way up along a line from the right anterior superior iliac spine to the umbilicus.

    Defining Surface Regions to Which Pain from the Gut is Referred

    The abdomen can be divided into nine regions by a midclavicular sagittal plane on each side and by the subcostal and intertubercular planes, which pass through the body transversely (Fig. 4.169). These planes separate the abdomen into:

    • Three central regions: epigastric region, umbilical region, pubic region
    • Three regions on each side: hypochondrium, flank, groin
    Region Referred Pain Origin
    Epigastric region Foregut (abdominal part)
    Umbilical region Midgut
    Pubic region Hindgut

    臨床重點：內臟痛（visceral pain）會依據相同的脊髓節段而被投射（referred）到特定的體表區域。Foregut 結構（如 stomach、duodenum、liver、pancreas）的疼痛投射至 epigastric region；Midgut（如 small intestine、cecum、appendix）的疼痛投射至 umbilical region；Hindgut（如 descending colon、sigmoid colon、rectum）的疼痛則投射至 pubic region。

    Pain from the abdominal part of the foregut is referred to the epigastric region, pain from the midgut is referred to the umbilical region, and pain from the hindgut is referred to the pubic region.

    Where to Find the Kidneys

    The kidneys project onto the back on either side of the midline and are related to the lower ribs (Fig. 4.170):

    • The left kidney is a little higher than the right and reaches as high as rib XI.
    • The superior pole of the right kidney reaches only as high as rib XII.
    • The lower poles of the kidneys occur around the level of the disc between the LIII and LIV vertebrae.
    • The hila of the kidneys and the beginnings of the ureters are at approximately the LI vertebra.
    • The ureters descend vertically anterior to the tips of the transverse processes of the lower lumbar vertebrae and enter the pelvis.

    臨床重點：臨床上以肋骨定位 kidney 位置時，應注意左右側高度差異（左側略高於右側）。Kidney 位置與 lower ribs 的關係在胸部或腹部外傷評估中非常重要，特別是 lower left rib fractures 可能暗示左側 kidney 或 spleen 受損。

    The ureters descend vertically anterior to the tips of the transverse processes of the lower lumbar vertebrae and enter the pelvis.

    Where to Find the Spleen

    The spleen projects onto the left side and back in the area of ribs IX to XI (Fig. 4.171). The spleen follows the contour of rib X and extends from the superior pole of the left kidney to just posterior to the midaxillary line.

    臨床重點：Spleen 的表面投影範圍從 left kidney 的 superior pole 延伸至 midaxillary line 後方。臨床上，left lower rib fractures 是脾臟受傷的重要指標，任何左側下肋骨骨折都應高度懷疑脾臟損傷的可能性，並進一步以超聲或 CT 評估。

    The spleen follows the contour of rib X and extends from the superior pole of the left kidney to just posterior to the midaxillary line.

    Clinical Cases（臨床案例）

    Case 1: TRAUMATIC RUPTURE OF THE DIAPHRAGM

    A 45-year-old man had mild epigastric pain, and a diagnosis of esophageal reflux was made. He was given appropriate medication, which worked well. However, at the time of the initial consultation, the family practitioner requested a chest radiograph, which demonstrated a prominent hump on the left side of the diaphragm and old rib fractures.

    The patient was recalled for further questioning. He was extremely pleased with the treatment he had been given for his gastroesophageal reflux, but was concerned about being recalled for further history and examination. During the interview, he revealed that he had previously been involved in a motorcycle accident and had undergone a laparotomy for a “rupture.” The patient did not recall what operation was performed, but was assured at the time that the operation was a great success.

    臨床重點：

    • 任何因機車事故導致的嚴重鈍性腹部創傷（blunt abdominal trauma），lower left-sided rib fractures 是極為重要的創傷指標，強烈暗示 spleen 受傷可能。
    • 回顧患者舊病歷發現，創傷當時已進行 splenectomy（脾臟切除術），但當時未發現 left hemidiaphragm dome 有輕微破裂。隨著時間推移，腸道逐漸通過裂孔進入胸腔，形成臨床上所見的「hump」影像。
    • 此類陳舊性創傷，患者若長期無症狀，預後通常良好，但仍需長期追蹤。

    Case 2: CHRONIC THROMBOSIS OF THE INFERIOR VENA CAVA

    A medical student was asked to inspect the abdomen of two patients. On the first patient he noted irregular veins radiating from the umbilicus. On the second patient he noted irregular veins, coursing in a caudal to cranial direction, over the anterior abdominal wall from the groin to the chest. He was asked to explain his findings and determine the significance of these features.

    Patient 1 — Radiating veins from umbilicus (Caput medusae):

    In the first patient the veins were draining radially away from the periumbilical region. In normal individuals, enlarged veins do not radiate from the umbilicus. In patients with portal hypertension the portal venous pressure is increased as a result of hepatic disease. Small collateral veins develop at and around the obliterated umbilical vein. These veins pass through the umbilicus and drain onto the anterior abdominal wall, forming a portosystemic anastomosis. The eventual diagnosis for this patient was cirrhosis of the liver.

    臨床重點：此種從臍部向四周輻射的靜脈曲張，臨床上稱為 Caput medusae（蛇髮女妖頭），是 portal hypertension 導致 portosystemic anastomosis 開放的特徵性表現，常見於 liver cirrhosis 患者。

    Patient 2 — Caudocranial draining veins (IVC obstruction):

    The finding of veins draining in a caudocranial direction on the anterior abdominal wall in the second patient is not typical for veins on the anterior abdominal wall. When veins are so prominent, it usually implies that there is an obstruction to the normal route of venous drainage and an alternative route has been taken.

    Typically, blood from the lower limbs and the retroperitoneal organs drains into the inferior vena cava and from here to the right atrium of the heart. This patient had a chronic thrombosis of the inferior vena cava, preventing blood from returning to the heart by the “usual” route.

    Blood from the lower limbs and the pelvis may drain via a series of collateral vessels, some of which include the superficial inferior epigastric veins, which run in the superficial fascia. These anastomose with the superior, superficial, and deep epigastric venous systems to drain into the internal thoracic veins, which in turn drain into the brachiocephalic veins and the superior vena cava.

    臨床重點：當 Inferior vena cava (IVC) 慢性血栓形成時，下肢及骨盆的靜脈血液無法經正常途徑回流心臟，必須繞道而行。Superficial inferior epigastric veins 與 epigastric venous systems 之間的吻合支（anastomoses）擴張形成替代途徑，最終經 internal thoracic veins 進入 superior vena cava 返回心臟。臨床上觀察到腹部靜脈血流方向由下而上（caudocranial）時，應高度懷疑 IVC obstruction。

    After the initial inferior vena cava thrombosis, the veins of the anterior abdominal wall and other collateral pathways hypertrophy to accommodate the increase in blood flow.

    Case 3: LIVER BIOPSY IN PATIENTS WITH SUSPECTED LIVER CIRRHOSIS

    A 55-year-old man developed severe jaundice and a massively distended abdomen. A diagnosis of cirrhosis of the liver was made, and further confirmatory tests demonstrated that the patient had significant ascites (free fluid within the peritoneal cavity). A liver biopsy was necessary to confirm the cirrhosis, but there was some debate about how this biopsy should be obtained (Fig. 4.172).

    Issue 1 — Impaired liver function:

    The liver function of patients with suspected liver disease is poor, as demonstrated by the patient’s jaundice—an inability to conjugate bilirubin. Importantly, because some liver products are blood-clotting factors involved in the clotting cascade, the blood-clotting ability of patients with severe liver disease is significantly impaired. These patients therefore have a high risk of bleeding.

    Issue 2 — Presence of ascites:

    Normally the liver rests against the lateral and anterior abdominal walls. This direct contact can be useful for care after a liver biopsy has been obtained. After the procedure, the patient lies over the region where the biopsy has been obtained and the weight of the liver stems any localized bleeding. When patients have significant ascites, the liver cannot be compressed against the walls of the abdomen and blood may pour freely into the ascitic fluid.

    臨床重點：在 cirrhosis 合併 ascites 的患者身上進行 liver biopsy，傳統經皮穿刺方式面臨兩大風險：

    1. 凝血功能障礙：肝臟無法製造足夠的 clotting factors，導致術後出血風險極高。
    2. Ascites 的存在：正常肝臟可被腹壁直接壓迫止血，但 ascites 患者肝臟漂浮於腹水中，無法有效壓迫止血，血液會自由流入 ascitic fluid 中。

    Transjugular approach — The solution:

    The patient was referred to the radiology department for a transjugular liver biopsy.

    The skin around the jugular vein in the neck was anesthetized. Access was obtained through insertion of a needle and a guidewire. The guidewire was advanced through the right internal jugular vein and into the right brachiocephalic vein. It entered the superior vena cava, was passed along the posterior wall of the atrium, and entered the superior aspect of the inferior vena cava. A catheter was inserted over the wire and directed into the right hepatic vein. Using a series of dilators, the hole was enlarged and a biopsy needle was placed over the wire and into the right hepatic vein. The liver was biopsied through the right hepatic vein and the biopsy sample was removed. A simple suture was used to close the internal jugular vein in the neck, and minor compression stemmed any blood flow.

    臨床重點：Transjugular liver biopsy 的優點在於：穿刺針經由 right hepatic vein 進入肝臟組織，若不慎穿透 liver capsule，血液會直接進入 hepatic vein 並立即回流至心臟，不會在腹腔內堆積。相較於傳統經皮穿刺，此法在凝血功能障礙及 ascites 患者中安全性顯著更高。

    Assuming that the biopsy needle does not penetrate the liver capsule, it is not important how much the patient bleeds from the liver, because this bleeding will enter the hepatic vein and is immediately returned to the circulation.

    Case 4: HODGKIN’S LYMPHOMA

    A 30-year-old man had a diffuse and poorly defined epigastric mass. Further examination revealed asymmetrical scrotal enlargement.

    As part of her differential diagnosis, the resident considered the possibility that the man had testicular cancer with regional abdominal para-aortic nodal involvement (the lateral aortic, or lumbar, nodes).

    A primary testicular neoplasm is the most common tumor in men between the ages of 25 and 34 and accounts for between 1% and 2% of all malignancies in men. A family history of testicular cancer and maldescent of the testis are strong predisposing factors.

    Spread of the tumor is typically to the lymph node chains that drain the testes.

    The testes develop from structures adjacent to the renal vessels in the upper abdomen, between the transversalis fascia and the peritoneum. They normally migrate through the inguinal canals into the scrotum just before birth. The testes take with them their arterial supply, their venous drainage, their nerve supply, and their lymphatics.

    臨床重點：Testes 從腎血管附近的結構發育而來，伴隨著它們的 arterial supply（testicular artery）、venous drainage（testicular vein）、nerve supply 和 lymphatics。淋巴引流最終進入 lateral aortic (lumbar) lymph nodes，因此睪丸腫瘤的淋巴轉移主要發生在腹主動脈旁淋巴結。

    A computed tomography scan revealed a para-aortic lymph node mass in the upper abdomen and enlarged lymph nodes throughout the internal and common iliac lymph node chains.

    Assuming the scrotal mass was a carcinoma of the testes, which would normally drain into the lateral aortic (lumbar) nodes in the upper abdomen, it would be very unusual for iliac lymphadenopathy to be present.

    Further examination of the scrotal mass:

    A transillumination test of the scrotum on the affected side was positive. An ultrasound scan revealed normal right and left testes and a large fluid collection around the right testis. A diagnosis of a right-sided hydrocele was made.

    Scrotal masses are common in young males, and determining the exact anatomical site of the scrotal mass is of utmost clinical importance. Any mass that arises from the testis should be investigated to exclude testicular cancer. Masses that arise from the epididymis and scrotal lesions, such as fluid (hydrocele) or hernias, are also clinically important but are not malignant.

    臨床重點：Hydrocele（陰囊積液）在超聲檢查下可見睪丸周圍有 fluid collection，透光試驗（transillumination）呈陽性。Epididymal cysts（副睪囊腫）也可在超聲下輕易辨識。臨床上區分睪丸腫瘤與 non-malignant scrotal lesions（如 hydrocele、hernia）非常重要，因為兩者的治療策略截然不同。

    A diagnosis of lymphoma was suspected.

    Lymphoma is a malignant disease of lymph nodes. Most lymphomas are divided into two specific types, namely Hodgkin’s lymphoma and non-Hodgkin’s lymphoma. If caught early the prognosis following radical chemotherapy is excellent.

    Biopsy approach:

    The patient underwent a biopsy, which was performed from the posterior approach. He was placed in the prone position in the computed tomography (CT) scanner. A fine needle with a special cutting device was used to obtain a lymph node sample. A left-sided approach was used because the inferior vena cava is on the right side and the nodes were in the para-aortic regions (i.e., the biopsy needle would have to pass between the inferior vena cava and the aorta from a posterior approach, which is difficult). The skin was anesthetized using local anesthetic at the lateral border of the quadratus lumborum muscle. The needle was angled at approximately 45° within the quadratus lumborum muscle and entered the retroperitoneum to lie beside the left-sided para-aortic lymph nodes.

    臨床重點：在 CT 導引下執行 para-aortic lymph node biopsy 時，左側進針路徑優於右側，因為 IVC 位於右側，若從右側進針，穿刺針必須穿越 IVC 與 aorta 之間的間隙，技術難度極高且風險大。以 quadratus lumborum muscle 的外側緣為定位點，針身以約 45 度角進入 retroperitoneum，可安全到達 left para-aortic lymph nodes。

    Because this procedure is performed using CT guidance, the operator can advance the needle slowly, taking care not to “hit” other retroperitoneal structures.

    A good biopsy was obtained and the diagnosis was Hodgkin’s lymphoma. The patient underwent chemotherapy and 2 years later is in full remission and leads an active life.

    Case 5: INGUINAL HERNIA

    A 35-year-old man had a soft mass approximately 3 cm in diameter in the right scrotum. The diagnosis was a right indirect inguinal hernia.

    What were the examination findings?

    The mass was not tender and the physician was not able to “get above it.” The testes were felt separate from the mass, and a transillumination test (in which a bright light is placed behind the scrotum and the scrotal sac is viewed from the front) was negative. (A positive test occurs when the light penetrates through the scrotum.) When the patient stood up, a positive cough “impulse” was felt within the mass.

    After careful and delicate maneuvering, the mass could be massaged into the inguinal canal, so emptying from the scrotum. When the massaging hand was removed, the mass recurred in the scrotum.

    臨床重點：

    • Indirect inguinal hernia：經 deep inguinal ring 進入 inguinal canal，再從 superficial inguinal ring（位於 external oblique aponeurosis）穿出，沿 spermatic cord 進入陰囊。Hernia sac 位於 pubic tubercle 的上內側。
    • Direct inguinal hernia：直接穿過 inguinal canal 的後壁，不會沿 inguinal canal 全程走行。若夠大，也可能通過 superficial inguinal ring 進入陰囊。
    • 兩者皆可觸及 cough impulse，但 indirect hernia 通常可被「推回」至 inguinal canal，而 direct hernia 的還納通常較困難。

    An indirect inguinal hernia enters the inguinal canal through the deep inguinal ring. It passes through the inguinal canal to exit through the superficial inguinal ring in the aponeurosis of the external oblique muscle. The hernia sac lies superior and medial to the pubic tubercle and enters into the scrotum within the spermatic cord.

    A direct inguinal hernia passes directly through the posterior wall of the inguinal canal. It does not pass down the inguinal canal. If large enough, it may pass through the superficial inguinal ring and into the scrotum.

    Case 6: URETERIC STONE

    A 25-year-old man developed severe pain in the left lower quadrant of his abdomen. The pain was diffuse and relatively constant but did ease for short periods of time. On direct questioning the patient indicated that the pain was in the inguinal region and radiated posteriorly into his left infrascapular region (loin). A urine dipstick was positive for blood (hematuria). A diagnosis of a ureteric stone (calculus) was made.

    The patient’s initial infrascapular pain, which later radiated to the left groin, relates to passage of the ureteric stone along the ureter.

    The origin of the pain relates to ureteral distention.

    A series of peristaltic waves along the ureter transport urine along the length of the ureter from the kidney to the bladder. As the ureteric stone obstructs the kidney, the ureter becomes distended, resulting in an exacerbation of the pain. The peristaltic waves are superimposed upon the distention, resulting in periods of exacerbation and periods of relief.

    臨床重點：Ureteric stone 引起的 renal colic 具特徵性的「陣發性加劇」模式（waxing and waning），是因為 peristaltic waves 叠加在 ureteral distention 上，造成規律的疼痛週期。疼痛起源於 ureteral distention，而非 stone 直接刺激。

    The pain is referred.

    The visceral afferent (sensory) nerve fibers from the ureter pass into the spinal cord, entering the first and second lumbar segments of the spinal cord. Pain is thus referred to cutaneous regions innervated by somatic sensory nerves from the same spinal cord levels.

    臨床重點：Ureter 的內臟感覺神經纖維（visceral afferent fibers）進入 L1-L2 脊髓節段，因此 ureteric stone 的疼痛會被投射（referred）到由 L1-L2 皮節神經支配的體表區域——即 inguinal region 和 loin（infrascapular region）。

    The patient was investigated by a CT scan.

    Traditionally patients are investigated with a plain radiograph to look for the radiopaque stone (90% of renal stones are radiopaque).

    An ultrasound scan may be useful to assess for pelvicaliceal dilatation and may reveal stones at the pelviureteral junction or the vesicoureteric junction. Ultrasound is also valuable for assessing other causes of obstruction (e.g., tumors at and around the ureteric orifices in the bladder).

    Usually an intravenous urogram would be carried out to enable assessment of the upper urinary tracts and precise location of the stone.

    Not infrequently, CT scans of the abdomen are also obtained. These scans not only give information about the kidneys, ureters, and bladder but also show the position of the stone and other associated pathology.

    If this patient’s infrascapular pain was on the right and predominantly within the right lower abdomen, appendicitis would also have to be excluded. A CT scan would enable differentiation of appendicitis and urinary colic.

    Case 7: INTRAABDOMINAL ABSCESS

    A 27-year-old woman was admitted to the surgical ward with appendicitis. She underwent an appendectomy. It was noted at operation that the appendix had perforated and there was pus within the abdominal cavity. The appendix was removed and the stump tied. The abdomen was washed out with warm saline solution. The patient initially made an uneventful recovery, but by day 7 she had become unwell, with pain over her right shoulder and spiking temperatures.

    This patient had developed an intraabdominal abscess.

    Any operation on the bowel may involve peritoneal contamination with fecal contents and fecal flora. This may not be appreciated at the time of the operation. Over the postoperative period an inflammatory reaction ensued and an abscess cavity developed, filling with pus. Typically, the observation chart revealed a “swinging” pyrexia (fever).

    The most common sites for abscess to develop are the pelvis and the hepatorenal recess.

    臨床重點：腸道手術後形成的 腹腔膿腫（intraabdominal abscess），最常見的位置是 骨盆腔 和 hepatorenal recess（又稱 Morison’s pouch）。臨床上的 “swinging pyrexia”（間歇性高燒）是膿腫形成的特徵性表現。

    When a patient is in the supine position, the lowest points in the abdominal and pelvic cavities are the posterior superior aspect of the peritoneal cavity (the hepatorenal recess) and, in women, the recto-uterine pouch (pouch of Douglas).

    The shoulder pain suggested that the abscess was in the hepatorenal recess and that the pain was referred from the diaphragm.

    The motor and sensory innervation of the diaphragm is from nerves C3 to C5. The somatic pain sensation from the parietal peritoneum covering the undersurface of the diaphragm is carried into the spinal cord by the phrenic nerve (C3 to C5) and is interpreted by the brain as coming from skin over the shoulder—a region supplied by other somatic sensory nerves entering the same levels of the spinal cord as those from the diaphragm.

    臨床重點：Phrenic nerve（C3-C5）同時支配 diaphragm 的運動與感覺。Diaphragm 下面的 parietal peritoneum 受到刺激時，疼痛會被 referred 到由相同脊髓節段（C3-C5）支配的體表區域——即 shoulder（由 supraclavicular nerves 支配）。因此，right shoulder pain 可能是 right subphrenic abscess 或 hepatorenal recess abscess 的間接證據。

    A chest radiograph demonstrated elevation of the right hemidiaphragm.

    This elevation of the right hemidiaphragm was due to the pus tracking from the hepatorenal space around the lateral and anterior aspect of the liver to sit on top of the liver in a subphrenic position. An ultrasound scan demonstrated this collection of fluid. The abscess cavity could be clearly seen by placing the ultrasound probe between ribs XI and XII. The inferior border of the right lower lobe lies at rib X in the midaxillary line. When the probe is placed between ribs XI and XII the ultrasound waves pass between the intercostal muscles and the parietal pleura laterally on the chest wall, and continue through the parietal pleura overlying the diaphragm into the cavity of the abscess, which lies below the diaphragm.

    臨床重點：超聲檢查時，將探頭放置於 第 XI 和 XII 肋骨之間（midaxillary line），超聲波穿過肋間肌肉和 parietal pleura，再穿透覆蓋在 diaphragm 上的 parietal pleuria，即可清晰顯示存在於 diaphragm 下的膿腫腔。此位置的超聲路徑可避開 lung tissue（right lower lobe 的下緣在 midaxillary line 為第 X 肋骨），是最理想的下胸部/上腹部超聲視窗。

    Drainage was not by an intercostal route. Instead, using CT guidance and local anesthesia, a subcostal drain was established and 1 liter of pus was removed (Fig. 4.173). It is important to bear in mind that placing a drain through the pleural cavity into the abdominal cavity effectively allows intraabdominal pus to pass into the thoracic cavity, and that this may produce an empyema (pus in the pleural space).

    臨床重點：Subphrenic abscess 的引流應避免經肋間路徑（intercostal route），因為穿刺針必須穿越 pleural cavity，可能將腹腔內的膿汁帶入胸腔，引發 empyema（膿胸）。Subcostal approach（沿肋骨下緣）可有效避開胸膜腔，是更安全的引流途徑。

    The patient made a slow and uneventful recovery.

    Case 8: COMPLICATIONS OF AN ABDOMINOPERINEAL RESECTION

    A 45-year-old man developed a low-grade rectal carcinoma just above the anorectal margin. He underwent an abdominoperineal resection of the tumor and was left with a left lower abdominal colostomy. Unfortunately, the man’s wife left him for a number of reasons, including lack of sexual desire. He “turned to drink” and over the ensuing years developed cirrhosis. He was brought into the emergency room with severe bleeding from enlarged veins around his colostomy. An emergency transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt (TIPS) was created, which stopped all bleeding (Figs. 4.174 and 4.175). He is now doing well in a rehabilitation program.

    A colostomy was necessary because of the low site of the tumor.

    Carcinoma of the colon and rectum usually develops in older patients, but some people do get tumors early in life. Most tumors develop from benign polyps, which undergo malignant change. As the malignancy develops it invades through the wall of the bowel and then metastasizes to local lymphatics. The tumor extends within the wall for a few centimeters above and below its origin. Lymphatic spread is to local and regional lymph nodes and then to the pre-aortic lymph node chain. These drain eventually into the thoracic duct.

    When this man was assessed for surgery, the tumor was so close to the anal margin that resection of the sphincters was necessary to be certain that the tumor margins were clear. The bowel cannot be joined to the anus without sphincters because the patient would be fecally incontinent. At surgery the tumor was excised, including the locoregional lymph node chains and the peritumoral fat around the rectum.

    The free end of the sigmoid colon was brought through a hole in the anterior abdominal wall. The bowel was then carefully sutured to the anterior abdominal wall to allow placement of a bag to collect the feces. This is a colostomy.

    臨床重點：當 rectal carcinoma 位置極低（close to the anal margin）時，手術必須切除 anal sphincters，術後將 sigmoid colon 的遊離端經 anterior abdominal wall 拉出並縫合固定於腹壁，即形成 colostomy（人工肛門）。這類患者術後需終身使用糞袋收集糞便，多數患者經康復訓練後可適應正常生活。

    Contrary to their usual immediate negative reaction to having a bag on the anterior abdominal wall, most patients cope extremely well, especially if they have been cured of cancer.

    Bleeding from colostomy — The complication:

    Years after his surgery, the patient developed cirrhosis of the liver and portal hypertension. Enlarged veins (varices) developed around his colostomy stoma as a result of portosystemic venous collaterals formation. These varices were prone to bleeding, which in this case was severe.

    An emergency transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt (TIPS) was created. This procedure involves placing a shunt between the portal vein and the hepatic vein within the liver substance, thereby reducing portal pressure and decompressing the varices.

    臨床重點：TIPS (Transjugular Intrahepatic Portosystemic Shunt) 是治療 portal hypertension 併發 variceal bleeding 的微創介入手術。經 jugular vein 穿刺置管，將支架置於 portal vein 與 hepatic vein之間，建立 liver 內的人工分流通道，直接降低 portal venous pressure，進而減輕 peristomal varices（結腸造口周圍靜脈曲張）的壓力並止血。

    Case 8 (續)

    Pelvic 神經損傷與門脈高壓

    此患者的 pelvic nerves 受到損傷。廣泛性骨盆手術剝離損傷了支配陰莖勃起所需的 pelvic parasympathetic nerve supply。不幸的是，這點並未充分告知患者，某種程度上導致了其婚姻關係的破裂。任何骨盆內的廣泛性手術都可能損傷支配陰莖或陰蒂的神經，進而影響sexual function。

    臨床重點：骨盆手術前應充分告知患者可能出現性功能障礙的風險，特別是涉及直腸、子宮或膀胱根除術時。

    此患者同時有stomatal varices 出血問題。由於患者出現嚴重的酗酒問題，肝臟发展为肝硬化 (cirrhosis)，正常肝臟結構受損。這導致了門靜脈 (portal vein) 的壓力升高，形成門脈高壓 (portal hypertension)。

    在門脈高壓患者中，門靜脈系統與體循環靜脈之間會形成細小的吻合血管 (anastomoses)。這些門體循環吻合 (portosystemic anastomoses) 通常影響不大；然而，在胃食道接合處 (gastroesophageal junction)，它們位於黏膜下層 (submucosal) 及黏膜層 (mucosal) 位置，容易受到創傷。即使是輕微的外傷也可能導致大出血，甚至因失血過多而死亡。這些靜脈曲張 (varices) 需要緊急處理，包括注射硬化劑 (sclerosant substances)、結紮術 (banding) 甚至手術結紮 (surgical ligation)。

    臨床重點：肝硬化合併門脈高壓患者出現嘔血或黑便時，應高度懷疑胃食道靜脈曲張破裂出血，需立即搶救。

    幸運的是，其他大多數門體循環吻合的臨床意義相對較小。在結腸造口 (colostomy) 患者中，來自大腸靜脈（門靜脈系統引流）與腹壁前側皮膚靜脈（體循環靜脈）之間可能形成細小靜脈。若這些靜脈因門脈高壓而擴大，在糞便通過colostomy 時容易受到創傷，若受損則可能導致大出血。

    門脈壓力降低術

    為了降低此患者的門靜脈壓力，醫師考慮了多種手術方式，包括將門靜脈側壁縫合至下腔靜脈 (inferior vena cava)（門腔靜脈分流術 (portacaval shunt)）以及將脾靜脈 (splenic vein) 縫合至腎靜脈 (renal vein)（脾腎靜脈分流術 (splenorenal shunt)）。然而，這些手術需要大型腹部切口且極為複雜。作為替代方案，決定採用經頸靜脈肝內門體循環分流術 (transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt, TIPS)。

    經頸靜脈肝內門體循環分流術是一項相對新穎的技術，可在局部麻醉下進行。採用右側頸內靜脈 (internal jugular vein) 途徑，將長針經由頸內靜脈、上腔靜脈 (superior vena cava)、右心房 (right atrium) 進入下腔靜脈。接著進行右肝靜脈 (right hepatic vein) 插管，並使用特殊導絲將針穿過肝實質，直接進入門靜脈右支 (right branch of the portal vein)。沿導絲送入小球囊，穿過肝實質後充氣擴張。移除球囊後，在肝臟內放置金屬支架 (metallic stent)（柔性金屬管）以維持通道暢通。此時，血液可自由從門靜脈流入右肝靜脈，形成門體循環分流。

    臨床重點：TIPS 術後需定期追蹤肝功能及分流通暢度，並注意可能併發的肝性腦病變。

    此手術後，患者的門靜脈系統壓力降低至與體循環靜脈壓力相近，減少了portosystemic anastomoses（即結腸造口處）出血的風險。

    Carcinoma of the Head of the Pancreas（胰頭癌）

    一位52歲女性因持續疲倦及嘔吐就診。醫師檢查發現，與先前就診時相比，患者體重明顯下降，並出現黃疸 (jaundice)。腹部觸診時，在右季肋部肝緣下方可觸及一個边界清楚、直徑約10公分的圓形腫塊（圖4.176）。

    臨床診斷：胰頭癌

    臨床重點：僅凭三個臨床徵象（體重下降、黃疸、可觸及的膽囊）即可作出胰頭癌的臨床診斷，對於有經驗的臨床醫師而言是可能的。

    阻塞發生在遠端膽管

    當患者出現黃疸時，原因包括：紅血球過度分解（肝前性黃疸 (prehepatic jaundice)）、肝臟衰竭（肝性黃疸 (hepatic jaundice)）以及肝後性原因，後者包括膽道系統任一部位的阻塞。

    嘔吐與腫瘤位置的關係

    嘔吐和體重減輕（惡病質 (cachexia)）在惡性腫瘤患者中並不少見。胰頭 (head of pancreas) 位於十二指腸 (duodenum) 曲內，主要緊鄰十二指腸降部 (descending part of the duodenum)。胰頭的任何腫塊都可能增大並包繞或侵犯十二指腸。不幸的是，此患者的腫瘤確實發生了這種情況，造成幾乎完全的腸道阻塞。進一步詢問患者後發現，她每餐飯後不久就會嘔吐未消化的食物。

    CT（掃描顯示的進一步併發症）

    胰頭及胰頸區域有複雜的解剖結構，可能受到惡性疾病的侵犯。CT 掃描確認胰頭處有腫塊，侵犯了十二指腸降部。腫塊延伸至胰頸 (neck of pancreas)，阻塞了膽總管和胰管 (pancreatic duct)。在後方，腫瘤直接侵犯了脾靜脈 (splenic vein) 和腸繫膜上靜脈 (superior mesenteric vein) 匯合成的門靜脈 (portal venous confluence)，形成了一系列胃靜脈曲張 (gastric varices)、脾靜脈曲張 (splenic varices) 和小腸靜脈曲張 (small bowel varices)。

    此患者接受了緩解性化學治療，但在7個月後去世。

    Case 10

    Caval Obstruction（下腔靜脈阻塞）

    一位62歲男性因雙腿腫脹及大型左側精索靜脈曲張 (varicocele)（左側睪丸周圍及左側蔓狀靜脈叢 (pampiniform plexus) 靜脈擴大紆曲）至急診就診。

    該患者已知患有左側腎細胞癌 (left renal cell carcinoma)，預定於隔週進行手術治療。

    臨床重點：腎細胞癌的生物學行為具有特徵性，了解其生長模式對於解釋臨床症狀至關重要。

    從解剖學角度，可以通過了解腫瘤的生物學特性，將所有發現與腎細胞癌聯繫起來。

    腎細胞癌傾向於穩定且可預測地生長。通常，當腫瘤小於3至4公分時，仍局限於腎臟內。較大的腫瘤則可能長入腎靜脈 (renal vein)、下腔靜脈甚至右心房 (right atrium)，並可穿過心臟進入肺動脈 (pulmonary artery)。

    腫瘤長入腎靜脈

    隨著腫瘤長入腎靜脈，它阻斷了所有引流至腎靜脈的分支靜脈，其中最大的是左睪丸靜脈 (left testicular vein)。左睪丸靜脈的阻塞導致左側睪丸周圍靜脈擴張（形成精索靜脈曲張）。

    腿部腫脹的原因

    雙腿腫脹是由下腔靜脈阻塞所致。腫瘤沿腎靜脈生長，進入下腔靜脈向心臟方向延伸。腎腫瘤可能快速生長；此案例中，腫瘤迅速長入下腔靜脈並造成阻塞。這增加了下肢靜脈的壓力，導致腫脹及腳踝凹陷性水腫 (pitting edema)。

    臨床重點：此患者在手術中不幸死亡。腫瘤組織的「舌狀」延伸長入了下腔靜脈。手術時，初始剝離將腎臟從血管蒂游離，但一大塊腫瘤組織在下腔靜脈內脫落。腫瘤栓子通過右心房和右心室，阻塞了肺動脈。術中無法清除，患者最終死亡。

    Diverticular Disease（憩室疾病）

    一位65歲男性商人因嚴重的下腹部疼痛至急診就診，疼痛主要位於中線及左側。他同時有疼痛放射至左側腰背部，並注意到排尿時有排氣和糞便殘渣。

    對其腹部和骨盆進行了CT掃描（圖4.177）。

    CT掃描顯示左側髂窩 (iliac fossa) 有液體聚積（可能為骨盆腔膿瘍 (pelvic abscess)）。與此液體聚積相關的是乙狀結腸 (sigmoid colon) 腸壁明顯增厚，以及遍佈乙狀結腸的多發小憩室 (diverticula)。膀胱內有氣體。左側輸尿管 (ureter) 及左側腎盞系統 (pelvicalyceal system) 發現阻塞。

    臨床重點：排氣和糞便殘渣隨尿液排出（氣尿 (pneumaturia) 和糞尿 (fecaluria)）是腸道與膀胱之間形成異常連通（瘻管 (fistula)）的特徵性表現，常見於憩室炎併發膀胱侵犯。

    緊急手術

    患者接受了緊急手術。外科醫師通過正中切口進入腹腔時，發現左側髂窩的組織顯著發炎。外科醫師用手游離乙狀結腸，進入一個腔隙，CT所示的膿疡處有「呼嘯」狀膿液湧出。膿液被沖洗並引流。乙狀結腸明顯增厚發炎，黏連於膀胱頂部 (dome of the bladder)。細心手指剝離後發現膀胱頂部有一小穿孔，允許糞便物質和氣體進入膀胱，產生了患者的氣尿和糞尿症狀。進行了乙狀結腸切除術 (sigmoid colectomy)。直腸殘端被縫合封閉，降結腸 (descending colon) 通過腹壁前側引出形成結腸造口 (colostomy)。膀胱留置導尿管，膀胱頂部的小孔被縫合封閉。

    患者在加護病房經歷了艱難的術後恢復過程，持續發燒並敗血症。結腸造口功能良好。

    超聲波檢查顯示左腎持續擴張，患者接受了腎造口術 (nephrostomy)。在超聲波引導下，將引流導管通過左側腎皮質 (renal cortex) 置入腎盂 (renal pelvis)。最初從腎道引流出大量膿液；然而24小時後，尿液自由流出。

    臨床重點：左側遠端輸尿管周圍的炎症可能是造成阻塞的原因。也可能發生了小的輸尿管穿孔 (ureteric perforation)，使得細菌進入泌尿系統。

    患者恢復順利，腎功能恢復正常後出院。

    數週後門診回診時，患者不願繼續使用結腸造口袋。經討論後，手術計劃「重新接通」患者。

    手術中，游離了結腸造口並識別出直腸殘端。然而，腸道兩端之間有明顯距離。為了能夠縫合腸道，將降結腸從後腹壁游離。進行了腸道吻合術 (anastomosis)，患者一週後出院，目前情況良好。

    Case 12

    Endoleak after Endovascular Repair of Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm（腹主動脈瘤血管內修復後的內漏）

    一位72歲男性因腹主動脈瘤 (abdominal aortic aneurysm)（腎下腹主動脈擴張）被送至急診。主動脈瘤直徑達10公分，經與患者討論後安排修復手術。

    腹主動脈瘤的治療多年來一直是手術方法，切除擴張的主動脈並植入人工血管移植片 (graft)。現代的替代方案是從血管內放置移植片襯附於瘤壁（血管內動脈瘤修復術 (endovascular aneurysm repair)）。此技術中，外科醫師游離股總動脈 (femoral artery) 並做一小切口。移植片被壓縮在導管 (catheter) 內，導管經股動脈、髂動脈系統進入遠端腹主動脈。移植片可在主動脈內釋放，有效地襯附主動脈壁，防止主動脈瘤進一步擴張。

    臨床重點：血管內修復術後仍需定期影像追蹤，以早期發現內漏或其他併發症。

    偶爾，襯附後的动脉瘤在植入血管內移植片後可能繼續增大，需要找出原因。

    超聲波都卜勒檢查和CT掃描顯示，在血管內襯附物與主動脈瘤壁之間有血流存在。

    出血的可能來源

    移植片通常起始於腎動脈 (renal arteries) 水平以下，分為兩支延伸至髂總動脈 (common iliac arteries)。主動脈瘤可能繼續從移植片與瘤壁之間的任何血管獲得血液供應。這些血管包括腰動脈 (lumbar arteries) 和下腸繫膜動脈 (inferior mesenteric artery)。有趣的是，血液通常從腹主動脈流入下腸繫膜動脈和腰動脈；然而，當移植片植入後血流動力學發生變化，血液可能通過這些分支以相反方向流動，導致主動脈瘤增大。

    血液流動是從腸繫膜上動脈 (superior mesenteric artery) 進入主動脈瘤囊 (aneurysm sac)。

    在移植片水平以上，腸繫膜上動脈正常起源。從右結腸動态 (right colic artery) 和中結腸動态 (middle colic artery) 分支發出的結腸邊緣分支，於脾曲 (splenic flexure) 區域與下腸繫膜動态的邊緣分支吻合（可形成增粗的Drummond 邊緣動态 (marginal artery of Drummond)）。在這種情況下，血液逆流進入下腸繫膜動态，充盈主動脈瘤囊，使其保持壓力並繼續擴大。

    臨床重點：腸繫膜上動脈與下腸繫膜動态之間的側枝循環在這種情況下成為了持續灌注主動脈瘤囊的途徑。

    腹腔鏡下結紮下腸繫膜動態後，主動脈瘤停止擴大。在接下來的6個月中，主動脈瘤收縮。患者目前健康良好，腹股溝有兩個小疤痕。

    Case 13

    Metastatic Lesions in the Liver（肝臟轉移性病灶）

    一位44歲女性因腳趾發現黑色素瘤 (melanoma)，接受了系列檢查。

    黑色素瘤（正式名稱為惡性黑色素瘤 (malignant melanoma)）是一種侵襲性強的皮膚癌，可轉移至淋巴結和體內多個器官。其惡性潛能取決於細胞結構和皮膚穿透深度。

    患者足部發生了惡性黑色素瘤，轉移至腹股溝淋巴結 (lymph nodes of the groin)。進行了腹股溝淋巴結切除術 (inguinal lymph node resection)；然而，後續影像檢查發現患者肝臟右葉有兩個轉移性病灶。

    外科醫師和內科醫師考慮了切除這些病灶的可能性。

    CT掃描顯示病灶位於肝臟第V和第VI段（圖4.178）。

    臨床重點：肝臟的分段解剖對於制定切除手術計畫至關重要。 Couinaud 分段法是臨床上最常用的肝臟功能分區方法。

    手術包括識別門靜脈 (portal vein) 和左右肝管 (hepatic ducts) 匯合處。將肝臟沿肝中靜脈 (middle hepatic vein) 的想像主平面分開。結紮主肝管和膽管分支，成功進行了右肝切除術 (right hepatectomy)。

    殘留的肝段包括肝左葉。

    患者接受了第V、VI、VII、VIII段的手術切除。殘留的肝段包括IVa、IVb、I、II和III段。重要的是要記住，肝臟葉 (lobes) 與肝臟體積並不完全對應。肝左葉僅包含第II和III段。肝右葉包含第IV、V、VI、VII和VIII段。因此，在規劃手術分段切除時，橫截面影像至關重要。

    Conceptual Overview — Pelvis and Perineum

    General Description

    骨盆 (Pelvis) 和會陰 (Perineum) 是與pelvic bones 和脊椎末端相關的相互連繫區域。骨盆分為兩個區域：

    • 與髂骨上部和下腰椎相關的上方區域稱為假骨盆 (false pelvis / greater pelvis)，通常被視為腹部的一部分（圖5.1）。
    • 與髂骨下部、骶骨 (sacrum) 和尾骨 (coccyx) 相關的區域稱為真骨盆 (true pelvis / lesser pelvis)，具有骨盆入口和出口。

    由真骨盆圍成的碗狀骨盆腔 (pelvic cavity) 包括骨盆入口 (pelvic inlet)、骨盆壁和骨盆底 (pelvic floor)。此腔隙向上與腹腔 (abdominal cavity) 延續，容納泌尿系統、胃腸系統和生殖系統的器官。

    會陰（圖5.1）位於骨盆底下方；其邊界形成骨盆出口 (pelvic outlet)。會陰包含外生殖器 (external genitalia) 以及泌尿生殖系統 (genitourinary system) 和胃腸系統的外部開口。

    Functions

    Contains and Supports the Bladder, Rectum, Anal Canal, and Reproductive Tracts

    在骨盆腔內，膀胱 (bladder) 位於前側，直腸 (rectum) 位於後側正中線。

    當膀胱充盈時，向上擴展進入腹部。它由鄰近的pelvic bone 結構和骨盆底 (pelvic floor) 支撐。尿道 (urethra) 穿過骨盆底進入會陰，在女性中，尿道在此向外開口（圖5.2A）；在男性中，尿道進入陰莖根部（圖5.2B）。

    直腸在SIII 椎體水平與乙狀結腸 (sigmoid colon) 連續，延伸至肛管 (anal canal)，肛管穿過骨盆底向會陰開口。肛管在直腸上向後成角。此彎曲由骨盆底肌肉維持，在排便時放鬆。當肛管和尿道各自穿過骨盆底時，都有骨骼肌括約肌 (skeletal muscle sphincter) 與之相伴。

    臨床重點：骨盆底肌肉在維持尿失禁和糞便控制中起關鍵作用。任何導致骨盆底損傷的狀況（如分娩、神經損傷或手術）都可能導致失禁。

    骨盆腔容納女性的大部分生殖道和男性的一部分生殖道：

    • 在女性中，陰道 (vagina) 穿過骨盆底，與骨盆腔內的**，子宮 (uterus)** 相連。卵巢 (uterine/fallopian) tube 向兩側延伸至骨盆壁，開口於卵巢 (ovary) 附近。
    • 在男性中，骨盆腔包含泌尿與生殖道的連接部位，還容納與生殖系統相關的主要腺體——前列腺 (prostate) 和兩個精囊 (seminal vesicles)。

    骨盆 Pelvic Bones

    骨盆 Pelvic Bone

    Pelvic bone 的形狀不規則，以骨骼內側表面的** oblique line** 區分為兩大部分：

    • oblong line 以上：構成** false pelvis**（屬於腹腔）的外側壁
    • oblong line 以下：構成包含** pelvic cavity** 的** true pelvis** 外側壁

    Linea terminalis 為此線的下 2/3，構成** pelvic inlet** 的邊緣。

    骨骼外側表面有大型關節窩 acetabulum，與** femur** 的股骨頭形成** hip joint**。

    臨床重點：Acetabulum 為髖關節的核心結構，其完整性對於步態和負重至關重要。

    Acetabulum 下方為大型** obturator foramen**，大部分由扁平結締組織膜 obturator membrane 封閉，上方留有小的** obturator canal**，作為** lower limb** 與** pelvic cavity** 之間的交通路線。

    骨骼後緣有兩個切跡，由** ischial spine** 分隔：

    • greater sciatic notch
    • lesser sciatic notch

    後緣向下延伸為大型** ischial tuberosity**。

    骨骼前緣不規則，有以下標記：

    • anterior superior iliac spine
    • anterior inferior iliac spine
    • pubic tubercle

    Perineum（會陰）

    會陰 Perineum — 概念性總覽

    Perineum 位於** pelvic floor** 下方、** lower limbs** 之間。其邊界由** pelvic outlet** 形成。Ischial tuberosities之間的假想線將會陰分為兩個三角區域：

    • urogenital triangle（泌尿生殖三角）：女性的** clitoris** 根部、** urethral orifice** 及** vaginal orifice**；男性的** penis** 根部及** penile urethra** 終端開口
    • anal triangle（肛門三角）：包含** anal aperture**

    臨床重點：會陰的完整性對於泌尿生殖功能和肛門控制至關重要。會陰撕裂常見於分娩過程，需妥善評估與處理。

    Pelvic Floor（骨盆底）

    Pelvic Floor（概念性總覽）

    Pelvic floor 由肌肉和筋膜構成，分隔** pelvic cavity** 與** perineum**。

    兩側** levator ani muscles** 附著於** pelvic walls**，於中線以結締組織縫隙相連。它們是構成盆狀或漏斗狀結構 pelvic diaphragm 的最大部分，後方由** coccygeus muscles** 完成。

    Pelvic diaphragm 的前方區域包含 U-shaped defect（U 形缺陷），與泌尿生殖系統結構相關。

    Anal canal 從** pelvis** 穿過** pelvic diaphragm** 的後方圓形開口進入** perineum**。

    Pelvic floor 前方由以下結構支撐：

    • perineal membrane
    • deep perineal pouch 的肌肉

    Perineal membrane 為厚的三角形筋膜板，填充** pubic arch** 兩臂之間的空間，具有游離的後緣。Deep perineal pouch 為** perineal membrane** 上方的狹窄區域。

    U-shaped defect 的邊緣與相關臟器的管壁融合，並與** deep perineal pouch** 下方的肌肉相連。

    Vagina 和** urethra** 穿過** pelvic floor**，從** pelvic cavity** 進入** perineum**。

    臨床重點：Pelvic floor 肌肉的弱化（常見於產後或老年）可導致** pelvic organ prolapse**（骨盆器官脫垂）和尿失禁。

    Pelvic Cavity（骨盆腔）

    Pelvic Cavity（概念性總覽）

    Pelvic cavity 襯有** peritoneum**，與腹腔** peritoneum** 相延續，包覆** pelvic viscera** 的上方，但在大多數區域未達** pelvic floor**。

    Pelvic viscera 位於** pelvic cavity** 中線：

    • bladder 位於前方
    • rectum 位於後方
    • 女性：uterus 位於** bladder** 和** rectum** 之間

    其他結構如** vessels**、nerves 和** lymphatics** 位於** peritoneum** 深層，沿** pelvic walls** 和** pelvic viscera** 兩側分布。

    組成部分 Component Parts

    Pelvic Inlet（骨盆入口）

    Pelvic inlet 呈心形，完全由骨骼環繞。後方以** SI vertebra** 的** body** 為界，突入 inlet 形成** sacral promontory**。椎骨兩側的翼狀橫突稱為** alae**（wings），構成 inlet 邊緣。外側，** pelvic bone** 上的突出邊緣延續邊界向前至** pubic symphysis**（兩側** pelvic bones** 於中線相連之處）。

    臨床重點：Pelvic inlet 的徑線在分娩過程中至關重要。胎兒需通過** pelvic inlet** 從腹腔進入** pelvic outlet**。

    Pelvic Walls（骨盆壁）

    True pelvis 的壁主要由骨骼、肌肉和韌帶構成，sacrum、coccyx 和** pelvic bones** 的下半部構成其大部分。

    兩條韌帶——sacrospinous ligament 和** sacrotuberous ligament**——是壁的重要建築元素，連接每側** pelvic bone** 至** sacrum** 和** coccyx**。這些韌帶也將** pelvic bones** 上的兩個切跡——greater 和** lesser sciatic notches**——轉換為** lateral pelvic walls** 上的** foramina**。

    完成壁的肌肉有** obturator internus** 和** piriformis muscles**（起源於** pelvis**，穿過** sciatic foramina** 作用於** hip joint**）。

    臨床重點：Sacrospinous ligament 和** sacrotuberous ligament** 在維持骨盆穩定性中扮演關鍵角色，並可作為微創手術的定位標誌。

    Pelvic Outlet（骨盆出口）

    菱形的** pelvic outlet** 由骨骼和韌帶共同構成。前方以中線的** pubic symphysis** 為界。

    每側，** pelvic bone** 的下緣從** pubic symphysis** 向後外側延伸，終止於明顯的** ischial tuberosity**。這些結構共同構成** pubic arch**，形成** pelvic outlet** 前半的邊緣。

    Sacrotuberous ligament 從** ischial tuberosity** 向後延續至** coccyx** 和** sacrum**，構成 outlet 的後半邊緣。

    Pubic symphysis、ischiopubic tuberosities 和** coccyx** 均可觸診。

    臨床重點：Ischial tuberosities 的觸診常用於評估會陰解剖，並在某些婦科手術中作為定位標誌。

    Pelvic Floor（骨盆底）

    Pelvic floor 分隔** pelvic cavity** 與** perineum**，由肌肉和筋膜構成。

    兩側** levator ani muscles** 周邊附著於** pelvic walls**，於中線以結締組織縫隙相連。它們是構成盆狀或漏斗狀結構** pelvic diaphragm** 的最大部分，後方由** coccygeus muscles** 完成。這些肌肉覆於** sacrospinous ligaments** 之上，介於** sacrum** 和** coccyx** 的邊緣與** pelvic bone** 上的明顯** ischial spine** 之間。

    Pelvic diaphragm 構成大部分** pelvic floor**，其前方區域包含與泌尿生殖系統結構相關的 U-shaped defect。

    Anal canal 從** pelvis** 穿過** pelvic diaphragm** 的後方圓形開口進入** perineum**。

    Pelvic floor 前方由以下結構支撐：

    • perineal membrane
    • deep perineal pouch 的肌肉

    Perineal membrane 為厚的三角形筋膜板，填充** pubic arch** 兩臂之間的空間，具有游離的後緣。Deep perineal pouch 為** perineal membrane** 上方的狹窄區域。

    U-shaped defect 的邊緣與相關臟器的管壁融合，並與** deep perineal pouch** 下方的肌肉相連。

    Vagina 和** urethra** 穿過** pelvic floor**，從** pelvic cavity** 進入** perineum**。

    Perineum（會陰）

    Perineum 位於** pelvic floor** 下方、** lower limbs** 之間。其邊界由** pelvic outlet** 形成。Ischial tuberosities 之間的假想線將會陰分為兩個三角區域：

    • Urogenital triangle（泌尿生殖三角）位於前方，包含：
    • 女性：clitoris 根部、urethra 和** vagina** 開口（圖 5.9A）
    • 男性：penis 根部，urethra 末端被勃起組織包圍，開口於penis 末端（圖 5.9B）
    • Anal triangle（肛門三角）位於後方，包含** anal aperture**

    與其他區域的關係 Relationship to Other Regions

    腹部 Abdomen

    True pelvis 的腔隙通過** pelvic inlet** 與腹腔相通。所有通過** pelvic cavity** 與腹腔之間的結構——包括** major vessels**、nerves、lymphatics、sigmoid colon 和** ureters**——均通過此入口。

    男性：每側的** ductus deferens** 穿過前腹壁，通過入口進入** pelvic cavity**。

    女性：ovarian vessels、nerves 和** lymphatics** 通過入口到達卵巢，卵巢位於入口下方兩側。

    臨床重點：Ureter 在** pelvic inlet** 附近與** ovarian vessels** 關係密切，在婦科手術中需特別注意以避免損傷。

    下肢 Lower Limb

    Pelvic wall 上有三個開口與下肢相通：

    • Obturator canal
    • Greater sciatic foramen
    • Lesser sciatic foramen

    Obturator canal 構成** pelvic cavity** 與大腿** adductor region** 之間的通道，位於** obturator foramen** 上方，由骨骼、結締組織膜和填充** foramen** 的肌肉共同構成。

    Lesser sciatic foramen 位於** pelvic floor** 下方，提供** gluteal region** 與** perineum** 之間的交通（圖 5.10B）。

    Pelvic cavity 也通過** pubic symphysis** 與** perineal membrane** 之間的小間隙直接與** perineum** 相通。通過此間隙的結構包括：

    • Dorsal vein of penis / clitoris
    • Urethra
    • Vagina

    臨床重點：了解** pelvic cavity** 與** perineum** 之間的交通路徑對於盆腔和會陰手術入路至關重要。

    重要特徵 Key Features

    骨盆腔向後投射

    在解剖姿勢下，anterior superior iliac spines 和** pubic symphysis** 的上緣位於同一垂直面。因此，pelvic inlet 相對於水平面向前傾斜 50°-60°，** pelvic cavity** 從腹腔向後投射。

    同時，** pelvic outlet** 的泌尿生殖部分（pubic arch）幾乎呈水平方向，而 outlet 的後部則更垂直。因此，會陰的** urogenital triangle** 朝向下方的泌尿生殖區域，而** anal triangle** 則朝向後方的肛門區域。

    臨床重點：骨盆傾斜角度的變化可反映骨盆創傷或先天性異常。理解骨盆的正常方向對於影像學評估和手術規劃至關重要。

    重要結構跨越輸尿管

    Ureters 引流** kidneys**，沿後腹壁下行，跨越** pelvic inlet** 進入** pelvic cavity**。它們沿** lateral pelvic wall** 繼續下行，最終連接至** bladder** 的底部。

    一個重要結構在男性和女性的** pelvic cavity** 中跨越** ureters**：

    • 女性：uterine artery 在** cervix** 外側跨越** ureter**
    • 男性：ductus deferens 在** bladder** 後方跨越** ureter**

    臨床重點：在** ureter** 跨越** uterine artery**（女性）或** ductus deferens**（男性）的部位，** ureter** 容易在手術中受損。Radical hysterectomy（根治性子宮切除術）中識別此解剖關係對預防術中併發症至關重要。

    ###男性的前列腺與女性的、子宮位於直腸前方

    男性：prostate gland 位於直腸前方，恰在** pelvic floor** 上方。可以通過直腸指檢進行觸診。

    兩性：anal canal 和** lower rectum** 也可以在直腸檢查中進行評估。女性：cervix 和** uterus** 下部也可觸診。然而，這些結構更適合通過** bimanual examination**（雙合診）進行檢查——臨床醫生的食指和中指放入陰道，另一隻手放在下前腹壁上。臟器在雙手之間被觸診。Bimanual examination 技術也可用於檢查** ovaries** 和** uterine tubes**。

    臨床重點：Prostate-specific antigen (PSA) 血液檢測與直腸指檢相結合是篩查** prostate cancer**（前列腺癌）的重要工具。Digital rectal examination (DRE) 是評估前列腺大小、質地和有無結節的常規方法。

    會陰由骶脊髓節段支配

    兩性會陰的** dermatomes** 來自** spinal cord levels S3** 至 S5，前方區域主要由** spinal cord level T11**（通過與腹壁相關的神經）支配。T12 至 S2 dermatomes 主要分布於下肢。

    大多數位於** perineum** 和** pelvic floor** 的骨骼肌——包括** external anal sphincter** 和** external urethral sphincter**——由** spinal cord levels S2** 至 S4 支配。

    Perineum 的大部分軀體運動和感覺神經支配由來自 S2 至 S4 的** pudendal nerve** 提供。

    神經與骨骼相關

    Pudendal nerve 是會陰的主要神經，直接與** pelvis** 的** ischial spine** 相關。在身體每側，這些** spines** 和附著的** sacrospinous ligaments** 分隔** lateral pelvic wall** 上的** greater sciatic foramina** 與** lesser sciatic foramina**。

    Pudendal nerve 通過** greater sciatic foramen** 離開** pelvic cavity**，然後立即繞過** ischial spine** 並通過** lesser sciatic foramen** 進入** pelvic floor** 下方的** perineum**。

    臨床重點：Ischial spine 可經陰道觸診（女性），是進行** pudendal nerve block**（陰部神經阻滯）的定位標誌。Pudendal nerve block 主要用於分娩時會陰側切修復的止痛。

    來自 S2 至 S4 的副交感神經支配控制勃起

    來自 S2 至 S4 的副交感神經支配控制女性和男性的生殖勃起。每側，來自** sacral spinal nerves** 前根的** preganglionic parasympathetic nerves** 進入** lateral pelvic wall** 上的** inferior hypogastric plexus (pelvic plexus)**。

    兩個** inferior hypogastric plexuses** 是沿後腹壁與** abdominal aorta** 相關聯的** abdominal prevertebral plexus** 的向下延伸。來自這些神經叢的神經穿透** pelvic floor**，支配** clitoris**（女性）和** penis**（男性）的勃起組織。

    臨床重點：Erectile dysfunction (ED) 的評估需考慮副交感神經功能。Pelvic splanchnic nerves（骨盆內臟神經）的損傷可導致勃起功能障礙。

    骨盆底和會陰的肌肉與筋膜在會陰體交匯

    Pelvic floor 的結構與** perineum** 的結構在** perineal body**（會陰體）交匯。這個界定不清的纖維肌性結節位於會陰中央，大約位於兩個** ischial tuberosities** 的中點。匯聚於** perineal body** 的結構包括：

    • Pelvic diaphragm 的** levator ani muscles**
    • Perineum 的泌尿生殖三角和肛門三角中的肌肉，包括與** urethra**、vagina 和** anus** 相關的骨骼肌** sphincters**

    臨床重點：Perineal body 的完整性對於維持盆腔器官的支持至關重要。Episiotomy（會陰切開術）時需注意保護** perineal body**，以避免產後會陰疼痛和功能障礙。

    男女尿道走勢不同

    女性：urethra 較短，從** bladder** 向下穿過** pelvic floor**，直接開口於** perineum**。

    男性：urethra 穿過** prostate**，然後穿過** deep perineal pouch** 和** perineal membrane**，之後被包埋在** penis** 的勃起組織內，最終開口於** penis** 末端。男性尿道的** penile part** 有兩個角度：

    • 較重要的一個是固定角度：尿道穿過** perineal membrane** 後，在** penis** 根部向前彎曲
    • 另一個角度位於遠端：penis 的游離部分向下彎曲——當** penis** 勃起時，這第二個角度消失

    臨床重點：在導尿和評估會陰損傷及骨盆病理時，必須考慮男女尿道的不同走勢。男性** membranous urethra**（膜部尿道）穿過** urogenital diaphragm**（泌尿生殖膈），是最容易受損的部位之一。

    區域解剖 Regional Anatomy

    骨盆 Pelvis

    Pelvis 是由** pelvic bones** 和** vertebral column** 下部環繞的身體區域。分為兩個主要區域：

    • 上方為** false (greater) pelvis**，屬於腹腔的一部分
    • 下方為** true (lesser) pelvis**，包含** pelvic cavity**

    盆狀的** pelvic cavity** 在上方與腹腔相延續。Pelvic cavity 的邊緣（pelvic inlet）完全由骨骼環繞。Pelvic floor 是分隔上方** pelvic cavity** 與下方** perineum** 的纖維肌肉結構。

    Perineum 位於** pelvic floor** 下方，其邊界由** pelvic outlet** 形成。Perineum 包含：

    • 胃腸道和泌尿系統的終端開口
    • 生殖道的外部開口
    • 外部生殖器的根部

    骨骼 Bones

    Pelvis 的骨骼由右側和左側** pelvic (hip) bones**、sacrum 和** coccyx** 構成。Sacrum 在上方通過** lumbosacral joint** 與** LV vertebra** 相連。

    Pelvic bones 在後方通過** sacroiliac joints** 與** sacrum** 相連，在前方通過** pubic symphysis** 彼此相連。

    Pelvic Bone

    Pelvic bone 形狀不規則，有兩個主要部分，由骨骼內側表面的一條** oblique line** 分開：

    • 此線上方的** pelvic bone** 代表** false pelvis** 的外側壁，屬於腹腔
    • 此線下方的** pelvic bone** 代表** true pelvis** 的外側壁，包含** pelvic cavity**

    Linea terminalis 為此線的下 2/3，構成** pelvic inlet** 的邊緣。

    Pelvic bone 的外側表面有大型關節窩 acetabulum，與** femur** 的股骨頭形成** hip joint**。

    Acetabulum 下方為大型** obturator foramen**，大部分由扁平結締組織膜 obturator membrane 封閉。上方留有小的** obturator canal**，提供** lower limb** 與** pelvic cavity** 之間的交通路線。

    骨骼的後緣有兩個切跡，由** ischial spine** 分隔：

    • greater sciatic notch
    • lesser sciatic notch

    後緣向下終止為大型** ischial tuberosity**。

    骨骼的不規則前緣有以下標記：

    • anterior superior iliac spine
    • anterior inferior iliac spine
    • pubic tubercle

    臨床重點：Pelvic bone 的骨折，特別是涉及** acetabulum** 或** sacroiliac joint** 的骨折，通常與高能量創傷相關，需要仔細的手術規劃和複雜的骨折固定技術。

    Pelvic Bone Components

    Each pelvic bone is formed by three elements: the ilium, pubis, and ischium. At birth, these bones are connected by cartilage in the area of the acetabulum; later, between 16 and 18 years of age, they fuse into a single bone.

    Ilium

    Of the three components of the pelvic bone, the ilium is the most superior in position.

    The ilium is separated into upper and lower parts by a ridge on the medial surface.

    • Posteriorly, the ridge is sharp and lies immediately superior to the surface of the bone that articulates with the sacrum. This sacral surface has a large L-shaped facet for articulating with the sacrum and an expanded, posterior roughened area for the attachment of the strong ligaments that support the sacro-iliac joint.

    • Anteriorly, the ridge separating the upper and lower parts of the ilium is rounded and termed the arcuate line.

    Clinical重點：骨髓切片檢查 (Bone Marrow Biopsy) 在某些疾病（如白血病）中，需取得骨髓樣本以評估病程與嚴重程度。髂骨嵴 (iliac crest) 因其位置淺近、易於觸摸，常作為骨髓切片檢查的部位。操作方式為：先在皮膚注射局部麻醉劑，再以切割針穿透髂骨嵴的皮質骨，抽取骨髓進行顯微鏡檢查。此方法亦可取得皮質骨樣本，用於評估骨質代謝狀況。

    Ilium — Superior Part (Wing)

    The upper part of the ilium expands to form a flat, fan-shaped “wing,” which provides bony support for the lower abdomen, or false pelvis. This part of the ilium provides attachment for muscles functionally associated with the lower limb.

    • The anteromedial surface of the wing is concave and forms the iliac fossa.
    • The external (gluteal) surface is marked by lines and roughenings and is related to the gluteal region of the lower limb.

    The entire superior margin of the ilium is thickened to form a prominent iliac crest, which is the site of attachment for muscles and fascia of the abdomen, back, and lower limb, and terminates:

    • Anteriorly as the anterior superior iliac spine
    • Posteriorly as the posterior superior iliac spine

    A prominent tubercle, the tuberculum of the iliac crest, projects laterally near the anterior end of the crest; the posterior end of the crest thickens to form the iliac tuberosity.

    Inferior to the anterior superior iliac spine, on the anterior margin of the ilium, is a rounded protuberance called the anterior inferior iliac spine. This structure serves as the point of attachment for the rectus femoris muscle of the anterior compartment of the thigh and the iliofemoral ligament associated with the hip joint.

    A less prominent posterior inferior iliac spine occurs along the posterior border of the sacral surface of the ilium, where the bone angles forward to form the superior margin of the greater sciatic notch.

    Ilium — Inferior Part (Pelvic Part)

    The portion of the ilium lying inferiorly to the arcuate line is the pelvic part of the ilium and contributes to the wall of the lesser (true) pelvis.

    The arcuate line forms part of the linea terminalis and the pelvic brim.

    Pubis

    The anterior and inferior part of the pelvic bone is the pubis. It has a body and two rami:

    • Body: Flattened dorsoventrally; articulates with the body of the opposite pubic bone at the pubic symphysis. The body has a rounded pubic crest on its superior surface that ends laterally as the prominent pubic tubercle.

    • Superior pubic ramus: Projects posterolaterally from the body and joins with the ilium and ischium at its base, positioned toward the acetabulum. Its sharp superior margin is termed the pecten pubis (pectineal line), which forms part of the linea terminalis of the pelvic bone and the pelvic inlet. Anteriorly, this line is continuous with the pubic crest, also part of the linea terminalis and pelvic inlet. The superior pubic ramus is marked on its inferior surface by the obturator groove, which forms the upper margin of the obturator canal.

    • Inferior ramus: Projects laterally and inferiorly to join with the ramus of the ischium.

    Ischium

    The ischium is the posterior and inferior part of the pelvic bone. It has:

    • A large body that projects superiorly to join with the ilium and the superior ramus of the pubis.
    • A ramus that projects anteriorly to join with the inferior ramus of the pubis.

    The posterior margin of the bone is marked by a prominent ischial spine that separates the lesser sciatic notch (below) from the greater sciatic notch (above).

    The most prominent feature of the ischium is a large ischial tuberosity on the posteroinferior aspect of the bone. This tuberosity is an important site for the attachment of lower limb muscles and for supporting the body when sitting.

    Sacrum

    The sacrum, which has the appearance of an inverted triangle, is formed by the fusion of the five sacral vertebrae.

    • Base: Articulates with vertebra LV
    • Apex: Articulates with the coccyx

    Each lateral surface of the bone bears a large L-shaped facet for articulation with the ilium of the pelvic bone. Posterior to the facet is a large roughened area for the attachment of ligaments that support the sacro-iliac joint.

    The superior surface of the sacrum is characterized by the superior aspect of the body of vertebra SI and is flanked on each side by an expanded wing-like transverse process termed the ala. The anterior edge of the vertebral body projects forward as the promontory.

    The anterior surface of the sacrum is concave; the posterior surface is convex.

    Because the transverse processes of adjacent sacral vertebrae fuse lateral to the position of the intervertebral foramina and lateral to the bifurcation of spinal nerves into posterior and anterior rami, the posterior and anterior rami of spinal nerves S1 to S4 emerge from the sacrum through separate foramina:

    • Four pairs of anterior sacral foramina (anterior surface) — for anterior rami
    • Four pairs of posterior sacral foramina (posterior surface) — for posterior rami

    The sacral canal is a continuation of the vertebral canal that terminates as the sacral hiatus.

    Coccyx

    The small terminal part of the vertebral column is the coccyx, which consists of four fused coccygeal vertebrae and, like the sacrum, has the shape of an inverted triangle.

    • Base: Directed superiorly; bears a facet for articulation with the sacrum and two cornua (horns), one on each side, that project upward to articulate or fuse with similar downward-projecting cornua from the sacrum.
    • Each lateral surface has a small rudimentary transverse process, extending from the first coccygeal vertebra.
    • Vertebral arches are absent from coccygeal vertebrae; therefore no bony vertebral canal is present in the coccyx.

    Joints

    Lumbosacral Joints

    The sacrum articulates superiorly with the lumbar part of the vertebral column. The lumbosacral joints are formed between vertebra LV and the sacrum and consist of:

    • The two zygapophysial joints, between adjacent inferior and superior articular processes
    • An intervertebral disc that joins the bodies of vertebrae LV and SI

    These joints are similar to those between other vertebrae, with the exception that the sacrum is angled posteriorly on vertebra LV. As a result, the anterior part of the intervertebral disc is thicker than the posterior part.

    The lumbosacral joints are reinforced by strong iliolumbar and lumbosacral ligaments that extend from the expanded transverse processes of vertebra LV to the ilium and the sacrum, respectively.

    Sacro-iliac Joints

    The sacro-iliac joints transmit forces from the lower limbs to the vertebral column. They are synovial joints between the L-shaped articular facets on the lateral surfaces of the sacrum and similar facets on the iliac parts of the pelvic bones. The joint surfaces have an irregular contour and interlock to resist movement. The joints often become fibrous with age and may become completely ossified.

    Each sacro-iliac joint is stabilized by three ligaments:

    • Anterior sacro-iliac ligament: A thickening of the fibrous membrane of the joint capsule; runs anteriorly and inferiorly to the joint.
    • Interosseous sacro-iliac ligament: The largest, strongest of the three; positioned immediately posterosuperior to the joint; attaches to adjacent expansive roughened areas on the ilium and sacrum, filling the gap between the two bones.
    • Posterior sacro-iliac ligament: Covers the interosseous sacro-iliac ligament.

    臨床重點：Sacro-iliac Joint 常見問題 Sacro-iliac joints 兼具纖維性與滑膜性成分，與許多承重關節相同，可能發生退化性變化並導致疼痛。此外，與 MHC antigen HLA-B27 相關的疾病（如類風濕性關節炎、乾癬、發炎性腸道疾病）可在這些關節中產生特定的发炎性變化。

    Pubic Symphysis

    The pubic symphysis lies anteriorly between the adjacent surfaces of the pubic bones. Each of the joint’s surfaces is covered by hyaline cartilage and is linked across the midline to adjacent surfaces by fibrocartilage. The joint is surrounded by interwoven layers of collagen fibers.

    The two major ligaments associated with it are:

    • Superior pubic ligament: Located above the joint
    • Inferior pubic ligament: Located below the joint

    Orientation of the Pelvis

    In the anatomical position, the pelvis is oriented so that the front edge of the top of the pubic symphysis and the anterior superior iliac spines lie in the same vertical plane. As a consequence:

    • The pelvic inlet is tilted to face anteriorly
    • The bodies of the pubic bones and the pubic arch are positioned in a nearly horizontal plane facing the ground

    Differences Between Men and Women

    The pelvises of women and men differ in a number of ways, many of which relate to passage of a baby through a woman’s pelvic cavity during childbirth:

    • Pelvic inlet: Circular in women vs. heart-shaped in men (the more circular shape is partly caused by the less distinct promontory and broader alae in women)
    • Pubic arch angle: Larger in women (80°–85°) than in men (50°–60°)
    • Ischial spines: Generally do not project as far medially in women as they do in men

    True Pelvis

    The true pelvis is cylindrical and has an inlet, a wall, and an outlet. The inlet is open, whereas the pelvic floor closes the outlet and separates the pelvic cavity (above) from the perineum (below).

    Pelvic Inlet

    The pelvic inlet is the circular opening between the abdominal cavity and the pelvic cavity through which structures traverse between the abdomen and pelvic cavity. It is completely surrounded by bones and joints:

    • Posterior margin: Formed by the promontory of the sacrum (midline) and the alae of the sacrum (on either side)
    • The margin then crosses the sacro-iliac joint and continues along the linea terminalis (arcuate line, pecten pubis, pubic crest) to the pubic symphysis

    Pelvic Wall

    The walls of the pelvic cavity consist of the sacrum, coccyx, pelvic bones inferior to the linea terminalis, two ligaments, and two muscles.

    Ligaments of the Pelvic Wall

    The sacrospinous and sacrotuberous ligaments are major components of the lateral pelvic walls that help define the apertures between the pelvic cavity and adjacent regions:

    • Sacrospinous ligament: The smaller of the two; triangular; apex attached to the ischial spine; base attached to related margins of the sacrum and coccyx.
    • Sacrotuberous ligament: Triangular and superficial to the sacrospinous ligament; base extends from the posterior superior iliac spine of the pelvic bone, along the dorsal and lateral margin of the sacrum, and onto the dorsolateral surface of the coccyx; apex attached to the medial margin of the ischial tuberosity.

    These ligaments stabilize the sacrum by resisting upward tilting of the inferior aspect of the sacrum. They also convert the greater and lesser sciatic notches of the pelvic bone into foramina:

    • Greater sciatic foramen: Superior to the sacrospinous ligament and ischial spine
    • Lesser sciatic foramen: Inferior to the ischial spine and sacrospinous ligament, between the sacrospinous and sacrotuberous ligaments

    臨床重點：骨盆骨折 (Pelvic Fracture) 骨盆可視為一系列解剖學環結構造。共有三個骨性環結（major bony pelvic ring 形成 pelvic inlet，兩個較小的 obturator foramina）和四個纖維骨性環結（greater and lesser sciatic foramina）。 骨性環結（如 pelvic inlet 和 obturator foramina）屬於脆性環結，臨床上若在一側發現骨折，必須懷疑對側也存在骨折。 骨盆骨折可能造成大量出血，形成巨大血腫，壓迫膀胱和輸尿管等器官。此外，可能導致尿道損傷、腸道破裂及神經損傷。

    Muscles of the Pelvic Wall

    Two muscles — the obturator internus and the piriformis — contribute to the lateral walls of the pelvic cavity. These muscles originate in the pelvic cavity but attach peripherally to the femur.

    Obturator Internus

    The obturator internus is a flat, fan-shaped muscle that originates from the deep surface of the obturator membrane and from associated regions of the pelvic bone surrounding the obturator foramen.

    The muscle fibers converge to form a tendon that:

    1. Leaves the pelvic cavity through the lesser sciatic foramen
    2. Makes a 90° bend around the ischium between the ischial spine and ischial tuberosity
    3. Passes posterior to the hip joint
    4. Inserts on the medial surface of the greater trochanter of the femur

    The obturator internus forms a large part of the anterolateral wall of the pelvic cavity.

    Piriformis

    The piriformis is triangular and originates in the bridges of bone between the four anterior sacral foramina. It passes laterally through the greater sciatic foramen, crosses the posterosuperior aspect of the hip joint, and inserts on the greater trochanter of the femur above the insertion of the obturator internus.

    A large part of the posterolateral wall of the pelvic cavity is formed by the piriformis. This muscle separates the greater sciatic foramen into two regions (one above, one below), through which vessels and nerves pass between the pelvic cavity and the gluteal region.

    Pelvis and Perineum

    Apertures in the Pelvic Wall（骨盆腔壁的開口）

    Each lateral pelvic wall has three major apertures through which structures pass between the pelvic cavity and other regions:

    • obturator canal（閉孔管）
    • greater sciatic foramen（坐骨大孔）
    • lesser sciatic foramen（坐骨小孔）

    Obturator Canal（閉孔管）

    At the top of the obturator foramen is the obturator canal, which is bordered by the obturator membrane, the associated obturator muscles, and the superior pubic ramus（圖 5.31）。Obturator nerve and vessels pass from the pelvic cavity to the thigh through this canal.

    Greater Sciatic Foramen（坐骨大孔）

    The greater sciatic foramen is a major route of communication between the pelvic cavity and the lower limb（圖 5.31）。It is formed by the greater sciatic notch in the pelvic bone, the sacrotuberous and the sacrospinous ligaments, and the spine of the ischium。

    The piriformis muscle passes through the greater sciatic foramen, dividing it into two parts:

    • The superior gluteal nerves and vessels pass through the foramen above the piriformis.
    • Passing through the foramen below the piriformis are the inferior gluteal nerves and vessels, the sciatic nerve, the pudendal nerve, the internal pudendal vessels, the posterior femoral cutaneous nerves, and the nerves to the obturator internus and quadratus femoris muscles.

    Lesser Sciatic Foramen（坐骨小孔）

    The lesser sciatic foramen is formed by the lesser sciatic notch of the pelvic bone, the ischial spine, the sacrospinous ligament, and the sacrotuberous ligament（圖 5.31）。

    The tendon of the obturator internus muscle passes through this foramen to enter the gluteal region of the lower limb.

    Clinical Point（臨床重點）： Because the lesser sciatic foramen is positioned below the attachment of the pelvic floor, it acts as a route of communication between the perineum and the gluteal region. The pudendal nerve and internal pudendal vessels pass between the pelvic cavity (above the pelvic floor) and the perineum (below the pelvic floor), by first passing out of the pelvic cavity through the greater sciatic foramen and then looping around the ischial spine and sacrospinous ligament to pass through the lesser sciatic foramen to enter the perineum.

    Pelvic Outlet（骨盆腔出口）

    The pelvic outlet is diamond-shaped, with the anterior part of the diamond defined predominantly by bone and the posterior part mainly by ligaments（圖 5.32）。

    In the midline anteriorly, the boundary of the pelvic outlet is the pubic symphysis（恥骨聯合）。

    Extending laterally and posteriorly, the boundary on each side is:

    • inferior border of the body of the pubis
    • inferior ramus of the pubis
    • ramus of the ischium
    • ischial tuberosity

    Together, the elements on both sides form the pubic arch（恥骨弓）。

    From the ischial tuberosities, the boundaries continue posteriorly and medially along the sacrotuberous ligament on both sides to the coccyx。

    Terminal parts of the urinary and gastrointestinal tracts and the vagina pass through the pelvic outlet.

    The area enclosed by the boundaries of the pelvic outlet and below the pelvic floor is the perineum（會陰）。

    Clinical Point（臨床重點）： Pelvic measurements in obstetrics（產科學骨盆測量） Transverse and sagittal measurements of a woman’s pelvic inlet and outlet can help in predicting the likelihood of a successful vaginal delivery. These measurements include:

    • the sagittal inlet（前後徑入口）
    • the maximum transverse diameter of the inlet
    • the bispinous outlet（坐骨棘間徑）
    • the sagittal outlet（前後徑出口）

    These measurements can be obtained using magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)（磁振造影），which carries no radiation risk for the fetus or mother.

    Pelvic Floor（骨盆腔底）

    The pelvic floor is formed by the pelvic diaphragm and, in the anterior midline, the perineal membrane and the muscles in the deep perineal pouch。The pelvic diaphragm is formed by the levator ani and the coccygeus muscles from both sides. The pelvic floor separates the pelvic cavity, above, from the perineum, below.

    The Pelvic Diaphragm（骨盆橫膈）

    The pelvic diaphragm is the muscular part of the pelvic floor. Shaped like a bowl or funnel and attached superiorly to the pelvic walls, it consists of the levator ani and the coccygeus muscles（圖 5.34 及 Table 5.2）。

    The pelvic diaphragm’s circular line of attachment to the cylindrical pelvic wall passes, on each side, between the greater sciatic foramen and the lesser sciatic foramen。Thus:

    • The greater sciatic foramen is situated above the level of the pelvic floor and is a route of communication between the pelvic cavity and the gluteal region of the lower limb.
    • The lesser sciatic foramen is situated below the pelvic floor, providing a route of communication between the gluteal region of the lower limb and the perineum.

    Levator Ani（提肛肌）

    The two levator ani muscles originate from each side of the pelvic wall, course medially and inferiorly, and join together in the midline. The attachment to the pelvic wall follows the circular contour of the wall and includes:

    • the posterior aspect of the body of the pubic bone
    • a linear thickening called the tendinous arch（腱弓），in the fascia covering the obturator internus muscle
    • the spine of the ischium

    At the midline, the muscles blend together posterior to the vagina in women and around the anal aperture in both sexes. Posterior to the anal aperture, the muscles come together as a ligament or raphe called the anococcygeal ligament（肛尾韌帶）and attaches to the coccyx。

    Anteriorly, the muscles are separated by a U-shaped defect or gap termed the urogenital hiatus（泌尿生殖裂孔）。The margins of this hiatus merge with the walls of the associated viscera and with muscles in the deep perineal pouch below. The hiatus allows the urethra（in both men and women），and the vagina（in women），to pass through the pelvic diaphragm.

    The levator ani muscles are divided into at least three collections of muscle fibers, based on site of origin and relationship to viscera in the midline: the pubococcygeus, the puborectalis, and the iliococcygeus muscles.

    • The pubococcygeus originates from the body of the pubis and courses posteriorly to attach along the midline as far back as the coccyx. This part of the muscle is further subdivided on the basis of association with structures in the midline into the puboprostaticus（levator prostatae），the pubovaginalis，and the puboanalis muscles.
    • A second major collection of muscle fibers, the puborectalis portion of the levator ani muscles, originates, in association with the pubococcygeus muscle, from the pubis and passes inferiorly on each side to form a sling around the terminal part of the gastrointestinal tract. This muscular sling maintains an angle or flexure, called the perineal flexure（會陰彎曲），at the anorectal junction. This angle functions as part of the mechanism that keeps the end of the gastrointestinal system closed.
    • The final part of the levator ani muscle is the iliococcygeus。This part of the muscle originates from the fascia that covers the obturator internus muscle。It joins the same muscle on the other side in the midline to form a ligament or raphe that extends from the anal aperture to the coccyx.

    The levator ani muscles help support the pelvic viscera and maintain closure of the rectum and vagina. They are innervated directly by branches from the anterior ramus of S4 and by branches of the pudendal nerve（S2 to S4）。

    Coccygeus（尾骨肌）

    The two coccygeus muscles，one on each side, are triangular and overlie the sacrospinous ligaments；together they complete the posterior part of the pelvic diaphragm（圖 5.34 及 Table 5.2）。They are attached, by their apices, to the tips of the ischial spines and, by their bases, to the lateral margins of the coccyx and adjacent margins of the sacrum。

    These coccygeus muscles are innervated by branches from the anterior rami of S3 and S4 and participate in supporting the posterior aspect of the pelvic floor.

As feces emerge from the anus, the longitudinal muscles of the rectum and levator ani bring the anal canal back up, the feces are expelled, and the anus and rectum return to their normal positions.

    The Perineal Membrane and Deep Perineal Pouch（會陰膜與深層會陰腔）

    The perineal membrane is a thick fascial, triangular structure attached to the bony framework of the pubic arch（圖 5.35A）。It is oriented in the horizontal plane and has a free posterior margin. Anteriorly, there is a small gap between the membrane and the inferior pubic ligament（a ligament associated with the pubic symphysis）。

    The perineal membrane is related above to a thin space called the deep perineal pouch（深層會陰腔）（deep perineal space）（圖 5.35B），which contains a layer of skeletal muscle and various neurovascular elements.

    The deep perineal pouch is open above and is not separated from more superior structures by a distinct layer of fascia. The parts of the perineal membrane and structures in the deep perineal pouch, enclosed by the urogenital hiatus above, therefore contribute to the pelvic floor and support elements of the urogenital system in the pelvic cavity.

    The perineal membrane and adjacent pubic arch provide attachment for the roots of the external genitalia and the muscles associated with them.

    The urethra penetrates vertically through a circular hiatus in the perineal membrane as it passes from the pelvic cavity, above, to the perineum, below. In women, the vagina also passes through a hiatus in the perineal membrane just posterior to the urethral hiatus.

    Within the deep perineal pouch, a sheet of skeletal muscle functions as a sphincter, mainly for the urethra, and as a stabilizer of the posterior edge of the perineal membrane（圖 5.36 及 Table 5.3）。

    • Anteriorly, a group of muscle fibers surround the urethra and collectively form the external urethral sphincter（尿道外括約肌）。
    • Two additional groups of muscle fibers are associated with the urethra and vagina in women:
    • One group forms the sphincter urethrovaginalis（尿道陰道括約肌），which surrounds the urethra and vagina as a unit.
    • The second group forms the compressor urethrae（尿道壓迫肌），on each side，which originate from the ischiopubic rami and meet anterior to the urethra. Together with the external urethral sphincter, the sphincter urethrovaginalis and compressor urethrae facilitate closing of the urethra.
    • In both men and women, a deep transverse perineal muscle on each side parallels the free margin of the perineal membrane and joins with its partner at the midline. These muscles are thought to stabilize the position of the perineal body（會陰體）。

    Perineal Body（會陰體）

    The perineal body is an ill-defined but important connective tissue structure into which muscles of the pelvic floor and the perineum attach（圖 5.37）。It is positioned in the midline along the posterior border of the perineal membrane, to which it attaches. The posterior end of the urogenital hiatus in the levator ani muscles is also connected to it.

    The deep transverse perineal muscles intersect at the perineal body；in women, the sphincter urethrovaginalis also attaches to the perineal body. Other muscles that connect to the perineal body include the external anal sphincter, the superficial transverse perineal muscles, and the bulbospongiosus muscles of the perineum.

    Clinical Point（臨床重點）： Episiotomy（會陰切開術） During childbirth the perineal body may be stretched and torn. Traditionally it was felt that if a perineal tear is likely, the obstetrician may proceed with an episiotomy. This is a procedure in which an incision is made in the perineal body to allow the head of the fetus to pass through the vagina. There are two types of episiotomies:

    • A median episiotomy cuts through the perineal body.
    • A mediolateral episiotomy is an incision 45° from the midline.

    The maternal benefits of this procedure have been thought to be less traumatic to the perineum and to result in decreased pelvic floor dysfunction after childbirth. However, more recent evidence suggests that an episiotomy should not be performed routinely. Review of data has failed to show a decrease in pelvic floor damage with routine use of episiotomies.

    Viscera（內臟）

    The pelvic viscera include parts of the gastrointestinal system, the urinary system, and the reproductive system.

    The viscera are arranged in the midline, from front to back；the neurovascular supply is through branches that pass medially from vessels and nerves associated with the pelvic walls.

    Gastrointestinal System（胃腸系統）

    Pelvic parts of the gastrointestinal system consist mainly of the rectum and the anal canal, although the terminal part of the sigmoid colon is also in the pelvic cavity（圖 5.38）。

    Rectum（直腸）

    The rectum is continuous:

    • Above, with the sigmoid colon at about the level of vertebra SIII
    • Below, with the anal canal as this structure penetrates the pelvic floor and passes through the perineum to end as the anus

    The rectum, the most posterior element of the pelvic viscera, is immediately anterior to, and follows the concave contour of the sacrum.

    The anorectal junction is pulled forward（perineal flexure）by the action of the puborectalis part of the levator ani muscle, so the anal canal moves in a posterior direction as it passes inferiorly through the pelvic floor.

    In addition to conforming to the general curvature of the sacrum in the anteroposterior plane, the rectum has three lateral curvatures: the upper and lower curvatures to the right and the middle curvature to the left. The lower part of the rectum is expanded to form the rectal ampulla（直腸壺腹）。

    Finally, unlike the colon, the rectum lacks distinct taeniae coli muscles, omental appendices, and sacculations（haustra of the colon）。

    Anal Canal（肛管）

    The anal canal begins at the terminal end of the rectal ampulla where it narrows at the pelvic floor. It terminates as the anus after passing through the perineum. As it passes through the pelvic floor, the anal canal is surrounded along its entire length by the internal and external anal sphincters, which normally keep it closed.

    The lining of the anal canal bears a number of characteristic structural features that reflect the approximate position of the anococcygeal membrane in the fetus（which closes the terminal end of the developing gastrointestinal system in the fetus）and the transition from gastrointestinal mucosa to skin in the adult（圖 5.38B）:

    • The upper part of the anal canal is lined by mucosa similar to that lining the rectum and is distinguished by a number of longitudinally oriented folds known as anal columns（肛柱）。
    • The middle part of the anal canal is lined by smooth, pale tissue called the anal pecten（肛梳），which is bounded above by the pectinate line（齒狀線）and below by the anocutaneous line（肛皮線）。
    • The lower part of the anal canal is lined by skin continuous with the skin surrounding the anus.

    The internal anal sphincter（肛門內括約肌）is a thickening of the circular smooth muscle of the rectal wall and is therefore involuntary. The external anal sphincter（肛門外括約肌）is a cylinder of skeletal muscle that surrounds the internal anal sphincter and is under voluntary control. The external anal sphincter is described as having three parts: subcutaneous, superficial, and deep parts, which blend with each other and with the puborectalis muscle superiorly.

    Clinical Point（臨床重點）： The internal anal sphincter is innervated by the inferior rectal branch of the pudendal nerve（S2–S4），and its relaxation is mediated by parasympathetic（S2–S4）input from the pelvic splanchnic nerves. The external anal sphincter is innervated by the inferior rectal branch of the pudendal nerve（S2–S4）and by the branch from the anterior ramus of S4。The puborectalis muscle is innervated by the branch from the anterior ramus of S4。

    Anal Canal

    Anal Columns, Anal Valves, and Pectinate Line

    The anal columns are longitudinal folds of mucosa in the upper anal canal. They are united inferiorly by crescentic folds called anal valves. Superior to each valve lies a depression termed the anal sinus. The anal valves together form a circle around the anal canal at a location known as the pectinate line, which marks the approximate position of the anal membrane in the fetus.

    Anal Pecten and Anocutaneous Line

    Inferior to the pectinate line is a transition zone known as the anal pecten (anal transition zone), which is lined by nonkeratinized stratified squamous epithelium. The anal pecten ends inferiorly at the anocutaneous line (also called the “white line”), or where the lining of the anal canal becomes true skin.

    Given the position of the colon and rectum in the abdominopelvic cavity and its proximity to other organs, it is extremely important to accurately stage colorectal tumors: a tumor in the pelvis, for example, could invade the uterus or bladder.

    臨床重點：評估腫瘤是否發生扩散可能需要超聲波掃描、電腦斷層（CT）和磁振造影（MRI）。

    Rectum and Anal Canal — Clinical Correlations

    Digital Rectal Examination (DRE)

    A digital rectal examination (DRE) is performed by placing the gloved and lubricated index finger into the rectum through the anus. The anal mucosa can be palpated for abnormal masses, and in women, the posterior wall of the vagina and the cervix can be palpated. In men, the prostate can be evaluated for any extraneous nodules or masses.

    In many instances the digital rectal examination may be followed by proctoscopy or colonoscopy. An ultrasound probe may be placed into the rectum to assess the gynecological structures in females and the prostate in the male before performing a prostatic biopsy.

    臨床重點：直腸指檢是快速篩查骨盆腔腫瘤的重要工具，臨床上常作為 Proctoscopy 或 Colonoscopy 的前導檢查。

    Carcinoma of the Colon and Rectum

    Carcinoma of the colon and rectum (colorectum) is a common and often lethal disease. Recent advances in surgery, radiotherapy, and chemotherapy have only slightly improved 5-year survival rates.

    The biological behavior of tumors of the colon and rectum is relatively predictable. Most tumors develop from benign polyps, some of which undergo malignant change. The overall prognosis is related to:

    • the degree of tumor penetration through the bowel wall,
    • the presence or absence of lymphatic dissemination, and
    • the presence or absence of systemic metastases.

    臨床重點：大腸直腸癌的預後與腫瘤穿透腸壁深度、淋巴轉移與遠端轉移與否密切相關。骨盆腔內的腫瘤可能侵犯膀胱、子宮或直腸本身。

    Vagina

    (Content in this segment is primarily referenced within clinical examination contexts. The vagina is described in relation to its anatomical position and proximity to other pelvic organs.)

    The vagina is a fibromuscular canal extending from the vestibule to the uterus. Its posterior wall is closely related to the rectum and anal canal, separated by the rectovaginal septum. During digital rectal examination in women, the posterior wall of the vagina and the cervix can be palpated through the anterior rectal wall.

    Vaginal Openings and Supporting Structures

    The vaginal opening in the vestibule is surrounded by the deep perineal pouch and perineal membrane. The relationship between the urethral opening (anterior) and vaginal opening (posterior) is clinically significant for catheterization and examination.

    臨床重點：在女性中，尿道較短（約 4 公分），使得她們比男性更容易發生膀胱感染（膀胱炎）。尿道開口位於前庭，陰道開口位於其後方。

    Urinary System — Pelvic Part

    Ureters in the Pelvis

    The pelvic parts of the urinary system consist of the terminal parts of the ureters, the bladder, and the proximal part of the urethra.

    The ureters enter the pelvic cavity from the abdomen by passing through the pelvic inlet. On each side, the ureter crosses the pelvic inlet and enters the pelvic cavity in the area anterior to the bifurcation of the common iliac artery. From this point, it continues along the pelvic wall and floor to join the base of the bladder.

    In the pelvis, the ureter is crossed by:

    • the ductus deferens in men, and
    • the uterine artery in women.

    臨床重點：臨床上，輸尿管在骨盆腔的手術中容易被忽略，特別是在，子宮切除術或直腸癌手術中，必須小心辨識並保護輸尿管。

    Bladder

    The bladder is the most anterior element of the pelvic viscera. Although it is entirely situated in the pelvic cavity when empty, it expands superiorly into the abdominal cavity when full.

    Bladder Shape and Surfaces

    The empty bladder is shaped like a three-sided pyramid that has tipped over to lie on one of its margins. It has an apex, a base, a superior surface, and two inferolateral surfaces.

    • Apex: directed toward the top of the pubic symphysis; the median umbilical ligament (a remnant of the embryological urachus) continues from it superiorly up the anterior abdominal wall to the umbilicus.
    • Base: shaped like an inverted triangle and faces posteroinferiorly. The two ureters enter the bladder at each of the upper corners of the base, and the urethra drains inferiorly from the lower corner. The smooth triangular area between the openings of the ureters and urethra on the inside of the bladder is known as the trigone.
    • Inferolateral surfaces: cradled between the levator ani muscles of the pelvic diaphragm and the adjacent obturator internus muscles above the attachment of the pelvic diaphragm.
    • Superior surface: slightly domed when the bladder is empty; balloons upward as the bladder fills.

    Neck of the Bladder

    The neck of the bladder surrounds the origin of the urethra at the point where the two inferolateral surfaces and the base intersect. The neck is the most inferior part of the bladder and also the most “fixed” part. It is anchored into position by a pair of tough fibromuscular bands, which connect the neck and pelvic part of the urethra to the posteroinferior aspect of each pubic bone.

    • In women, these fibromuscular bands are termed pubovesical ligaments.
    • In men, the paired bands are known as puboprostatic ligaments because they blend with the fibrous capsule of the prostate.

    臨床重點：雖然膀胱在成人被視為骨盆腔器官，但在兒童時期位置較高。出生時膀胱幾乎完全位於腹腔，隨著年齡增長下降，直到青春期後才達到成人位置。

    Bladder Stones

    In some patients, small calculi (stones) form in the kidneys. These may pass down the ureter, causing ureteric obstruction, and into the bladder, where insoluble salts further precipitate on these small calculi to form larger calculi. Often these patients develop (or may already have) problems with bladder emptying, which leaves residual urine in the bladder. This urine may become infected, which alters the pH of the urine, permitting further precipitation of insoluble salts.

    If small enough, the stones may be removed via a transurethral route using specialized instruments. If the stones are too big, it may be necessary to make a suprapubic incision and enter the bladder retroperitoneally to remove them.

    臨床重點：膀胱結石的形成與尿路阻塞、感染及尿液滯留密切相關。治療方式取決於結石大小：小結石可經尿道移除，大結石則需經恥骨上切口進行開放手術。

    Bladder Cancer

    Bladder cancer is the most common tumor of the urinary tract and is usually a disease of the sixth and seventh decades, although there is an increasing trend for younger patients to develop this disease.

    Approximately one-third of bladder tumors are multifocal; fortunately, two-thirds are superficial tumors and amenable to local treatment.

    Bladder tumors may spread through the bladder wall and invade local structures, including the rectum, uterus (in women), and lateral walls of the pelvic cavity. Prostatic involvement is not uncommon in male patients. The disease spreads via the internal iliac lymph nodes. Spread to distant metastatic sites rarely includes the lung.

    Treatment for early-stage tumors includes local resection with preservation of the bladder. Diffuse tumors may be treated with local chemotherapy; more extensive tumors may require radical surgical removal of the bladder and, in men, the prostate.

    Large bladder tumors may produce complications, including invasion and obstruction of the ureters. Ureteric obstruction can then obstruct the kidneys and induce kidney failure. Moreover, bladder tumors can invade other structures of the pelvic cavity.

    臨床重點：膀胱癌最常見的病理類型為泌尿道上皮癌（urothelial carcinoma）。腫瘤分期決定治療策略：淺表性腫瘤可作局部切除，侵犯性腫瘤可能需要根除性膀胱切除術（radical cystectomy）。

    Urethra

    The urethra begins at the base of the bladder and ends with an external opening in the perineum. The paths taken by the urethra differ significantly in women and men.

    Urethra in Women

    In women, the urethra is short, being about 4 cm long. It travels a slightly curved course as it passes inferiorly through the pelvic floor into the perineum, where it passes through the deep perineal pouch and perineal membrane before opening in the vestibule that lies between the labia minora.

    The urethral opening is anterior to the vaginal opening in the vestibule. The inferior aspect of the urethra is bound to the anterior surface of the vagina. Two small paraurethral mucous glands (Skene’s glands) are associated with the lower end of the urethra. Each drains via a duct that opens onto the lateral margin of the external urethral orifice.

    臨床重點：女性尿道較短（約 4 公分），使得她們比男性更容易發生膀胱感染。Skene’s glands（斯基恩腺）是尿道旁腺，為女性前列腺素（prostaglandin）的同源結構。

    Urethra in Men

    In men, the urethra is long, about 20 cm, and bends twice along its course. Beginning at the base of the bladder and passing inferiorly through the prostate, it passes through the deep perineal pouch and perineal membrane and immediately enters the root of the penis. As the urethra exits the deep perineal pouch, it bends forward to course anteriorly in the root of the penis. When the penis is flaccid, the urethra makes another bend, this time inferiorly, when passing from the root to the body of the penis. During erection, the bend between the root and body of the penis disappears.

    The urethra in men is divided into four parts:

    1. Preprostatic Part

    The preprostatic part of the urethra is about 1 cm long, extends from the base of the bladder to the prostate, and is associated with a circular cuff of smooth muscle fibers (the internal urethral sphincter). Contraction of this sphincter prevents retrograde movement of semen into the bladder during ejaculation.

    2. Prostatic Part

    The prostatic part of the urethra is 3 to 4 cm long and is surrounded by the prostate. In this region, the lumen of the urethra is marked by a longitudinal midline fold of mucosa (the urethral crest). The depression on each side of the crest is the prostatic sinus; the ducts of the prostate empty into these two sinuses.

    Midway along its length, the urethral crest is enlarged to form a somewhat circular elevation (the seminal colliculus). In men, the seminal colliculus is used to determine the position of the prostate gland during transurethral resection of the prostate.

    A small blind-ended pouch—the prostatic utricle (thought to be the homologue of the uterus in women)—opens onto the center of the seminal colliculus. On each side of the prostatic utricle is the opening of the ejaculatory duct. Therefore the connection between the urinary and reproductive tracts in men occurs in the prostatic part of the urethra.

    3. Membranous Part

    The membranous part of the urethra is narrow and passes through the deep perineal pouch. During its transit through this pouch, the urethra, in both men and women, is surrounded by skeletal muscle of the external urethral sphincter.

    4. Spongy (Penile) Part

    The spongy urethra is surrounded by erectile tissue (the corpus spongiosum) of the penis. It is enlarged to form a bulb at the base of the penis and again at the end of the penis to form the navicular fossa. The two bulbourethral glands in the deep perineal pouch are part of the male reproductive system and open into the bulb of the spongy urethra. The external urethral orifice is the sagittal slit at the end of the penis.

    臨床重點：臨床上，經尿道導尿或膀胱鏡檢查時必須注意男性尿道的三個狹窄處（internal urethral sphincter、membranous urethra 及 external urethral orifice）以及兩個彎曲（preprostatic curve 和 bend at the root-body junction of the penis）。

    Clinical Correlations — Urethra

    Bladder Infection (Cystitis)

    The relatively short length of the urethra in women makes them more susceptible than men to bladder infection. The primary symptom of urinary tract infection in women is usually inflammation of the bladder (cystitis). The infection can be controlled in most instances by oral antibiotics and resolves without complication. In children under 1 year of age, infection from the bladder may spread via the ureters to the kidneys, where it can produce renal damage and ultimately lead to renal failure. Early diagnosis and treatment are necessary.

    臨床重點：一歲以下幼童的膀胱炎可能經輸尿管逆行上升至腎臟，造成腎損傷甚至腎衰竭，需要早期診斷與治療。

    Urethral Catheterization

    Urethral catheterization is often performed to drain urine from a patient’s bladder when the patient is unable to micturate. When inserting urinary catheters, it is important to appreciate the gender anatomy of the patient.

    In men:

    • The spongy urethra is surrounded by the erectile tissue of the bulb of the penis immediately inferior to the deep perineal pouch. The wall of this short segment of urethra is relatively thin and angles superiorly to pass through the deep perineal pouch; at this position the urethra is vulnerable to damage, notably during cystoscopy.
    • The membranous part of the urethra runs superiorly as it passes through the deep perineal pouch.
    • The prostatic part of the urethra takes a slight concave curve anteriorly as it passes through the prostate gland.

    In women, it is much simpler to pass catheters and cystoscopes because the urethra is short and straight. Urine may therefore be readily drained from a distended bladder without significant concern for urethral rupture.

    Occasionally, it is impossible to pass any form of instrumentation through the urethra to drain the bladder, usually because there is a urethral stricture or prostatic enlargement. In such cases, an ultrasound of the lower abdomen will demonstrate a full bladder behind the anterior abdominal wall. A suprapubic catheter may be inserted into the bladder with minimal trauma through a small incision under local anesthetic.

    臨床重點：當導尿管無法通過尿道時（如尿道狹窄或前列腺肥大），超聲波可確認膀胱脹大，**恥骨上導管（suprapubic catheter）**可經恥骨上方小切口安全置入膀胱引流。

    Suprapubic Catheterization

    In certain instances it is necessary to catheterize the bladder through the anterior abdominal wall—for example, when the prostate is markedly enlarged and it is impossible to pass a urethral catheter, a suprapubic catheter may be placed.

    The bladder is a retroperitoneal structure and when full lies adjacent to the anterior abdominal wall. Ultrasound visualization of the bladder may be useful in assessing the size of this structure and, importantly, differentiating this structure from other potential abdominal masses.

    The procedure of suprapubic catheterization is straightforward and involves the passage of a small catheter on a needle in the midline approximately 2 cm above the pubic symphysis. The catheter passes easily into the bladder without compromise of other structures and permits free drainage.

    臨床重點：恥骨上導管置入術的優點是可避免尿道操作，減少尿道損傷風險，特別適用於男性前列腺肥大或尿道狹窄病患。

    Male Reproductive System — Overview

    The reproductive system in men has components in the abdomen, pelvis, and perineum. The major components are a testis, epididymis, ductus deferens, and ejaculatory duct on each side, and the urethra and penis in the midline. In addition, three types of accessory glands are associated with the system:

    • a single prostate,
    • a pair of seminal vesicles, and
    • a pair of bulbourethral glands.

    Testes

    The testes originally develop high on the posterior abdominal wall and then descend, normally before birth, through the inguinal canal in the anterior abdominal wall and into the scrotum of the perineum. During descent, the testes carry their vessels, lymphatics, and nerves, as well as their principal drainage ducts, the ductus deferens (vas deferens) with them. The lymph drainage of the testes is therefore to the lateral aortic or lumbar nodes and preaortic nodes in the abdomen, and not to the inguinal or pelvic lymph nodes.

    Each ellipsoid-shaped testis is enclosed within the end of an elongated musculofascial pouch, which is continuous with the anterior abdominal wall and projects into the scrotum. The spermatic cord is the tube-shaped connection between the pouch in the scrotum and the abdominal wall.

    The sides and anterior aspect of the testis are covered by a closed sac of peritoneum (the tunica vaginalis), which originally connected to the abdominal cavity. Normally after testicular descent, the connection closes, leaving a fibrous remnant.

    Each testis is composed of seminiferous tubules and interstitial tissue surrounded by a thick connective tissue capsule (the tunica albuginea). Spermatozoa are produced by the seminiferous tubules. The 400 to 600 highly coiled seminiferous tubules are modified at each end to become straight tubules, which connect to a collecting chamber (the rete testis) in a thick, vertically oriented linear wedge of connective tissue (the mediastinum testis), projecting from the capsule into the posterior aspect of the gonad. Approximately 12 to 20 efferent ductules originate from the upper end of the rete testis, penetrate the capsule, and connect with the epididymis.

    臨床重點：睪丸的淋巴引流至腹主動脈旁淋巴結和腰淋巴結，而非腹股溝或骨盆腔淋巴結。這對睪丸腫瘤的分期和治療有重要意義。

    Testicular Tumors

    Tumors of the testis account for a small percentage of malignancies in men. However, they generally occur in younger patients (between 20 and 40 years of age). When diagnosed at an early stage, most of these tumors are curable by surgery and chemotherapy.

    Early diagnosis of testicular tumor is extremely important. Abnormal lumps can be detected by palpation, and diagnosis can be made using ultrasound. Simple ultrasound scanning can reveal the extent of the local tumor, usually at an early stage.

    Surgical removal of the malignant testis is often carried out using an inguinal approach. The testis is not usually removed through a scrotal incision, because it is possible to spread tumor cells into the subcutaneous tissues of the scrotum, which has a different lymphatic drainage than the testis.

    臨床重點：睪丸腫瘤的治療原則是經腹股溝睪丸切除術（radical inguinal orchiectomy），而非經陰囊切口，以避免腫瘤細胞擴散至陰囊皮下組織，進而改變淋巴引流路徑。

    Epididymis

    The epididymis courses along the posterolateral side of the testis. It has two distinct components:

    • the efferent ductules, which form an enlarged coiled mass that sits on the posterior superior pole of the testis and forms the head of the epididymis; and
    • the true epididymis, which is a single, long coiled duct into which the efferent ductules all drain, and which continues inferiorly along the posterolateral margin of the testis as the body of the epididymis and enlarges to form the tail of the epididymis at the inferior pole of the testis.

    During passage through the epididymis, spermatozoa acquire the ability to move and fertilize an egg. The epididymis also stores spermatozoa until ejaculation. The end of the epididymis is continuous with the ductus deferens.

    Ductus Deferens

    The ductus deferens (vas deferens) is a long muscular duct that transports spermatozoa from the tail of the epididymis in the scrotum to the ejaculatory duct in the pelvic cavity. It ascends in the scrotum as a component of the spermatic cord and passes through the inguinal canal in the anterior abdominal wall.

    After passing through the deep inguinal ring, the ductus deferens bends medially around the lateral side of the inferior epigastric artery and crosses the external iliac artery and the external iliac vein at the pelvic inlet to enter the pelvic cavity.

    The duct descends medially on the pelvic wall, deep to the peritoneum, and crosses the ureter posterior to the bladder. It continues inferomedially along the base of the bladder, anterior to the rectum, almost to the midline, where it is joined by the duct of the seminal vesicle to form the ejaculatory duct. The ejaculatory duct penetrates through the prostate gland to connect with the prostatic urethra.

    Between the ureter and ejaculatory duct, the ductus deferens expands to form the ampulla of the ductus deferens.

    臨床重點：輸精管（Ductus deferens）在骨盆腔中位於腹膜下方，先橫過骼外血管，再於膀胱底與輸尿管交叉，最後與精囊管匯合成射精管。

    Vasectomy

    The ductus deferens transports spermatozoa from the tail of the epididymis in the scrotum to the ejaculatory duct in the pelvic cavity. Because it has a thick smooth muscle wall, it can be easily palpated in the spermatic cord between the testes and the superficial inguinal ring. Also, because it can be accessed through skin and superficial fascia, it is amenable to surgical dissection and surgical division. When this is carried out bilaterally (vasectomy), the patient is rendered sterile—this is a useful method for male contraception.

    臨床重點：輸精管切除術（Vasectomy）是一種男性絕育手術，通過雙側切斷輸精管阻止精子輸出。術後仍需一段時間（及多次精液分析）才能確認避孕效果，因為殘餘精子可在術後數週內持續存在。

    Seminal Vesicle

    每個 seminal vesicle 是男性生殖系統的附屬腺體，由 ductus deferens（輸精管）基部發出一個盲端管狀突起發育而成（圖 5.46A）。該管盤繞並具有許多囊狀突起，被結締組織包覆，形成位於膀胱與直腸之間的長條狀結構。該腺體位於膀胱基部外側，與輸精管走行方向一致。

    Seminal vesicle 的排泄管與 ductus deferens 匯合，形成 ejaculatory duct（射精管）（圖 5.47）。Seminal vesicle 的分泌物占精液（semen）體積的很大比例。

    Prostate

    Prostate（前列腺）是男性生殖系統中不成對的附屬結構，位於骨盆腔中環繞 urethra（尿道）（圖 5.46A 與 5.47）。其位於膀胱正下方、pubic symphysis（恥骨聯合）後方、直腸前方。

    Prostate 形似倒置的圓錐，底部較寬，向上與膀胱頸部連續；頂部較窄，向下坐落於骨盆底。Prostate 的下外側表面與 levator ani muscles（提肛肌）相接，兩側肌肉共同托住前列腺。

    Prostate 由 30 至 40 個獨立的複雜腺體發育而成，這些腺體從 urethra（尿道）上皮向尿道周圍壁內生長。所有腺體 collectively 使尿道壁增厚形成所謂的前列腺；但各腺體仍保留各自的排泄管，獨立開口於尿道腔後壁的 prostatic sinuses（前列腺竇）（見圖 5.44C）。

    Prostate 的分泌物與 seminal vesicles 的分泌物共同構成射精時的精液。

    Ejaculatory ducts 幾乎垂直地沿前下方向穿過 prostate 後部，開口於 prostatic urethra（前列腺尿道）。

    臨床重點：前列腺解剖學的分區（周邊區、過渡區、中央區）對臨床診斷極為重要，各區域與不同疾病的發生部位相關（見圖 5.47）。

    Zonal Anatomy（分區解剖）

    • Transitional zone（過渡區）：前方（非腺體區域）
    • Peripheral zone（周邊區）
    • Central zone（中央區）
    • Area of seminal colliculus（精阜區域）

    Bulbo-urethral Glands

    Bulbo-urethral glands（尿道球腺）（見圖 5.46A），左右各一，是位於deep perineal pouch（深會陰窩）內的小型豌豆狀黏液腺。它們位於 membranous urethra（膜部尿道）的外側。每側腺體的排泄管向下內側穿過 perineal membrane（會陰膜），開口於陰莖根部 spongy urethra（海綿部尿道）的 bulb（球部）。

    Bulbo-urethral glands 與沿海綿部尿道全程的小腺體共同作用，潤滑尿道及射精前的陰莖分泌物。

    In the Clinic — Prostate Problems

    臨床重點：Prostate cancer（前列腺癌）是男性最常見的惡性腫瘤之一，確診時往往已屬晚期。前列腺癌通常發生於前列腺的周邊區（peripheral zone）（見圖 5.47），且相對無症狀。多數病例透過數位直腸檢查（DRE）（圖 5.48A）及血液檢查確診，包括血清酸性磷酸酶及血清前列腺特異抗原（PSA）。直腸檢查時，腫瘤化的前列腺觸感如「石頭」般堅硬。診斷通常需取得前列腺多處活檢組織。超聲波在活檢過程中用於成像，以測量前列腺大小及引導針頭定位。

    臨床重點：Benign prostatic hypertrophy（良性前列腺增生）是一种隨年齡增長而影響大多數男性的疾病（圖 5.48B）。此病通常涉及前列腺較中央的區域（見圖 5.47），使前列腺逐漸增大。直腸檢查時前列腺觸感「厚實」。由於前列腺中央區域的增生性改變，尿道受到壓迫，部分患者出現尿路出口阻塞。長期下來，膀胱可能因應尿路出口阻塞而出現肥厚。某些男性患者阻塞嚴重到無法排尿，需進行經尿道或恥骨上導尿。儘管是良性疾病，良性前列腺增生對許多患者的日常生活有顯著影響。

    Female Reproductive Tract — Overview

    女性生殖道主要位於骨盆腔和會陰，懷孕期間，子宮會擴展進入腹腔。系統的主要組成包括：

    • 每側的 ovary（卵巢）
    • 正中的 uterus（子宮）、vagina（陰道）和 clitoris（陰蒂）（圖 5.49）
    • 一對附屬腺體（greater vestibular glands，前庭大腺）與生殖道相連

    Ovaries

    與男性睪丸類似，ovaries（卵巢）最初在後腹壁高位發育，出生前下降，攜帶其血管、淋巴管和神經。不同的是，卵巢不通過腹股溝管進入會陰，而是停在骨盆腔側壁的位置（圖 5.50）。

    Ovaries 是卵子生成（oogenesis）的場所。成熟卵子在排卵時進入腹膜腔，通常由輸卵管的纖毛將其引導至輸卵管的開口。

    Ovaries 位於骨盆入口下方，鄰近骨盆側壁。兩側卵巢均呈杏仁狀，約 3 公分長，由一層 mesentery（mesovarium，卵巢繫膜）懸吊，該繫膜是broad ligament（寬韌帶）的後延伸。

    In the Clinic — Ovarian Cancer

    臨床重點：Ovarian cancer（卵巢癌）是腫瘤學領域的重大挑戰之一。卵巢含有多種細胞類型，各皆可發生惡性變化，需不同的影像學和治療方案，且預後各異。

    卵巢腫瘤最常起源於覆蓋卵巢的表面上皮，該上皮在 sharp transition zone（銳利轉換區）與卵巢繫膜的腹膜延續。

    許多因素與卵巢腫瘤的發生相關，包括強烈的家族史。卵巢癌可發生於任何年齡，但更典型地見於老年女性。

    卵巢癌可經血液和淋巴管轉移，並經常直接擴散至腹膜腔。這種直接腹膜腔擴散使腫瘤細胞可沿**腸繫膜溝槽（paracolic gutters）**及肝臟表面傳播，導致疾病輕易蔓延。不幸的是，許多患者確診時已有轉移性和瀰漫性疾病（圖 5.51）。

    In the Clinic — Imaging the Ovary

    臨床重點：卵巢可使用超聲波進行顯像。若患者飲入足夠水分，膀胱會擴張充盈。這一充滿液體的腔隙提供絕佳的聲學窗口，經此可由經腹壁超聲波掃描識別，子宮和卵巢。此技術也讓婦產科醫師和技術人員能夠觀察胎兒並記錄其整個孕期的生長發育。

    部分患者不適合經腹壁掃描，此時可將探頭插入陰道，近距離顯像子宫、子宮直腸窩（pouch of Douglas，道格拉斯窩）內容物及卵巢。卵巢亦可透過腹腔鏡進行顯像。

    Uterus

    Uterus（子宫）是一個厚壁肌肉器官，位於膀胱與直腸之間的正中線上（見圖 5.50）。由body（宮體）和cervix（宮頸）組成，下方與vagina（陰道）相連（圖 5.52）。上方，uterine tubes（输卵管）從子宫體上部兩側伸出，開口於紧邻卵巢的腹膜腔。

    子宫體前後扁平，在输卵管起点水平以上（圖 5.52），其上端呈圓形（fundus of the uterus，子宫底）。子宫體腔側面觀呈窄縫，前面觀呈倒三角形。宮腔上部兩角分別與输卵管腔相通，下角與子宫頸管相通。

    受精卵（blastocyst） 通常植入子宫體。懷孕期間，子宫顯著向上擴展進入腹腔。

    In the Clinic — Hysterectomy

    臨床重點：Hysterectomy（子宫切除术）是手术切除子宫。通常完全切除子宫體、底和宫颈，偶爾可保留宫颈。有時也一併切除输卵管（uterine/fallopian tubes）和卵巢，此稱為经腹全子宫切除术及双侧输卵管卵巢切除术（total abdominal hysterectomy and bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy）。

    子宫切除术、卵巢切除术及输卵管卵巢切除术可应用于生殖系统恶性肿瘤（如子宫颈癌、子宫体癌及卵巢癌）的患者。其他适应症包括强烈的家族性生殖疾病史、子宫内膜异位症（endometriosis） 及异常出血。偶爾因产后出血过多，需进行产后子宫切除。

    子宫切除术經由横向耻骨上切口（Pfannenstiel’s incision）进行。术中需极大谨慎辨认远端输尿管，并在不损伤输尿管的情况下结扎附近的子宫动脉。

    Uterine Tubes

    Uterine tubes（输卵管）从子宫体上端两侧延伸至骨盆侧壁，包埋于broad ligament（宽韧带）mesosalpinx 部的上缘内（見 p. 483）。由於卵巢悬吊于宽韧带后方，输卵管从其上方越过，终止于卵巢的外侧。

    每條输卵管末端呈喇叭狀擴大（infundibulum，漏斗部），环绕相關卵巢的上外极（圖 5.53）。infundibulum 边缘有稱為 fimbriae（纖毛）的指狀突起。输卵管腔经 infundibulum 的狭窄端开口于腹膜腔。infundibulum 内侧，管腔扩大形成 ampulla（壶腹），随后变窄形成 isthmus（峡部），最后连于子宫体。

    纖毛狀 infundibulum 有助於收集卵巢排出的卵子。受精通常在 ampulla 發生。

    In the Clinic — Tubal Ligation

    臨床重點：排卵後，未受精卵由输卵管纖毛收集。卵子進入输卵管，通常在 ampulla 受精。受精卵隨即開始發育，進入子宫腔，植入子宫壁。

    一種簡單有效的避孕方法是手術結扎（夾閉）输卵管，阻止精子到達卵子。這一簡短手術在全身麻醉下進行。經腹膜腔置入小型腹腔鏡，使用特殊設備辨識输卵管後進行夾閉。

    Cervix

    Cervix（宫颈）構成子宫的下部，呈短而寬的圓柱狀，中央有狹窄通道。子宫體通常向前拱起（相對於宫颈呈前屈（anteflexed）），覆於排空膀胱的上表面（圖 5.54A）。此外，宫颈相對於阴道向前傾斜（前傾（anteverted）），使得宫颈下端突入陰道上部前方。宫颈末端呈穹頂狀，突入陰道，在宫颈與陰道壁交界處形成穹窿（fornix）（圖 5.54B）。宫颈的管狀中央管在下方以external os（外口）開口於陰道腔，在上方以internal os（內口）開口於子宫腔。

    In the Clinic — Carcinoma of the Cervix and Uterus

    臨床重點：宫颈癌（圖 5.55）和子宫体癌是女性的常見疾病。診斷方法包括視診、細胞學檢查（宮頸細胞檢驗）、影像學檢查、活檢及擴張刮宮術（D&C）。

    宫颈及子宫体癌的治療可採用局部切除、子宫切除术及輔助化療。腫瘤經淋巴管轉移至髂內淋巴結和髂總淋巴結。

    Vagina

    Vagina（阴道）是女性的性交器官，是一個可擴張的纖維肌肉管道，從會陰延伸穿過骨盆底進入骨盆腔（圖 5.56A）。管道內端擴大形成阴道穹窿（vaginal vault）。

    阴道前壁與膀胱底及尿道相鄰；事實上，尿道嵌入或融合於阴道前壁。阴道後方主要與直腸相鄰。

    阴道下端開口於會陰前庭，位於尿道外口的正後方。從其外口（introitus，陰道口），阴道向後上走行，穿過會陰膜進入骨盆腔，其前壁附著於宫颈的環形邊緣。

    阴道穹窿（vaginal fornix） 是宫颈边缘与阴道壁之间的凹陷。按位置分為後穹窿（posterior fornix）、前穹窿（anterior fornix） 和兩個側穹窿（lateral fornices）（圖 5.56A，見圖 5.54）。

    阴道腔通常處於塌陷狀態，前壁與後壁相接。醫師可使用**窺器（speculum）**打開阴道腔，觀察宫腔下端的穹頂狀末端、阴道穹窿及宫颈管外口（圖 5.56B）。

    性交時，精液沉積於阴道穹窿。精子進入宫颈管外口，穿過宫颈管進入子宫腔，然後繼續進入输卵管，受精通常在 ampulla 發生。

    Pelvic Fascia

    骨盆腔內的pelvic fascia（骨盆筋膜）襯於骨盆壁，包繞骨盆內臟器的基部，並形成繞經骨盆壁到達正中線內臟的神經血管鞘。骨盆筋膜是腹部腹膜外結締組織層的延續。

    In Women

    女性中，rectovaginal septum（直腸陰道隔）將阴道後表面與直腸分開（圖 5.57A）。筋膜增厚形成韌帶，從宫颈延伸到：

    • 前方：pubocervical ligament（恥骨宮頸韌帶）
    • 側方：transverse cervical ligament 或 cardinal ligament（宮頸橫韌帶/主韌帶）
    • 後方：uterosacral ligament（宮骶韌帶）

    這些韌帶連同會陰膜、levator ani muscles（提肛肌）及perineal body（會陰體），共同維持子宫在骨盆腔中的穩定。其中最重要的是宮頸橫韌帶/主韌帶，從宫颈和阴道穹窿每側延伸到相應的骨盆壁。

    臨床重點：Recto-uterine pouch（直腸，子宮窩，又稱 pouch of Douglas）是介於直腸與子宫之間極為重要的臨床區域。病人仰臥時，recto-uterine pouch 是腹膜腔的最低處，是感染和液體聚集的常見部位。經腹壁無法觸診此區域，但可通過經陰道和經直腸指檢進行檢查。若疑似膿腫，可經陰道或直腸引流，無需經腹手術。

    In Men

    男性中，環繞前列腺前方和外側區域的筋膜（prostatic fascia，前列腺筋膜）包裹並圍繞前列腺靜脈叢（prostatic plexus of veins），後方與rectovesical septum（直腸膀胱隔）延續，該隔將前列腺及膀胱底後表面與直腸分開（圖 5.57B）。

    Peritoneum

    骨盆腔的peritoneum（腹膜）在骨盆入口處與腹部腹膜連續。在骨盆腔中，腹膜覆蓋在正中的骨盆內臟，形成：

    • 相鄰內臟之間的陷窩（pouches）
    • 內臟與骨盆壁之間的皺襞和韌帶（folds and ligaments）

    前方，median 和 medial umbilical folds（臍正中皺襞和臍內側皺襞）覆蓋胚胎結構臍尿管（urachus） 和臍動脈（umbilical arteries） 的遺蹟（圖 5.58）。這些皺襞向上離開骨盆腔，進入前腹壁。後方，腹膜覆蓋直腸上三分之一的正面和外側面，但僅直腸中三分之一的前表面被腹膜覆蓋；直腸下三分之一完全無腹膜覆蓋。

    In Women

    女性中，uterus 位於膀胱與直腸之間，uterine tubes 從子宫上部延伸到骨盆側壁（圖 5.58A）。因此，前方在膀胱與子宫之間形成淺的膀胱，子宮窩（vesico-uterine pouch），後方在子宫與直腸之間形成深的直腸，子宮窩（recto-uterine pouch，pouch of Douglas）。此外，每側子宫旁有一大片腹膜皺襞（broad ligament，寬韌帶），其上緣包繞输卵管，後方附著卵巢，從子宫延伸到骨盆側壁。

    正中線上，腹膜下降覆蓋子宫和宫颈的後表面，並延伸到阴道後壁邻近後阴道穹窿處，然後折返覆蓋直腸的前壁和側壁。在直腸前表面與子宫、宫颈及阴道後表面之間形成的深腹膜窩即為直腸，子宮窩。在直腸，子宮窩基部附近，每側有一尖銳的鏟形腹膜皺襞（recto-uterine fold），覆蓋於**宮骶韌帶（uterosacral ligaments）**之上，宮骶韌帶是從宫颈延伸到骨盆後側壁的骨盆筋膜增厚帶。

    Broad Ligament

    Broad ligament（寬韌帶）是一片腹膜摺疊，冠狀面定向，從骨盆側壁延伸到子宫，上緣包繞输卵管，後方懸吊卵巢（圖 5.58A）。Uterine arteries（子宫动脉）在寬韌帶基部越過输尿管，卵巢韌帶（ligament of ovary） 和子宫圓韌帶（round ligament of uterus） 分別包埋在寬韌帶與卵巢和子宫相關的部分中。寬韌帶分為三部：

    • Mesometrium：寬韌帶的最大部分，從骨盆側壁延伸到子宫體
    • Mesosalpinx：寬韌帶的最上部分，在骨盆腔中懸吊输卵管
    • Mesovarium：寬韌帶向後的延伸，附著於卵巢

    Mesovarium 的腹膜與卵巢表面緊密附著，成為卵巢的表面上皮（surface epithelium）。卵巢長軸呈垂直方向。卵巢血管、神經和淋巴管從外側進入卵巢上極，被另一層抬高的腹膜皺襞覆蓋，該皺襞及其包含的結構共同形成卵巢懸韌帶（suspensory ligament of the ovary，infundibulopelvic ligament）。

    卵巢下極附著於一條纖維肌肉組織帶（ligament of the ovary，卵巢韌帶），該韌帶沿 mesovarium 緣向內側走行至子宫，然後繼續向前外側延伸為子宫圓韌帶（round ligament of the uterus）（圖 5.58A）。子宫圓韌帶越過骨盆入口到達深腹股溝環（deep inguinal ring），然後穿過腹股溝管，終止於會陰中與labium majus（大陰唇）相關的結締組織。卵巢韌帶和子宫圓韌帶都是胚胎時期引帶（gubernaculum） 的遺蹟，引帶使性腺附著於陰唇陰囊隆起。

    Internal Iliac Artery

    The internal iliac artery originates from the common iliac artery on each side, approximately at the level of the intervertebral disc between LV and SI, and lies anteromedial to the sacro-iliac joint (Fig. 5.63). The vessel courses inferiorly over the pelvic inlet and then divides into anterior and posterior trunks at the level of the superior border of the greater sciatic foramen. Branches from the posterior trunk contribute to the supply of the lower posterior abdominal wall, the posterior pelvic wall, and the gluteal region. Branches from the anterior trunk supply the pelvic viscera, the perineum, the gluteal region, the adductor region of the thigh, and, in the fetus, the placenta.

    臨床重點：內骼動器的分支部於骨盆入口附近，在胎兒時期還負責將血液輸送至胎盤。出生後，臍動器會閉合並形成內臍韌帶（medial umbilical ligament）。

    Posterior Trunk

    Branches of the posterior trunk of the internal iliac artery are the iliolumbar artery, the lateral sacral artery, and the superior gluteal artery (Fig. 5.63).

    Iliolumbar Artery

    The iliolumbar artery ascends laterally back out of the pelvic inlet and divides into a lumbar branch and an iliac branch. The lumbar branch contributes to the supply of the posterior abdominal wall, psoas and quadratus lumborum muscles, and cauda equina, via a small spinal branch that passes through the intervertebral foramen between LV and SI. The iliac branch passes laterally into the iliac fossa to supply muscle and bone.

    Lateral Sacral Arteries

    The lateral sacral arteries, usually two, originate from the posterior division of the internal iliac artery and course medially and inferiorly along the posterior pelvic wall. They give rise to branches that pass into the anterior sacral foramina to supply related bone and soft tissues, structures in the vertebral (sacral) canal, and skin and muscle posterior to the sacrum.

    Superior Gluteal Artery

    The superior gluteal artery is the largest branch of the internal iliac artery and is the terminal continuation of the posterior trunk. It courses posteriorly, usually passing between the lumbosacral trunk and anterior ramus of S1, to leave the pelvic cavity through the greater sciatic foramen above the piriformis muscle and enter the gluteal region of the lower limb. This vessel makes a substantial contribution to the blood supply of muscles and skin in the gluteal region and also supplies branches to adjacent muscles and bones of the pelvic walls.

    Anterior Trunk

    Branches of the anterior trunk of the internal iliac artery include the superior vesical artery, the umbilical artery, the inferior vesical artery, the middle rectal artery, the uterine artery, the vaginal artery, the obturator artery, the internal pudendal artery, and the inferior gluteal artery (Fig. 5.64).

    Umbilical Artery

    The first branch of the anterior trunk is the umbilical artery, which gives origin to the superior vesical artery and then travels forward just inferior to the margin of the pelvic inlet. Anteriorly, the vessel leaves the pelvic cavity and ascends on the internal aspect of the anterior abdominal wall to reach the umbilicus. In the fetus, the umbilical artery is large and carries blood from the fetus to the placenta. After birth, the vessel closes distally to the origin of the superior vesical artery and eventually becomes a solid fibrous cord. On the anterior abdominal wall, the cord raises a fold of peritoneum termed the medial umbilical fold. The fibrous remnant of the umbilical artery itself is the medial umbilical ligament.

    Superior Vesical Artery

    The superior vesical artery normally originates from the root of the umbilical artery and courses medially and inferiorly to supply the superior aspect of the bladder and distal parts of the ureter. In men, it also may give rise to an artery that supplies the ductus deferens.

    Inferior Vesical Artery

    The inferior vesical artery occurs in men and supplies branches to the bladder, ureter, seminal vesicle, and prostate. The vaginal artery in women is the equivalent of the inferior vesical artery in men and, descending to the vagina, supplies branches to the vagina and to adjacent parts of the bladder and rectum.

    Middle Rectal Artery

    The middle rectal artery courses medially to supply the rectum. The vessel anastomoses with the superior rectal artery, which originates from the inferior mesenteric artery in the abdomen, and the inferior rectal artery, which originates from the internal pudendal artery in the perineum.

    Obturator Artery

    The obturator artery courses anteriorly along the pelvic wall and leaves the pelvic cavity via the obturator canal. Together with the obturator nerve, above, and obturator vein, below, it enters and supplies the adductor region of the thigh.

    Internal Pudendal Artery

    The internal pudendal artery courses inferiorly from its origin in the anterior trunk and leaves the pelvic cavity through the greater sciatic foramen inferior to the piriformis muscle. In association with the pudendal nerve on its medial side, the vessel passes laterally to the ischial spine and then through the lesser sciatic foramen to enter the perineum. The internal pudendal artery is the main artery of the perineum. Among the structures it supplies are the erectile tissues of the clitoris and the penis.

    Inferior Gluteal Artery

    The inferior gluteal artery is a large terminal branch of the anterior trunk of the internal iliac artery. It passes between the anterior rami S1 and S2 or S2 and S3 of the sacral plexus and leaves the pelvic cavity through the greater sciatic foramen inferior to the piriformis muscle. It enters and contributes to the blood supply of the gluteal region and anastomoses with a network of vessels around the hip joint.

    Uterine Artery

    The uterine artery in women courses medially and anteriorly in the base of the broad ligament to reach the cervix (Figs. 5.64B and 5.65). Along its course, the vessel crosses the ureter and passes superiorly to the lateral vaginal fornix. Once the vessel reaches the cervix, it ascends along the lateral margin of the uterus to reach the uterine tube, where it curves laterally and anastomoses with the ovarian artery. The uterine artery is the major blood supply to the uterus and enlarges significantly during pregnancy. Through anastomoses with other arteries, the vessel contributes to the blood supply of the ovary and vagina as well.

    Ovarian Arteries

    In women, the gonadal (ovarian) vessels originate from the abdominal aorta and then descend to cross the pelvic inlet and supply the ovaries. They anastomose with terminal parts of the uterine arteries (Fig. 5.65). On each side, the vessels travel in the suspensory ligament of the ovary (the infundibulopelvic ligament) as they cross the pelvic inlet to the ovary. Branches pass through the mesovarium to reach the ovary and through the mesometrium of the broad ligament to anastomose with the uterine artery. The ovarian arteries enlarge significantly during pregnancy to augment the uterine blood supply.

    Median Sacral Artery

    The median sacral artery (Figs. 5.64A and 5.65) originates from the posterior surface of the aorta just superior to the aortic bifurcation at vertebral level LIV in the abdomen. It descends in the midline, crosses the pelvic inlet, and then courses along the anterior surface of the sacrum and coccyx. It gives rise to the last pair of lumbar arteries and to branches that anastomose with the iliolumbar and lateral sacral arteries.

    Pelvic Veins

    Pelvic veins follow the course of all branches of the internal iliac artery except for the umbilical artery and the iliolumbar artery (Fig. 5.66A). On each side, the veins drain into internal iliac veins, which leave the pelvic cavity to join common iliac veins situated just superior and lateral to the pelvic inlet.

    Within the pelvic cavity, extensive interconnected venous plexuses are associated with the surfaces of the viscera (bladder, rectum, prostate, uterus, and vagina). Together, these plexuses form the pelvic plexus of veins.

    Rectal Venous Plexus

    The part of the venous plexus surrounding the rectum and anal canal drains via superior rectal veins (tributaries of inferior mesenteric veins) into the hepatic portal system, and via middle and inferior rectal veins into the caval system. This pelvic plexus is an important portacaval shunt when the hepatic portal system is blocked (Fig. 5.66B).

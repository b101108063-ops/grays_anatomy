# Lower Limb — Regional Anatomy (Thigh & Knee)

## 600 — Arteries of the Thigh

Three arteries enter the thigh: the **femoral artery**, the **obturator artery**, and the **inferior gluteal artery**. Of these, the femoral artery is the largest and supplies most of the lower limb. The three arteries contribute to an **anastomotic network of vessels** around the hip joint.

---

### Femoral Artery

The femoral artery is the continuation of the **external iliac artery** and begins as the external iliac artery passes under the **inguinal ligament** to enter the **femoral triangle** on the anterior aspect of the upper thigh (Fig. 6.63). The femoral artery is palpable in the femoral triangle just inferior to the inguinal ligament midway between the **anterior superior iliac spine** and the **pubic symphysis**.

The femoral artery passes vertically through the femoral triangle and then continues down the thigh in the **adductor canal**. It leaves the canal by passing through the **adductor hiatus** in the **adductor magnus muscle** and becomes the **popliteal artery** behind the knee.

A cluster of four small branches—**superficial epigastric artery**, **superficial circumflex iliac artery**, **superficial external pudendal artery**, and **deep external pudendal artery**—originate from the femoral artery in the femoral triangle and supply cutaneous regions of the upper thigh, lower abdomen, and perineum.

> **臨床重點**：Femoral artery 在股三角中，於 inguinal ligament 下方、anterior superior iliac spine 與 pubic symphysis 中點處可觸及脈搏。此位置是臨床上重要的注射及導管插入部位。

---

### Deep Artery of Thigh (Profunda Femoris Artery)

The largest branch of the femoral artery in the thigh is the **deep artery of the thigh** (profunda femoris artery), which originates from the lateral side of the femoral artery in the femoral triangle and is the major source of blood supply to the thigh (Fig. 6.63). The deep artery of the thigh immediately passes:

- **Posteriorly** between the **pectineus** and **adductor longus** muscles and then between the **adductor longus** and **adductor brevis** muscles, and
- Then travels inferiorly between the **adductor longus** and **adductor magnus**, eventually penetrating through the **adductor magnus** to connect with branches of the **popliteal artery** behind the knee.

The deep artery of the thigh has **lateral circumflex femoral** and **medial circumflex femoral** branches and **three perforating branches**.

---

### Lateral Circumflex Femoral Artery

The lateral circumflex femoral artery normally originates proximally from the lateral side of the deep artery of the thigh, but may arise directly from the femoral artery (Fig. 6.64). It passes deep to the **sartorius** and **rectus femoris** and divides into three terminal branches:

- **Ascending branch**: Ascends laterally deep to the **tensor fasciae latae** muscle and connects with a branch of the medial circumflex femoral artery to form a channel, which circles the neck of the femur and supplies the neck and head of the femur.
- **Descending branch**: Descends deep to the **rectus femoris**, penetrates the **vastus lateralis muscle**, and connects with a branch of the popliteal artery near the knee.
- **Transverse branch**: Passes laterally to pierce the **vastus lateralis** and then circles around the proximal shaft of the femur to anastomose with branches from the **medial femoral circumflex artery**, the **inferior gluteal artery**, and the **first perforating artery** to form the **cruciate anastomosis** around the hip.

> **臨床重點**：Lateral circumflex femoral artery 的 transverse branch 參與形成髖關節周圍的 cruciate anastomosis，提供股骨頭及股骨頸的重要血液循環。

---

### Medial Circumflex Femoral Artery

The medial circumflex femoral artery normally originates proximally from the posteromedial aspect of the deep artery of the thigh, but may originate from the femoral artery (Fig. 6.64). It passes medially around the shaft of the femur, first between the **pectineus** and **iliopsoas** and then between the **obturator externus** and **adductor brevis** muscles. Near the margin of the adductor brevis the vessel gives off a small branch, which enters the hip joint through the **acetabular notch** and anastomoses with the acetabular branch of the obturator artery.

The main trunk of the medial circumflex femoral artery passes over the superior margin of the **adductor magnus** and divides into two major branches deep to the **quadratus femoris muscle**:

- **Ascending branch**: Ascends to the **trochanteric fossa** and connects with branches of the gluteal and lateral circumflex femoral arteries.
- **Descending branch**: Passes laterally to participate with branches from the lateral circumflex femoral artery, the inferior gluteal artery, and the first perforating artery in forming an anastomotic network of vessels around the hip.

---

### Perforating Arteries

The **three perforating arteries** branch from the deep artery of the thigh (Fig. 6.64) as it descends anterior to the **adductor brevis muscle**—the first originates above the muscle, the second originates anterior to the muscle, and the third originates below the muscle. All three penetrate through the **adductor magnus** near its attachment to the **linea aspera** to enter and supply the **posterior compartment of the thigh**. Here, the vessels have ascending and descending branches, which interconnect to form a longitudinal channel, which participates above in forming an anastomotic network of vessels around the hip and inferiorly anastomoses with branches of the popliteal artery behind the knee.

---

### Obturator Artery

The **obturator artery** originates as a branch of the **internal iliac artery** in the pelvic cavity and enters the medial compartment of the thigh through the **obturator canal** (Fig. 6.65). As it passes through the canal, it bifurcates into an **anterior branch** and a **posterior branch**, which together form a channel that circles the margin of the obturator membrane and lies within the attachment of the **obturator externus muscle**.

Vessels arising from the anterior and posterior branches supply adjacent muscles and anastomose with the **inferior gluteal** and **medial circumflex femoral arteries**. In addition, an acetabular vessel originates from the posterior branch, enters the hip joint through the **acetabular notch**, and contributes to the supply of the head of the femur.

---

## Veins of the Thigh

Veins in the thigh consist of **superficial** and **deep veins**. Deep veins generally follow the arteries and have similar names. Superficial veins are in the superficial fascia, interconnect with deep veins, and do not generally accompany arteries. The largest of the superficial veins in the thigh is the **great saphenous vein**.

### Great Saphenous Vein

The **great saphenous vein** originates from a venous arch on the dorsal aspect of the foot and ascends along the medial side of the lower limb to the proximal thigh. Here it passes through the **saphenous ring** in deep fascia covering the anterior thigh to connect with the **femoral vein** in the femoral triangle.

> **臨床重點**：Great saphenous vein 是靜脈曲張最常見的發生部位。在冠狀动脉繞道手術中，此靜脈也常被取用做為移植血管。

---

## Nerves of the Thigh

There are three major nerves in the thigh, each associated with one of the three compartments:

- **Femoral nerve** — associated with the anterior compartment
- **Obturator nerve** — associated with the medial compartment
- **Sciatic nerve** — associated with the posterior compartment

---

### Femoral Nerve

The **femoral nerve** originates from the **lumbar plexus** (spinal cord segments L2-L4) on the posterior abdominal wall and enters the femoral triangle of the thigh by passing under the **inguinal ligament** (Fig. 6.66). In the femoral triangle the femoral nerve lies on the lateral side of the **femoral artery** and is outside the **femoral sheath**, which surrounds the vessels.

Before entering the thigh, the femoral nerve supplies branches to the **iliacus** and **pectineus** muscles.

Immediately after passing under the inguinal ligament, the femoral nerve divides into **anterior** and **posterior branches**, which supply muscles of the anterior compartment of the thigh and skin on the anterior and medial aspects of the thigh and on the medial sides of the leg and foot.

**Branches of the femoral nerve** (Fig. 6.66) include:

- **Anterior cutaneous branches**: Penetrate deep fascia to supply skin on the front of the thigh and knee
- **Motor nerves**: Supply the **quadriceps femoris muscles** (rectus femoris, vastus lateralis, vastus intermedius, and vastus medialis muscles) and the **sartorius muscle**
- **Saphenous nerve**: One long cutaneous nerve, which supplies skin as far distally as the medial side of the foot

The saphenous nerve accompanies the femoral artery through the **adductor canal**, but does not pass through the adductor hiatus with the femoral artery. Rather, the saphenous nerve penetrates directly through connective tissues near the end of the canal to appear between the **sartorius** and **gracilis** muscles on the medial side of the knee. Here the saphenous nerve penetrates deep fascia and continues down the medial side of the leg to the foot, and supplies skin on the medial side of the knee, leg, and foot.

> **臨床重點**：Femoral nerve 受損會導致股四頭肌無力，造成膝蓋伸展困難，以及大腿前側及小腿內側感覺減退。

---

### Obturator Nerve

The **obturator nerve** is a branch of the **lumbar plexus** (spinal cord segments L2-L4) on the posterior abdominal wall. It descends in the **psoas muscle**, and then passes out of the medial margin of the psoas muscle to enter the pelvis (Fig. 6.67). The obturator nerve continues along the lateral pelvic wall and then enters the medial compartment of the thigh by passing through the **obturator canal**. It supplies most of the adductor muscles and skin on the medial aspect of the thigh.

As the obturator nerve enters the thigh, it divides into two branches, an **anterior branch** and a **posterior branch**, which are separated by the **adductor brevis muscle**:

- **Posterior branch**: Descends behind the **adductor brevis muscle** and on the anterior surface of the **adductor magnus muscle**, and supplies the **obturator externus** and **adductor brevis** muscles and the part of the **adductor magnus** that attaches to the linea aspera.
- **Anterior branch**: Descends on the anterior surface of the **adductor brevis muscle** and is behind the **pectineus** and **adductor longus muscles**—it supplies branches to the **adductor longus**, **gracilis**, and **adductor brevis** muscles, and often contributes to the supply of the **pectineus muscle**, and cutaneous branches innervate the skin on the medial side of the thigh.

> **臨床重點**：Obturator nerve 受損會導致內收肌群無力，造成大腿內收困難，並伴有大腿內側感覺異常。

---

### Sciatic Nerve

The **sciatic nerve** is a branch of the **lumbosacral plexus** (spinal cord segments L4-S3) and descends into the posterior compartment of the thigh from the gluteal region (Fig. 6.68). It innervates all muscles in the posterior compartment of the thigh and then its branches continue into the leg and foot.

In the posterior compartment of the thigh, the sciatic nerve lies on the **adductor magnus muscle** and is crossed by the **long head of the biceps femoris muscle**.

Proximal to the knee, and sometimes within the pelvis, the sciatic nerve divides into its two terminal branches: the **tibial nerve** and the **common fibular nerve**. These nerves travel vertically down the thigh and enter the **popliteal fossa** posterior to the knee. Here, they meet the popliteal artery and vein.

---

#### Tibial Nerve

The **tibial part** of the sciatic nerve, either before or after its separation from the common fibular nerve, supplies branches to all muscles in the posterior compartment of the thigh (**long head of biceps femoris**, **semimembranosus**, **semitendinosus**) except the short head of the biceps femoris, which is innervated by the common fibular part (Fig. 6.68).

The tibial nerve descends through the popliteal fossa, enters the posterior compartment of the leg, and continues into the sole of the foot.

The tibial nerve innervates:
- All muscles in the posterior compartment of the leg
- All intrinsic muscles in the sole of the foot including the first two **dorsal interossei muscles**
- Skin on the posterolateral side of the lower half of the leg and lateral side of the ankle, foot, and little toe, and skin on the sole of the foot and toes

---

#### Common Fibular Nerve

The **common fibular part** of the sciatic nerve innervates the **short head of the biceps femoris** in the posterior compartment of the thigh and then continues into the lateral and anterior compartments of the leg and onto the foot (Fig. 6.68).

The common fibular nerve innervates:
- All muscles in the anterior and lateral compartments of the leg
- One muscle (**extensor digitorum brevis**) on the dorsal aspect of the foot
- The first two **dorsal interossei muscles** in the sole of the foot
- Skin over the lateral aspect of the leg, ankle, and over the dorsal aspect of the foot and toes

> **臨床重點**：Sciatic nerve 損傷會導致膝蓋以下大部分肌肉癱瘓，表現為「垂足」(foot drop) 及大腿後側、小腿及足部感覺喪失。

---

## Knee Joint

The **knee joint** is the largest synovial joint in the body. It consists of:

- The articulation between the **femur** and **tibia**, which is weight-bearing, and
- The articulation between the **patella** and **femur**, which allows the pull of the **quadriceps femoris muscle** to be directed anteriorly over the knee to the tibia without tendon wear (Fig. 6.69).

Two fibrocartilaginous **menisci**, one on each side, between the femoral condyles and tibia accommodate changes in the shape of the articular surfaces during joint movements.

The detailed movements of the knee joint are complex, but basically the joint is a **hinge joint** that allows mainly flexion and extension. Like all hinge joints, the knee joint is reinforced by **collateral ligaments**, one on each side of the joint. In addition, two very strong ligaments (the **cruciate ligaments**) interconnect the adjacent ends of the femur and tibia and maintain their opposed positions during movement.

Because the knee joint is involved in weight-bearing, it has an efficient "locking" mechanism to reduce the amount of muscle energy required to keep the joint extended when standing.

---

### Articular Surfaces

The articular surfaces of the bones that contribute to the knee joint are covered by **hyaline cartilage**. The major surfaces involved include:

- The two **femoral condyles**, and
- The adjacent surfaces of the superior aspect of the **tibial condyles**

The surfaces of the femoral condyles that articulate with the tibia in flexion of the knee are curved or round, whereas the surfaces that articulate in full extension are flat (Fig. 6.70).

The articular surfaces between the femur and patella are the V-shaped trench on the anterior surface of the distal end of the femur where the two condyles join and the adjacent surfaces on the posterior aspect of the patella.

The joint surfaces are all enclosed within a single articular cavity, as are the intraarticular menisci between the femoral and tibial condyles.

---

### Menisci

There are two menisci, which are fibrocartilaginous C-shaped cartilages, in the knee joint, one **medial (medial meniscus)** and the other **lateral (lateral meniscus)** (Fig. 6.71). Both are attached at each end to facets in the intercondylar region of the **tibial plateau**.

The **medial meniscus** is attached around its margin to the capsule of the joint and to the **tibial collateral ligament**, whereas the **lateral meniscus** is unattached to the capsule. Therefore, the lateral meniscus is more mobile than the medial meniscus.

The menisci are interconnected anteriorly by a **transverse ligament of the knee**. The lateral meniscus is also connected to the tendon of the **popliteus muscle**, which passes superolaterally between this meniscus and the capsule to insert on the femur.

The menisci improve congruency between the femoral and tibial condyles during joint movements where the surfaces of the femoral condyles articulating with the tibial plateau change from small curved surfaces in flexion to large flat surfaces in extension.

> **臨床重點**：Menisci 損傷是常見的運動傷害。Medial meniscus 由於與關節囊及 tibial collateral ligament 相連，活動度較低，比 lateral meniscus 更易受傷。MRI 是診斷 meniscus 撕裂的最佳影像學工具。

## 603 — Clinical Correlation: Peripheral Vascular Disease

> **臨床重點**：**Peripheral vascular disease (周邊血管疾病)**
>
> Peripheral vascular disease is often characterized by **reduced blood flow to the legs**. This disorder may be caused by stenoses (narrowing) and/or occlusions (blockages) in the lower aorta and the iliac, femoral, tibial, and fibular vessels. Patients typically have chronic leg ischemia and "acute on chronic" leg ischemia.

### Chronic Leg Ischemia

Chronic leg ischemia is a disorder in which vessels have undergone **atheromatous change**, and often there is significant luminal narrowing (usually over 50%). Most patients with peripheral arterial disease have widespread arterial disease (including cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease), which may be clinically asymptomatic. Some of these patients develop such severe ischemia that the viability of the limb is threatened (**critical limb ischemia**).

The commonest symptom of chronic leg ischemia is **intermittent claudication**. Patients typically have a history of pain that develops in the calf muscles (usually associated with occlusions or narrowing in the femoral artery) or the buttocks (usually associated with occlusion or narrowing in the aorto-iliac segments). The pain experienced in these muscles is often cramplike and occurs with walking. The patient rests and is able to continue walking up to the same distance until the pain recurs and stops walking as before.

### Acute on Chronic Ischemia

In some patients with chronic limb ischemia, an acute event blocks the vessels or reduces the blood supply to such a degree that the viability of the limb is threatened. Occasionally a leg may become acutely ischemic with no evidence of underlying atheromatous disease. In these instances a blood clot is likely to have embolized from the heart. Patients with **mitral valve disease** and **atrial fibrillation** are prone to embolic disease.

### Critical Limb Ischemia

**Critical limb ischemia** occurs when the blood supply to the limb is so poor that the viability of the limb is severely threatened, and in this case many patients develop **gangrene**, **ulceration**, and severe **rest pain** in the foot. These patients require urgent treatment, which may be in the form of surgical reconstruction, radiological angioplasty, or even amputation.

## Knee Joint（膝關節）

---

### Synovial Membrane（關節滑膜）

**Knee joint（膝關節）** 的 Synovial membrane（滑膜）附著於關節面的邊緣，以及**Menisci（半月板）**的上、下外緣（圖 6.72A）。兩條 **Cruciate ligaments（十字韌帶）** 附著於脛骨的髁間窩下方與股骨的髁間窩上方，雖然位於關節腔之外，卻被膝關節的纖維膜包覆。

在關節後方，滑膜沿著 **Posterior cruciate ligament（後十字韌帶）** 兩側從關節囊的纖維膜反折，並向前包繞兩條韌帶，將它們排除於關節腔之外。

在關節前方，滑膜與 **Patellar ligament（臏韌帶）** 之間隔著一個**Infrapatellar fat pad（髕下脂肪墊）**。脂肪墊的兩側，滑膜形成具有毛邊的邊緣（**Alar fold（翼狀皺襞）**），突入關節腔。此外，覆盖髕下脂肪墊下部的滑膜向上突出形成一個尖銳的中線皺襞 directed posteriorly（**Infrapatellar synovial fold（髕下滑膜皺襞）**），附著於股骨髁間窩的邊緣。

**Knee joint（膝關節）** 的滑膜在兩處形成滑膜囊，以提供低摩擦面供關節相關肌腱活動：

- 最小的一個是 **Subpopliteal recess（膝下隱窩）**（圖 6.72A），從關節腔向後外側延伸，介於 **Lateral meniscus（外側半月板）** 與穿过關節囊的 **Popliteus muscle（腘肌）** 肌腱之間。
- 第二個是 **Suprapatellar bursa（髕上囊）**（圖 6.72B），是一個大滑膜囊，是關節腔向上延伸至股骨骨幹遠端與 **Quadriceps femoris muscle（股四頭肌）** 及肌腱之間的部分——此囊的頂端附著於小的 **Articularis genus muscle（膝關節肌）**，後者在膝關節伸直時將滑膜囊拉離關節。

其他與膝關節相關但不與關節腔相通的滑膜囊包括**Subcutaneous prepatellar bursa（皮下髕前囊）**、**Deep infrapatellar bursa（深層髕下囊）**、**Subcutaneous infrapatellar bursa（皮下髕下囊）**，以及關節周圍眾多與肌腱和韌帶相關的滑膜囊（圖 6.72B）。

**Prepatellar bursa（髕前囊）** 為皮下結構，位於髕骨前方。**Deep infrapatellar bursa（深層髕下囊）** 和 **Subcutaneous infrapatellar bursa（皮下髕下囊）** 分別位於臏韌帶的深層和皮下側。

> **臨床重點**：
> 髕前囊和髕下囊的發炎（bursitis）常見於需要長時間跪地的職業（如地板安裝工人），臨床上稱為「 housemaid's knee 」或「 clergyman's knee 」。這些囊的感染可能導致蜂窩性組織炎，需與關節感染作鑑別診斷。

---

### Fibrous Membrane（纖維膜）

**Knee joint（膝關節）** 的纖維膜非常廣泛，部分由周圍肌肉肌腱的延伸所形成和加固（圖 6.73）。一般而言，纖維膜包繞關節腔和髁間區域：

- 在膝關節的內側，纖維膜與 **Tibial collateral ligament（脛側副韌帶）** 融合，並在其內側面附著於 **Medial meniscus（內側半月板）**。
- 在外側，纖維膜的外表面與 **Fibular collateral ligament（腓側副韌帶）** 之間隔著空隙，纖維膜的內表面不附著於 **Lateral meniscus（外側半月板）**。
- 在前方，纖維膜附著於髕骨邊緣，並由 **Vastus lateralis（股外側肌）** 和 **Vastus medialis（股內側肌）** 肌腱的延伸所加固，這些結構向上與股四頭肌腱融合，向下與臏韌帶融合。

纖維膜在前外側由 **Iliotibial tract（髂脛束）** 的纖維延伸加固，在後內側由 **Semimembranosus muscle（半膜肌）** 肌腱的延伸（**Oblique popliteal ligament（腘斜韌帶）**）加固——此韌帶從內側到外側向上斜行反折於纖維膜的後方。

**Popliteus muscle（腘肌）** 的上端穿過纖維膜後外側的一個孔洞，當其肌腱繞過關節附著於股骨外側髁的外側面時，被纖維膜包繞。

---

### Ligaments（韌帶）

**Knee joint（膝關節）** 相關的主要韌帶包括 **Patellar ligament（臏韌帶）**、**Tibial (medial) collateral ligament（脛側/內側副韌帶）**、**Fibular (lateral) collateral ligament（腓側/外側副韌帶）**，以及 **Anterior cruciate ligament（前十字韌帶）** 和 **Posterior cruciate ligament（後十字韌帶）**。

#### Patellar Ligament（臏韌帶）

**Patellar ligament（臏韌帶）** 本質上是髕骨下方股四頭肌腱的延續（圖 6.73）。其上方附著於髕骨的邊緣和尖端，下方附著於**脛骨粗隆（Tibial tuberosity）**。

#### Collateral Ligaments（側副韌帶）

兩側各有一條 **Collateral ligaments（側副韌帶）**，穩定膝關節的鉸鏈樣運動（圖 6.74）。

> **臨床重點**：
> **Tibial collateral ligament（內側副韌帶）** 的損傷常因膝關節外翻暴力（valgus stress）引起，常見於足球或滑雪運動中。單獨的內側副韌帶損傷通常預後較好，但若合併前十字韌帶和內側半月板撕裂，則構成所謂的「 unhappy triad 」，恢復時間顯著延長。

**Fibular collateral ligament（腓側副韌帶）** 呈索狀，上方附著於股骨外上髁，正好在 **Popliteus tendon（腘肌腱）** 溝的上方。下方附著於腓骨頭外側面的一个凹陷。它與纖維膜之間隔著一個滑膜囊。

**Tibial collateral ligament（脛側副韌帶）** 寬而扁平，其大部分深面附著於下方的纖維膜。它向上附著於股骨內上髁，正好在 **Adductor tubercle（內收肌結節）** 的下方，向前下延伸附著於脛骨內側面的上部，位於 **Sartorius（縫匠肌）**、**Gracilis（股薄肌）** 和 **Semitendinosus（半腱肌）** 肌腱附著處的後上方。

#### Cruciate Ligaments（十字韌帶）

兩條 **Cruciate ligaments（十字韌帶）** 位於膝關節的髁間區域，連接股骨與脛骨（圖 6.74D、6.75）。它們被稱為「十字韌帶」（cruciate 源自拉丁語，意為「交叉形」），因為它們在經過股骨和脛骨附著點之間的矢狀面上相互交叉：

- **Anterior cruciate ligament（前十字韌帶）**附著於脛骨髁間區前部的一個關節面，向上後行附著於股骨髁間窩外側壁後部的一個關節面。
- **Posterior cruciate ligament（後十字韌帶）**附著於脛骨髁間區的後部，向上前行附著於股骨髁間窩的內側壁。

前十字韌帶在穿過髁間區時位於後十字韌帶的外側。

> **臨床重點**：
> **Anterior cruciate ligament（前十字韌帶）** 損傷是最常見的膝關節運動傷害之一，尤其好發於需要突然改變方向或跳躍落地時（如籃球、足球、滑雪）。前十字韌帶撕裂後，患者常描述聽到「 pop 」聲並感到關節不穩定。手術重建（前十字韌帶重建術）是许多活跃患者恢復運動功能的選項。

**Anterior cruciate ligament（前十字韌帶）** 防止脛骨相對於股骨的前移，而 **Posterior cruciate ligament（後十字韌帶）** 限制脛骨的後移（圖 6.75）。

---

### Locking Mechanism（鎖定機制）

站立時，**Knee joint（膝關節）** 處於鎖定位置，從而減少維持站立姿勢所需的肌肉工作量（圖 6.76）。

鎖定機制的一個組成部分是股骨關節面（與脛骨相關節的）在形狀和大小的變化：

- 在屈曲時，相關節的表面是股骨髁後側的彎曲、圓形區域。
- 當膝關節伸直時，相關節的表面移至股骨髁下側的寬而平的區域。

因此，在伸直時關節面變得更大、更穩定。

鎖定機制的另一個組成部分是伸直時股骨在脛骨上的**內旋（medial rotation）**。內旋和完全伸直使所有相關韌帶拉緊。

維持膝關節伸直站立姿勢的另一個因素是，身體重心沿著一條穿過膝關節前方的垂直線定位。

**Popliteus muscle（腘肌）** 通过啟動股骨在脛骨上的外旋來解鎖膝關節。

---

### Vascular Supply and Innervation（血管供應與神經支配）

**Knee joint（膝關節）** 的血管供應主要來自大腿的 **Femoral artery（股動脈）**、**Popliteal artery（膕動脈）** 和 **Lateral circumflex femoral artery（旋股外側動脈）** 的下行支和關節支，以及小腿的 **Circflex fibular artery（旋腓动脉）** 和 **Anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）** 的返支。這些血管在關節周圍形成**吻合血管網（anastomotic network）**（圖 6.77）。

**Knee joint（膝關節）** 由 **Obturator nerve（閉孔神經）**、**Femoral nerve（股神經）**、**Tibial nerve（脛神經）** 和 **Common fibular nerve（腓總神經）** 的分支神經支配。

---

### In the Clinic — Soft Tissue Injuries to the Knee（臨床探討：膝關節軟組織損傷）

**Soft tissue injuries（軟組織損傷）** 在膝關節及周圍很常見。

典型的損傷包括 **Anterior cruciate ligament（前十字韌帶）** 和 **Posterior cruciate ligament（後十字韌帶）** 撕裂、**Meniscal tears（半月板撕裂）**，以及 **Collateral ligaments（側副韌帶）** 的創傷。單獨的軟組織損傷可能發生，但某些類型的損傷同時發生並不罕見，例如前十字韌帶撕裂、脛側副韌帶撕裂和內側半月板撕裂。

膝關節及周圍的任何軟組織損傷都可能涉及**神經血管束**，在軟組織損傷患者的管理中，評估神經血管結構至關重要。

> **臨床重點**：
> 膝關節損傷的評估必須包括詳細的神經血管檢查。**Popliteal artery（膕動脈）** 在膝關節後方走行，受損時可能導致危及肢體的缺血。**Common fibular nerve（腓總神經）** 繞過腓骨頭，該處的骨折或石膏压迫可導致踝關節背屈和足趾伸展的完全喪失。

---

### In the Clinic — Degenerative Joint Disease/Osteoarthritis（臨床探討：退化性關節疾病/骨關節炎）

**Degenerative joint disease（退化性關節疾病）** 發生於身體的許多關節。關節退化可能源於正常軟骨上的異常應力，或異常軟骨上的正常應力。

退化性關節疾病通常發生在**滑膜關節（synovial joints）**，此過程稱為 **Osteoarthritis（骨關節炎）**。發生骨關節炎的關節，軟骨和骨組織通常受累，而滑膜的變化有限。典型發現包括關節間隙變窄、**Eburnation（關節硬化）**、**Osteophytosis（骨刺形成）** 和**囊腫形成（Bony cyst formation）**。隨著疾病進展，關節可能出現對線不良，活動嚴重受限，並可能伴隨顯著疼痛。

骨關節炎最常見的部位包括手和腕的小關節，在下肢，**Hip（髖關節）** 和 **Knee（膝關節）** 通常受累，儘管跗跖關節和跖趾關節也可能發生類似變化。

退化性關節疾病的病因尚不明確，但有一些相關因素，包括**遺傳傾向**、**年齡增長**（男性往往比女性更早受累）、**關節過度使用或使用不足**，以及**營養和代謝異常**。其他因素包括關節創傷和既有關節疾病或畸形。

骨關節炎的組織學發現包括軟骨和軟骨下骨的退化性變化進一步的關節損傷加劇這些變化，促進對關節的進一步異常應力。隨著疾病進展，典型表現為疼痛，通常在起床時和一天活動結束時更嚴重。通常在活動範圍的極限或不習慣的用力時加重。可能隨之出現僵硬和活動受限。

> **臨床重點**：
> 骨關節炎的治療首先包括改變生活方式以預防疼痛和簡單的止痛治療。隨著症狀進展，可能需要關節置換術，但儘管關節置換術似乎是退化性關節疾病的萬靈丹，它並非沒有風險和併發症，包括短期和長期的感染和失敗。

---

### In the Clinic — Examination of the Knee Joint（臨床探討：膝關節檢查）

在進行任何檢查之前，了解患者的主訴性質非常重要。病史應包括症狀、相關體徵和生活方式（活動水平）的信息。

檢查應包括站立評估、行走時評估和躺床檢查。必須將受影響側與未受影響側進行比較。

膝關節檢查有許多測試和技術，包括以下內容：

#### Tests for Anterior Instability（前側不穩定性測試）

- **Lachman's test（Lachman 測試）**：患者仰臥於檢查床上。檢查者一隻手環繞遠端股骨，另一隻手環繞近端脛骨，然後抬起膝關節，使其屈曲 20°。患者的腳跟放在檢查床上。檢查者的拇指必須位於脛骨粗隆。握住脛骨的手施加一個快速向前的作用力。如果脛骨在股骨上的運動突然停止，則為堅實的終點（firm endpoint）。如果沒有突然停止，則終點被描述為柔軟的（soft），與前十字韌帶撕裂相關。

- **Anterior drawer test（前側抽屜測試）**：陽性的前側抽屜測試是指患者的近端脛骨可以在股骨上向前拉動。患者仰臥於檢查床上。膝關節屈曲至 90°，患者的腳跟和足底放在檢查床上。檢查者輕輕坐在患者的腳上，腳已放置於中立位置。檢查者用食指檢查膕繩肌是否放鬆，同時其他手指環繞脛骨上端並向前拉。如果脛骨向前移動，則前十字韌帶撕裂。其他周邊結構，如內側半月板或半月板脛骨韌帶，也必須受損才能引出此徵象。

- **Pivot shift test（軸移測試）**：此測試有多種變化。患者的腳夾在檢查者身體和肘部之間。檢查者一隻手平放在脛骨下方，在膝關節伸直時向前推。下肢被檢查者的肘部輕微外展，檢查者的身體作為支點產生外翻。檢查者保持脛骨的前向平移和外翻，並開始屈曲患者的膝關節。在約 20°-30° 時，隨著外側脛骨平台復位，將發生軸移。此測試顯示膝關節後外側角和前十字韌帶的損傷。

#### Tests for Posterior Instability（後側不穩定性測試）

- **Posterior drawer test（後側抽屜測試）**：陽性的後側抽屜測試是指患者的近端脛骨可以在股骨上向後推動。患者仰臥，膝關節屈曲至約 90°，腳位於中立位置。檢查者輕輕坐在患者的腳上，雙拇指放在脛骨粗隆上，向後推脛骨。如果脛骨平台移動，則後十字韌帶撕裂。

#### Assessment of Other Structures of the Knee（膝關節其他結構的評估）

- 通過在膝關節上施加外翻應力，可以評估 **Tibial collateral ligament（脛側副韌帶）**。
- 評估外側和後外側膝關節結構需要更複雜的臨床測試。

膝關節也將被評估：

- 關節線壓痛
- 髕股關節的活動性和不穩定性
- 關節積液的存在
- 肌肉損傷
- 膕窩腫塊

#### Further Investigations（進一步檢查）

完成臨床檢查後，進一步檢查通常包括**X 光片**和可能的**磁共振成像（MRI）**，後者允許放射科醫生評估半月板、十字韌帶、側副韌帶、骨和軟骨表面以及軟組織。

可進行**關節鏡檢查（Arthroscopy）**，修復或修剪任何內部結構的損傷。**關節鏡（Arthroscope）** 是一種小型相機，通過膝關節的前外側或前內側進入關節。關節內充滿生理鹽水，然後操縱關節鏡環繞膝關節以評估十字韌帶、半月板和軟骨表面。

### In the Clinic — Anterolateral Ligament of the Knee（臨床探討：膝關節的前外側韌帶）

> **臨床重點**：
> 描述了一個與膝關節腓側副韌帶起源相關的韌帶。這個韌帶（膝關節的**Anterolateral ligament（ALL，前外側韌帶）**）從股骨外上髁走行至近端脛骨的前外側區域，可能控制脛骨的內旋。（J Anat 2013;223:321-328）

---

### Tibiofibular Joint（脛腓關節）

小的**Proximal tibiofibular joint（近端脛腓關節）** 是滑膜型關節，只允許非常小的活動（圖 6.78）。對應的關節面——脛骨外側髁的下側面和腓骨頭的上內側面——是平的、圓形的。關節囊由**前韌帶（anterior ligament）** 和**後韌帶（posterior ligament）** 加固。

---

### Popliteal Fossa（膕窩）

**Popliteal fossa（膕窩）** 是大腿和小腿之間的一個重要的過渡區域，是結構從一個區域通過到另一個區域的主要途徑。

**Popliteal fossa（膕窩）** 是膝關節後方的一個菱形間隙，由大腿和小腿後腔室的肌肉形成（圖 6.79A）：

- 菱形上部的邊界在內側由 **Semitendinosus muscle（半腱肌）** 和 **Semimembranosus muscle（半膜肌）** 的遠端形成，在外側由 **Biceps femoris muscle（股二頭肌）** 的遠端形成。

> **臨床重點**：
> **Popliteal fossa（膕窩）** 是臨床上非常重要的區域，包含重要的神經血管結構。**Popliteal artery（膕動脈）** 是股動脈的延續，在膝關節後方下行，進入小腿後分為 **Anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）** 和 **Posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）**。**Popliteal aneurysm（膕动脉瘤）** 是腿动脉瘤中最常見的類型，可表現為膕窩搏動性腫塊。**Baker's cyst（貝克氏囊腫）** 是源於膝關節的滑膜囊腫，常在膕窩中發現，可能類似於血栓性靜脈炎。

## Popliteal Fossa（臏窩）

### 邊界與結構

Popliteal fossa（臏窩）為膝關節後方的菱形空間，其邊界與組成如下：

- **上外側界（Upper lateral border）：** 由 **biceps femoris muscle（股二頭肌）** 形成。
- **上內側界（Upper medial border）：** 由 **semitendinosus muscle（半腱肌）** 與 **semimembranosus muscle（半膜肌）** 形成。
- **下外側界（Lower lateral border）：** 由 **plantaris muscle（蹠肌）** 與 **lateral head of gastrocnemius muscle（腓腸肌外側頭）** 形成。
- **下內側界（Lower medial border）：** 由 **medial head of gastrocnemius muscle（腓腸肌內側頭）** 形成。
- **底部（Floor）：** 由 **knee joint capsule（膝關節囊）**、**femur（股骨）** 與 **tibia（脛骨）** 的相鄰表面構成，更下方由 **popliteus muscle（腘肌）** 形成。
- **頂部（Roof）：** 由 **deep fascia（深筋膜）** 形成，上方與大腿的 **fascia lata（闊筋膜）** 延續，下方與小腿的深筋膜延續。

### 內容物

Popliteal fossa 的主要內容物為 **popliteal artery（臏動脈）**、**popliteal vein（臏靜脈）**、**tibial nerve（脛神經）** 與 **common fibular nerve（腓總神經）**。

#### Tibial Nerve 與 Common Fibular Nerve

**Tibial nerve** 與 **common fibular nerve** 起源於 popliteal fossa 近端，為 **sciatic nerve（坐骨神經）** 的兩大分支。它們是 popliteal fossa 中最淺層的神經血管結構，直接從上方沿 biceps femoris muscle 邊緣進入：

- **Tibial nerve：** 垂直下行穿過 popliteal fossa，從 plantaris muscle 邊緣深層離開，進入小腿的後筋膜室（posterior compartment of leg）。
- **Common fibular nerve：** 沿 biceps femoris tendon 從 popliteal fossa 下外側緣離開，繼續至小腿外側，繞過腓骨頸（neck of fibula）進入小腿的外側筋膜室（lateral compartment of leg）。

#### Popliteal Artery 與 Popliteal Vein

**Popliteal artery** 是大腿前筋膜室中 **femoral artery（股動脈）** 的延續，當 femoral artery 穿過 **adductor magnus muscle（內收大肌）** 的 **adductor hiatus（收肌裂孔）** 向後走行時開始。

Popliteal artery 出現在 popliteal fossa 的上內側緣，沿 semimembranosus muscle 邊緣。它斜向下與 tibial nerve 一同穿過 fossa，進入小腿後筋膜室，在小腿正中線外側分為 **anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）** 與 **posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）**。

Popliteal artery 是 popliteal fossa 中最深層的神經血管結構，因此難以觸診；但通常可在正中線深壓時感受到脈搏。

Popliteal artery 在 popliteal fossa 發出分支供應相鄰肌肉，並發出一系列 **geniculate arteries（膝關節動脈）**，參與膝關節周圍的血管吻合。

**Popliteal vein** 淺層於並與 popliteal artery 伴行，向上經 adductor hiatus 穿出成為 **femoral vein（股靜脈）**。

### Roof of Popliteal Fossa（臏窩頂部）

Popliteal fossa 的屋頂由淺筋膜與皮膚覆蓋。淺筋膜中最重要的結構是 **small saphenous vein（小隱靜脈）**。此靜脈從足背靜脈弓（dorsal venous arch）的外側緣垂直上行於小腿後側的淺筋膜中，上行至膝關節後方，穿透構成 popliteal fossa 屋頂的深筋膜，匯入 popliteal vein。

另一個穿過 fossa 屋頂的結構是 **posterior cutaneous nerve of the thigh（大腿後皮神經）**，它下行於大腿後側淺層，通過 popliteal fossa 屋頂後，與 small saphenous vein 繼續下行，支配小腿後側上半部的皮膚感覺。

## Leg（小腿）

小腿是膝關節與踝關節之間的下肢部分：

- **近端：** 大多數主要結構通過或位於膝關節後方的 popliteal fossa，在大腿與小腿之間通行。
- **遠端：** 結構在小腿與足之間主要通過踝關節後內側的 **tarsal tunnel（跗骨隧道）**，例外是 **anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）** 與 **deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）** 與 **superficial fibular nerve（腓淺神經）** 的末端，它們在踝關節前方進入足部。

小腿的骨骼框架由兩塊骨頭——**tibia（脛骨）** 與 **fibula（腓骨）** 平行排列而成。

**Fibula** 比 tibia 小得多，位於小腿外側。它在上方與 proximal tibia 的外側髁（lateral condyle）下方關節面形成關節，但不參與膝關節的構成。Fibula 的遠端通過纖維關節牢固地錨定於 tibia，形成踝關節的 **lateral malleolus（外踝）**。

**Tibia** 是小腿的承重骨，因此比 fibula 大得多。它在上方參與膝關節的構成，在下方形成 **medial malleolus（內踝）** 以及踝關節處小腿與足關節的大部分骨性關節面。

小腿被分為：
- **前筋膜室（Anterior compartment / extensor compartment）**
- **後筋膜室（Posterior compartment / flexor compartment）**
- **外側筋膜室（Lateral compartment / fibular compartment）**

由以下結構分隔：
- **Interosseous membrane（骨間膜）**：連接 tibia 與 fibula 相鄰的骨間緣；
- **兩塊 intermuscular septa（肌間隔）**：介於 fibula 與肢體周圍的深筋膜之間；
- **深筋膜直接附著於 tibia 前緣與內側緣的骨膜**。

前筋膜室肌肉負責踝關節背屈、足趾伸展與足內翻；後筋膜室肌肉負責踝關節蹠屈、足趾屈曲與足內翻；外側筋膜室肌肉負責足外翻。主要的神經與血管供應或穿過各筋膜室。

### Bones（骨骼）

#### Tibia（脛骨）

##### Shaft and Distal End of Tibia

Tibia 的骨幹橫切面呈三角形，有三個邊（anterior、interosseous、medial）與三個面（medial、lateral、posterior）：

- **Anterior 緣與內側緣** 以及整個內側面位於皮下，容易觸及。
- **Tibia 的 interosseous 緣** 通過骨間膜與 fibula 的 interosseous 緣沿其長度連接。
- **後側面** 有一條斜線（**soleal line（比目魚肌線）**）。

Soleal line 從外側斜向內側穿過骨頭，在內側緣融合。此外，上後表面從 soleal line 中點向下延伸一條垂直線，消失於脛骨下三分之一處。

脛骨骨幹在上下兩端擴大，以支撐膝關節與踝關節處的身體重量。

脛骨遠端呈矩形盒狀，內側有骨性突起（**medial malleolus（內踝）**）。盒狀物的上部與脛骨骨幹連續，下表面與 medial malleolus 與其中一塊跗骨（**talus（距骨）**）構成踝關節的大部分關節面。

矩形盒狀脛骨遠端的後表面有一條垂直溝，繼續向下內側延伸至 medial malleolus 的後表面。此溝用於 **tibialis posterior muscle（脛後肌）** 的肌腱。

脛骨遠端的外側面被深的三角形切跡（**fibular notch（腓骨切跡）**）佔據，腓骨遠端通過骨間膜增厚部錨定於此。

#### Fibula（腓骨）

##### Shaft and Distal End of Fibula

Fibula 不參與承重，因此其骨幹比脛骨骨幹窄得多。此外，除兩端外，腓骨被肌肉包繞。

與脛骨相似，腓骨骨幹橫切面呈三角形，有三個邊與三個面，用於肌肉、肌間隔與韌帶的附著。Fibula 的 interosseous 緣朝向並通過骨間膜與脛骨的 interosseous 緣連接。肌間隔附著於前緣與後緣。肌肉附著於三個面。

- **內側面：** 朝向前筋膜室
- **外側面：** 朝向外側筋膜室
- **後側面：** 朝向後筋膜室

後表面有一條垂直嵴（**medial crest（內側嵴）**），將後表面分為兩部分，各附著不同的深層屈肌。

腓骨遠端擴大形成鏟狀的 **lateral malleolus（外踝）**。

Lateral malleolus 的內側面有一個關節面，與距骨的外側面關節，形成踝關節的外側部分。此關節面上方有一個三角形區域，嵌入脛骨遠端的腓骨切跡。在此，脛骨與腓骨通過骨間膜遠端連接。關節面下方有一個窩或窩（**malleolar fossa（踝窩）**），用於附著踝關節的 **posterior talofibular ligament（後距腓韌帶）**。

Lateral malleolus 的後表面有淺溝，用於 **fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）** 與 **fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）** 肌腱。

---

### Joints（關節）

#### Interosseous Membrane of Leg（小腿骨間膜）

小腿的骨間膜是連接脛骨與腓骨骨幹相對骨間緣的堅韌纖維結締組織板。膠原纖維從脛骨 interosseous 緣斜向下至腓骨 interosseous 緣，除了上方有一條韌帶束從脛骨上行至腓骨。

骨間膜有兩個孔，一個在上方，另一個在下方，供血管通過小腿前、後筋膜室。

骨間膜不僅連接脛骨與腓骨，還為肌肉附著提供更大的表面積。

腓骨與脛骨遠端由骨間膜下方部分固定連接，跨越脛骨遠端外側面的腓骨切跡與腓骨遠端相應表面之間的狹窄間隙。骨間膜的這一下端擴展部分由 **anterior 與 posterior tibiofibular ligaments（脛腓前韌帶與脛腓後韌帶）** 加強。脛骨與腓骨遠端的這種牢固連接對形成踝關節與足關節的骨骼框架至關重要。

---

### Posterior Compartment of Leg（小腿後筋膜室）

#### Muscles（肌肉）

小腿後（屈肌）筋膜室的肌肉分為兩組——淺層與深層，由一層深筋膜分隔。一般來說，這些肌肉主要使足蹠屈、內翻並屈曲足趾。它們全部由 **tibial nerve（脛神經）** 神經支配。

##### Superficial Group（淺層群）

小腿後筋膜室的淺層肌肉群包括三塊肌肉——**gastrocnemius（腓腸肌）**、**plantaris（蹠肌）** 與 **soleus（比目魚肌）**，它們都附著於足的跟骨（calcaneus），並在踝關節處蹠屈足部（圖 6.83）。作為一個整體，這些肌肉大而有力，因為它們在行走時推動身體向前離開固定的足，並能在站立時將身體向上抬至腳趾。其中兩塊肌肉（gastrocnemius 與 plantaris）起源於股骨遠端，也能屈膝。

###### Gastrocnemius（腓腸肌）

**Gastrocnemius muscle（腓腸肌）** 是小腿後筋膜室中最淺層的肌肉，也是小腿最大的肌肉之一。它起源於兩個頭——外側頭與內側頭：

- **Medial head（內側頭）：** 附著於股骨遠端後面、內收結節（adductor tubercle）後方、內側髁關節面上方的狹長粗糙區域。
- **Lateral head（外側頭）：** 起自股骨外側髁上部外側面與外側髁上線（lateral supracondylar line）交界處的明顯小面。

在膝關節處，gastrocnemius 兩個頭的相對緣形成 popliteal fossa 下端的外側緣與內側緣。

在上小腿處，gastrocnemius 的兩個頭合併形成單一的長肌腹，構成小腿軟組織隆起（即「小腿肚」）的大部分。

在下小腿處，gastrocnemius 的肌纖維與更深的 soleus 肌肉的肌纖維會合，形成 **calcaneal tendon（跟腱）**，附著於足的跟骨。

Gastrocnemius 在踝關節處蹠屈足部，也能屈曲小腿。它由 tibial nerve 神經支配。

> **臨床重點：**
> **Achilles Tendon Rupture（阿基里斯腱斷裂）**
> 阿基里斯腱（跟腱）斷裂通常與突然或直接的創傷有關。這類損傷經常發生在正常健康的肌腱上。此外，某些條件可能使肌腱易於斷裂，包括：肌腱病變（由於過度使用或年齡相關的退行性變化），以及先前的跟腱干預措施如藥物注射與某些抗生素（氟喹諾酮類）的使用。阿基里斯腱斷裂的診斷相對簡單。患者通常主訴踝關節後側「被踢」或「中槍」的感覺，臨床檢查常可發現肌腱有缺口。

###### Plantaris（蹠肌）

**Plantaris** 近端有一個小肌腹，遠端有一條長細肌腱，穿過小腿並加入跟腱。該肌肉從股骨外側髁上線（lateral supracondylar ridge）下部與膝關節的 oblique popliteal ligament（斜腘韌帶）上方起源。

Plantaris 短紡錘狀的肌肉體下行至內側，深於 gastrocnemius 的外側頭，形成細肌腱，通過 gastrocnemius 與 soleus 之間，最終在跟腱附著於跟骨處附近與跟腱的內側融合。

Plantaris 參與踝關節處足的蹠屈與膝關節處小腿的屈曲，由 tibial nerve 神經支配。

###### Soleus（比目魚肌）

**Soleus** 是位於 gastrocnemius 下方的寬扁平肌肉。它附著於腓骨與脛骨的近端，以及跨越腓骨與脛骨兩個附著點之間的韌帶：

- 在腓骨近端，soleus 從腓骨頭後面與腓骨頸及近端骨幹的相鄰表面起源。
- 在脛骨，soleus 從 soleal line 與相鄰的內側緣起源。
- 跨越脛骨與腓骨之間的韌帶，拱越過 popliteal vessels 與 tibial nerve，它們從 popliteal fossa 進入小腿後筋膜室的深層。

在下小腿處，soleus 肌肉變細，與附著於跟骨的跟腱會合。

Soleus 與 gastrocnemius 和 plantaris 一起，在踝關節處蹠屈足部。它由 tibial nerve 神經支配。

##### Deep Group（深層群）

小腿後筋膜室深層有四塊肌肉——**popliteus（腘肌）**、**flexor hallucis longus（𧿹長屈肌）**、**flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）** 與 **tibialis posterior（脛後肌）**。Popliteus 作用於膝關節，而其他三塊肌肉主要作用於足部。

###### Popliteus（腘肌）

**Popliteus** 是小腿後筋膜室深層中最小且最上方的肌肉。它在屈曲開始時解鎖伸展的膝關節，並通過抵抗脛骨在股骨上的外側（外旋）旋轉來穩定膝關節。它呈扁平三角形。

###### Flexor Hallucis Longus（𧿹長屈肌）

Flexor hallucis longus 位於腓骨後表面，在其遠端通過跟骨與距骨的溝前行至足底，附著於大趾（𧿹趾）的遠節趾骨。

###### Flexor Digitorum Longus（趾長屈肌）

Flexor digitorum longus 起於脛骨後面，其肌腱前行至足底，分為四腱，附著於第 2-5 趾的遠節趾骨。

###### Tibialis Posterior（脛後肌）

**Tibialis posterior** 是最深層的肌肉，起於骨間膜與相鄰的脛骨與腓骨表面。其肌腱通過踝關節後方，附著於足底多塊骨骼。

## 小腿後側深層肌群

### Popliteus（腘肌）

Popliteus 肌肉形成膕窩底（popliteal fossa）的一部分，並附著於脛骨後面近端高於 soleal line 的廣大三角區域。

Popliteus 肌肉上行於膝關節外側，穿過關節囊纖維膜的肌腱而起源。肌腱於關節外側繞行，通過外側半月軟骨與纖維膜之間，然後進入股骨外側髁下外側面的淺溝。肌腱附著並起源於溝前端之凹陷處。

> **臨床重點**：行走時，從站立姿勢開始，Popliteus 收縮使股骨在固定脛骨上外旋，解除膝關節鎖定。Popliteus 由 **Tibial nerve（脛神經）** 支配。

---

### Flexor hallucis longus（拇長屈肌）

Flexor hallucis longus 肌肉起源於小腿後側筋膜室外側，附著於足內側之拇指（Fig. 6.84）。主要起始於腓骨後面下2/3及相鄰之骨間膜。

Flexor hallucis longus 的肌纖維向下匯集形成粗大索狀肌腱，經過脛骨遠端後方，穿過相鄰跗骨（距骨）後面之明確淺溝。肌腱先向前下方彎曲通過距骨下方，再通過由跟骨內側突出之骨性支架（sustentaculum tali）下方，然後繼續前行通過足底，附著於拇指遠端趾骨底之下面。

> **臨床重點**：Flexor hallucis longus 屈拇指。行走時於腳尖離地階段特別活躍，此時身體由支撐腿向前推進，拇指為足部最後離開地面之部分。此肌亦可輔助踝關節之蹠屈，並由 **Tibial nerve（脛神經）** 支配。

---

### Flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）

Flexor digitorum longus 肌肉起源於小腿後側筋膜室內側，附著於足部外側四趾（Fig. 6.84）。主要起始於脛骨後面內側、低於 soleal line 之區域。

Flexor digitorum longus 下降形成肌腱，於踝關節附近淺層越過 tibialis posterior 肌腱後方，並位於此肌腱內側，經過內踝後方之淺溝，然後向前下方進入足底。越過 flexor hallucis longus 肌腱下方到達足內側，分成四條肌腱，附著於第II–V趾遠端趾骨底之跖面。

> **臨床重點**：Flexor digitorum longus 屈外側四趾。行走時參與抓握地面並於支撐期结束时推动身体向前由脚趾離地。 由 **Tibial nerve（脛神經）** 支配。

---

### Tibialis posterior（脛後肌）

Tibialis posterior 肌肉起源於骨間膜及相鄰之脛骨、腓骨後面（Fig. 6.84）。位於 flexor digitorum longus 與 flexor hallucis longus 肌肉之間並被覆蓋。

在踝關節附近，Tibialis posterior 肌腱被 flexor digitorum longus 肌腱淺層越過，位於內踝後方溝槽中此肌腱之內側。肌腱向前下方彎曲通過內踝，進入足內側，繞過足內側緣，附著於足內側跗骨之跖面，主要附著於舟狀骨結節及相鄰之內側楔骨。

> **臨床重點**：Tibialis posterior 使足內翻及蹠屈，步行時支撐足內側縱弓。由 **Tibial nerve（脛神經）** 支配。

---

## 臨床探討：腿部神經學檢查

> **臨床重點**：影響腿部的常見疾病包括周邊神經病變（尤其與糖尿病相關）、腰神經根病變（與椎間盤病變相關）、腓神經麻痺及痙攣性下身麻痺。

**檢查項目**：

- **觀察肌肉萎縮**：肌肉容積消失可能表示神經支配喪失或減少。
- **測試肌群力量**：
 - 髖屈曲（L1, L2—iliopsoas，伸直抬腿測試）
 - 膝屈曲（L5至S2—hamstrings，患者屈膝時檢查者施力保持膝關節伸直）
 - 膝伸展（L3, L4—quadriceps femoris，患者尝试保持腿部伸直時檢查者施力屈膝）
 - 踝蹠屈（S1, S2—患者向下推足，檢查者於足底施力背屈踝關節）
 - 踝背屈（L4, L5—患者向上拉足，檢查者於足背施力蹠屈踝關節）
- **檢查膝反射與踝反射**：以叩診槌敲擊髕韌帶測試L3–L4脊髓節段；敲擊跟腱測試S1–S2脊髓節段。
- **評估感覺輸入**：測試下肢皮節之感覺，包括輕觸、針刺及震動覺。

---

## Arteries（動脈）

### Popliteal artery（膕動脈）

Popliteal artery 是腿部和足部主要血液供應來源，從膝關節後方之膕窩進入小腿後側筋膜室（Fig. 6.85）。

Popliteal artery 進入小腿後側筋膜室，介於 gastrocnemius 與 popliteus 肌肉之間。繼續下行時通過 soleus 肌肉腓骨頭與脛骨頭之間形成的腱弓下方，進入小腿後側筋膜室深層，隨即分為 **Anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）** 及 **Posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）**。

兩條大型腓腸動脈（sural arteries）左右各一，由 popliteal artery 分出，供應 gastrocnemius、soleus 及 plantaris 肌肉（Fig. 6.85）。此外，popliteal artery 發出分支參與膝關節周圍側支血管網絡。

---

### Anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）

Anterior tibial artery 穿過骨間膜上端之孔向前進入並供應小腿前側筋膜室。繼續下行至足背。

---

### Posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）

Posterior tibial artery 供應小腿後側及外側筋膜室，並繼續進入足底（Fig. 6.85）。

Posterior tibial artery 下降穿過小腿後側筋膜室深層，位於 tibialis posterior 與 flexor digitorum longus 肌肉之淺層。通過內踝後方之跗骨隧道（tarsal tunnel）進入足底。

在小腿，Posterior tibial artery 供應相鄰肌肉與骨，並有兩條主要分支：

- **Circumflex fibular artery（旋腓動脈）**：穿過 soleus 肌肉及繞過腓骨頸，與膝關節周圍之血管吻合網連接（Fig. 6.85）。
- **Fibular artery（腓動脈）**：與脛後動脈平行，但下降於小腿後側筋膜室外側，位於腓骨後面之內側嵴旁，該嵴為 tibialis posterior 與 flexor hallucis longus 肌肉附著處之分界。

Fibular artery 供應小腿後側筋膜室之相鄰肌肉與骨，並發出分支穿過肌間隔進入小腿外側筋膜室之腓骨肌。

Fibular artery 之穿通分支起源於小腿遠端，穿過骨間膜下方孔向前與 anterior tibial artery 分支吻合。Fibular artery 通過脛骨與腓骨遠端之間之附著處後方，終止於跟骨外側面之血管網絡。

---

## Veins（靜脈）

小腿後側筋膜室之深部靜脈大致伴隨同名動脈行走。

---

## Nerves（神經）

### Tibial nerve（脛神經）

小腿後側筋膜室之神經為 **Tibial nerve（Fig. 6.86）**，為坐骨神經之主要分支，從膕窩下降進入小腿後側筋膜室。

Tibial nerve 穿過 soleus 肌肉腓骨頭與脛骨頭之間形成之腱弓下方，垂直通過小腿後側筋膜室深層，與脛後血管共同位於 tibialis posterior 肌肉淺層。

Tibial nerve 通過內踝後方之跗骨隧道（tarsal tunnel）離開小腿後側筋膜室，進入足部供應大多數固有肌肉與皮膚。

> **臨床重點**：Tibial nerve 在小腿發出：
> - 支配小腿後側筋膜室所有肌肉之分支
> - 兩條皮支：**Sural nerve（腓腸神經）** 及 **Medial calcaneal nerve（內側跟骨神經）**

Tibial nerve 支配小腿後側筋膜室淺層肌群及 deep group 中 popliteus 肌肉之分支起源於小腿高處，介於 gastrocnemi us 兩頭之間之膕窩遠側（Fig. 6.87）。分支支配 gastrocnemius、plantaris、soleus 肌肉，並更深層進入 popliteus 肌肉。

支配後側筋膜室深層肌肉之分支起源於 soleus 肌肉深層之 Tibial nerve，位於小腿上半部，支配 tibialis posterior、flexor hallucis longus 及 flexor digitorum longus 肌肉。

---

#### Sural nerve（腓腸神經）

Sural nerve 起起源於小腿高處介於 gastrocnemius 兩頭之間（Fig. 6.86）。下行於 gastrocnemius 肌腹淺層，約在小腿中段穿過深層筋膜，此時與 common fibular nerve 之腓腸交通支結合。下降經過外踝周圍進入足部。

Sural nerve 支配小腿下後外側皮膚及足外側與小趾。

---

#### Medial calcaneal nerve（內側跟骨神經）

Medial calcaneal nerve 通常為多支，起源於小腿遠側靠近踝關節之 Tibial nerve，下降至足跟內側。

Medial calcaneal nerve 支配足跟內側面及足底之皮膚（Fig. 6.86）。

---

## Lateral Compartment of Leg（小腿外側筋膜室）
### 肌肉

小腿外側筋膜室有兩塊肌肉：**Fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）** 及 **Fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）**（Fig. 6.87 及 Table 6.8）。兩者均使足外翻（將足底轉向外側），並由 **Superficial fibular nerve（淺腓神經）** 支配，該神經為 common fibular nerve 之分支。

#### Table 6.8 小腿外側筋膜室肌肉

| Muscle（肌肉） | Origin（起始） | Insertion（附著） | Innervation（神經支配） | Function（功能） |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）** | 腓骨上外側面、腓骨頭、偶爾為脛骨外側髁 | 內側楔骨遠端下面及第一蹠骨底之下面外側 | Superficial fibular nerve（L5, S1, S2） | 足外翻及蹠屈；支撐足弓 |
| **Fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）** | 腓骨骨幹外側面下2/3 | 第五蹠骨底之外側結節 | Superficial fibular nerve（L5, S1, S2） | 足外翻 |

---

### Fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）

Fibularis longus 肌肉起源於小腿外側筋膜室，但肌腱於足底交叉附著於內側骨骼（Fig. 6.87）。起源於腓骨上外側面及腓骨頭之前面，偶爾向上延伸至脛骨外側髁相鄰區域。

Common fibular nerve 於腓骨頸前方繞行，介於 fibularis longus 至腓骨頭與骨幹之附著處之間。

Fibularis longus 遠端下降形成肌腱，依序：

1. 經過外踝後方之淺骨溝
2. 向前下方進入足外側
3. 傾斜下行於足外側面，在跟骨之骨性結節（fibular trochlea）下方向前彎曲
4. 進入另一跗骨（骰骨，cuboid）下面之深溝
5. 於足底交叉至足內側，附著於足內側骨骼之下面（第一蹠骨底外側面及內側楔骨遠端）

> **臨床重點**：Fibularis longus 使足外翻及蹠屈。此外，Fibularis longus、Tibialis anterior 與 Tibialis posterior 三塊均附著於足內側骨骼下面之肌肉，共同形成類似腳蹬之作用以支撐足弓。Fibularis longus 主要支撐外側縱弓及橫弓。由 **Superficial fibular nerve（淺腓神經）** 支配。

---

### Fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）

Fibularis brevis 肌肉位於小腿中 Fibularis longus 深層，起源於腓骨骨幹外側面下2/3（Fig. 6.87）。

Fibularis brevis 肌腱與 Fibularis longus 肌腱共同經過外踝後方，然後向前彎曲越過跟骨外面，附著於第五蹠骨底（小趾相關蹠骨）外側結節。

> **臨床重點**：Fibularis brevis 輔助足外翻。由 **Superficial fibular nerve（淺腓神經）** 支配。

---

### Arteries（外側筋膜室）

小腿外側筋膜室無主要動脈垂直通過。供應來源主要為小腿後側筋膜室中 **Fibular artery（腓動脈）** 之分支穿入外側筋膜室（Fig. 6.88）。

---

### Veins（靜脈）

深部靜脈大致伴隨同名動脈行走。

---

### Nerves（神經）

#### Superficial fibular nerve（淺腓神經）

小腿外側筋膜室之神經為 **Superficial fibular nerve**。此神經為 common fibular nerve 兩大分支之一，從膕窩進入小腿外側筋膜室（Fig. 6.88B）。

Common fibular nerve 起起源於坐骨神經（位於大腿後側筋膜室或膕窩），沿 biceps femoris 肌腱內緣越過 gastrocnemius 外側頭，向腓骨方向延伸。於此發出兩條皮支下行：

- **Sural communicating nerve（腓腸交通神經）**：與 tibial nerve 之 sural 分支結合，支配小腿下後外側皮膚之感覺神經
- **Lateral sural cutaneous nerve（外側腓腸皮神經）**：支配小腿上外側皮膚

Common fibular nerve 繼續繞過腓骨頸，通過 fibularis longus 至腓骨頭與骨幹之附著處之間，進入外側筋膜室。此處 Common fibular nerve 分為兩條終支：

- **Superficial fibular nerve（淺腓神經）**
- **Deep fibular nerve（深腓神經）**

Superficial fibular nerve 下行於外側筋膜室，位於 Fibularis longus 深層，支配 Fibularis longus 及 Fibularis brevis（Fig. 6.87B）。然後穿過小腿遠端深層筋膜進入足部，分為內側支與外側支，支配足背及趾背區域，但有以下例外：

- **拇指與第二趾間之蹠間隙**由 deep fibular nerve 支配
- **小趾外側**由 tibial nerve 之 sural 分支支配

Deep fibular nerve 向前內側穿過肌間隔進入小腿前側筋膜室並支配該筋膜室。

---

## Anterior Compartment of Leg（小腿前側筋膜室）
### 肌肉

小腿前側筋膜室有四塊肌肉：**Tibialis anterior（脛前肌）**、**Extensor hallucis longus（拇長伸肌）**、**Extensor digitorum longus（趾長伸肌）** 及 **Fibularis tertius（第三腓骨肌）**（Fig. 6.89 及 Table 6.9）。集體功能為於踝關節背屈足部、伸展足趾及內翻足部。所有肌肉均由 **Deep fibular nerve（深腓神經）** 支配，該神經為 common fibular nerve 之分支。

---

### Tibialis anterior（脛前肌）

Tibialis anterior 肌肉為小腿前側筋膜室中最前內側之肌肉（Fig. 6.89）。起源於外側脛骨髁及相鄰之腓骨骨幹。肌肉下行形成粗大肌腱，通過踝關節前方及足背，附著於內側楔骨下面及第一蹠骨底。

Tibialis anterior 使足背屈並內翻足部，為步行時主要抬足肌肉。由 **Deep fibular nerve（深腓神經）** 支配。

---

### Extensor hallucis longus（拇長伸肌）

Extensor hallucis longus 肌肉起源於腓骨前面及相鄰之骨間膜（Fig. 6.89）。肌腱通過踝關節前方之深層伸肌支持帶，附著於拇指遠端趾骨底之上方。

Extensor hallucis longus 伸展拇指，並輔助踝關節背屈。由 **Deep fibular nerve（深腓神經）** 支配。

---

### Extensor digitorum longus（趾長伸肌）

Extensor digitorum longus 肌肉起源於腓骨前面及脛骨外側髁（Fig. 6.89）。肌腱通過踝關節前方，分為四條，分別附著於外側四趾之遠端趾骨。

Extensor digitorum longus 伸展外側四趾，並輔助踝關節背屈。由 **Deep fibular nerve（深腓神經）** 支配。

---

### Fibularis tertius（第三腓骨肌）

Fibularis tertius 為 extensor digitorum longus 之部分，通常視為獨立肌肉。起源於腓骨前面，肌腱穿過踝關節前方附著於第五蹠骨底背面。

Fibularis tertius 輔助足外翻及背屈。由 **Deep fibular nerve（深腓神經）** 支配。

---

## 總結與臨床關聯

### 肌肉神經支配快速參照

| 筋膜室 | 主要肌肉 | 支配神經 |
|---|---|---|
| **後側（深層）** | Popliteus、Tibialis posterior、Flexor hallucis longus、Flexor digitorum longus | Tibial nerve（L4–S3） |
| **外側** | Fibularis longus、Fibularis brevis | Superficial fibular nerve（L5–S2） |
| **前側** | Tibialis anterior、Extensor hallucis longus、Extensor digitorum longus、Fibularis tertius | Deep fibular nerve（L4–L5） |

### 主要血管

| 血管 | 路徑 |
|---|---|
| **Popliteal artery** | 膕窩 → 小腿後側筋膜室 → 分為 Anterior tibial artery 及 Posterior tibial artery |
| **Posterior tibial artery** | 小腿後側深層 → 跗骨隧道 → 足底 |
| **Fibular artery** | 小腿後側 → 穿支至外側筋膜室 |
| **Anterior tibial artery** | 穿骨間膜 → 小腿前側筋膜室 → 足背 |

> **臨床重點**：本段內容涵蓋小腿部神經血管結構。臨床上需注意：
> - **腓骨頸骨折**可能損傷 Common fibular nerve，導致足下垂（deep fibular nerve 支配前側及外側室肌肉）
> - **踝關節骨折或脫位**可能同時損傷重要血管及神經
> - **小腿筋膜室症候群**：創傷後可能因筋膜室內壓力增加導致血液循環受阻，需要緊急減壓

## Anterior Compartment of Leg（小腿前側筋膜室）
### Muscles of the Anterior Compartment of Leg（小腿前側筋膜室肌肉）
> **神經支配說明**：脊髓節段以粗體標示者為支配該肌肉的主要節段。

| **肌肉** | **起始部** | **附著部** | **神經支配** | **功能** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tibialis anterior** | 脛骨外側面及相鄰的骨間膜 | 內側楔骨與第一蹠骨基底部的內側及下方表面 | **Deep fibular nerve**（L4, L5） | 踝關節背屈；足部內翻；动态支撑足部內側縱弓 |
| **Extensor hallucis longus** | 腓骨內側面中部二分之一及相鄰骨間膜 | 拇指遠端趾骨背側基底 | **Deep fibular nerve**（L5, S1） | 伸展拇指；背屈足部 |
| **Extensor digitorum longus** | 腓骨內側面近端二分之一及脛骨外側髁相鄰面 | 經由背側指部伸肌腱膜附著至外側四趾中、遠端趾骨基底背側 | **Deep fibular nerve**（L5, S1） | 伸展外側四趾；背屈足部 |
| **Fibularis tertius** | 腓骨內側面遠端 | 第五蹠骨基底背內側面 | **Deep fibular nerve**（L5, S1） | 背屈及可能的外翻 |

---

### Tibialis anterior

**Tibialis anterior** 為小腿前側筋膜室中最大、最表層的肌肉（圖 6.89）。主要起始於脛骨骨幹外側面近三分之二及骨間膜相鄰面，亦起始於深筋膜。

Tibialis anterior 的肌纖維在小腿遠端三分之一處匯集形成肌腱，向下延伸至足部內側，附著於一塊跗骨（**內側楔骨**）的內側及下方表面，以及與拇指相關的第一蹠骨相鄰部位。

Tibialis anterior 可使足部在踝關節處背屈，並在跗骨間關節處使足部內翻。行走時，此肌肉為足部內側縱弓提供動態支撐。

Tibialis anterior 由 **deep fibular nerve** 神經支配。

### Extensor hallucis longus

**Extensor hallucis longus** 肌肉緊鄰並部分被 Tibialis anterior 覆蓋（圖 6.89）。起始於腓骨內側面中部二分之一及相鄰的骨間膜。

Extensor hallucis longus 的肌腱出現在小腿遠端二分之一處，介於 Tibialis anterior 與 Extensor digitorum longus 兩肌腱之間，然後向下延伸入足部。它沿足部背側內側面前行，至拇指末端附近附著於遠端趾骨基底的上表面。

Extensor hallucis longus 可伸展拇指。由於此肌肉跨越踝關節前方，亦可使足部在踝關節處背屈。與前側筋膜室所有肌肉相同，Extensor hallucis longus 由 **deep fibular nerve** 支配。

### Extensor digitorum longus

**Extensor digitorum longus** 是小腿前側筋膜室中最後外側的肌肉（圖 6.89）。主要起始於腓骨內側面近端二分之一（位於 Extensor hallucis longus 起點的外側及上方），並向上延伸至脛骨外側髁。與 Tibialis anterior 相同，亦起始於深筋膜。

Extensor digitorum longus 向下形成肌腱，延續至足部背側，分成四條肌腱，經由背側指部伸肌腱膜附著至外側四趾中段及遠端趾骨基底的背側表面。

Extensor digitorum longus 可伸展各趾，並在踝關節處背屈足部，由 **deep fibular nerve** 支配。

### Fibularis tertius

**Fibularis tertius** 臨床上通常被視為 Extensor digitorum longus 的一部分（圖 6.89）。Fibularis tertius 起於腓骨內側面，緊鄰 Extensor digitorum longus 起點下方，兩肌肉通常相互連接。

Fibularis tertius 的肌腱與 Extensor digitorum longus 的肌腱一同向下延伸入足部。在足部背側，它向外偏斜，附著於第五蹠骨（與小趾相關的蹠骨）基底的背內側表面。

Fibularis tertius 輔助足部背屈，並可能輔助外翻，由 **deep fibular nerve** 支配。

---

## Arteries（小腿前側筋膜室的動脈）
### Anterior Tibial Artery

與小腿前側筋膜室相關的動脈為 **anterior tibial artery**，起源於小腿後側筋膜室的 popliteal artery，經由骨間膜上的孔道向前穿入小腿前側筋膜室。

Anterior tibial artery 沿骨間膜下行通過前側筋膜室（圖 6.90）。在小腿遠端，介於 Tibialis anterior 與 Extensor hallucis longus 兩肌腱之間。它離開小腿時經過脛骨遠端及踝關節前方，延續至足部背側，成為 **dorsalis pedis artery**。

在小腿近端，anterior tibial artery 有一條返回支，與膝關節周圍的血管吻合網相連。

沿途，anterior tibial artery 發出多條分支供應相鄰肌肉，並接受fibular artery 的穿支，該穿支經骨間膜下部從小腿後側筋膜室向前穿出。遠端處，anterior tibial artery 發出 **anterior medial malleolar artery** 及 **anterior lateral malleolar artery**，分別繞過脛骨及腓骨遠端後方，與後側脛骨及腓骨來的血管會合，形成踝關節周圍的吻合網。

## Nerves（小腿前側筋膜室的神經）
### Deep Fibular Nerve

與小腿前側筋膜室相關的神經為 **deep fibular nerve**（圖 6.90）。此神經起源於小腿外側筋膜室，為 common fibular nerve 的兩條分支之一。

Deep fibular nerve 向內前方穿過分隔外側與前側筋膜室的肌間隔，然後穿過 Extensor digitorum longus 深層。它到達前側骨間膜，與 anterior tibial artery 會合並伴隨下行。

Deep fibular nerve：
- 支配前側筋膜室的所有肌肉；
- 繼續進入足部背側，支配 **extensor digitorum brevis**，參與第一、二背側骨間肌的神經支配，並供應拇指與第二趾間的皮膚感覺。

> **臨床重點**：**垂足（Footdrop）**
>
> 垂足是指無法背屈足部的病症。垂足患者具有典型的「階梯樣步態（steppage gait）」。患者行走時，受影響肢體在擺動期會將膝關節抬高至異常高度，以防止足部拖曳。擺動期末期，足部會「拍打」地面。此外，未受影響的肢體在站立期常呈現典型的蹠腳尖步態。垂足的典型原因是 **common fibular nerve** 損傷。其他原因包括椎間盤突出壓迫 L5 神經根、sciatic nerve 及腰骶叢病變、脊髓及腦部病理。

## Foot（足部）
### 概述

**足部**是踝關節遠端的下肢區域，分為踝部、蹠骨部（metatarsus）及趾部（digits）。

足部有五根趾：內側為拇指（digit I），向外依次為四趾，最外側為小趾（digit V）（圖 6.91）。

足部有上表面（**dorsum of foot**）及下表面（**sole**）（圖 6.91）。

趾的外展與內收以第二趾的長軸為參考定義。與手部不同，拇指與其他手指成 90 度定向，而拇指與其他趾的定向相同。足部是人體與地面的接觸點，為直立站姿提供穩定的平台，並在行走時推動身體向前。

---

### Bones（足部骨骼）
足部骨骼可分為三群（圖 6.92）：

- **七塊跗骨**，構成踝關節的骨骼框架；
- **五塊蹠骨**（I 至 V），為蹠骨部的骨骼；
- **趾骨**，為趾部的骨骼——每趾有三節趾骨，拇指僅有兩節。

#### 跗骨群（Tarsal Bones）

跗骨排列為近端群與遠端群，兩群之間在足部內側有一塊中間骨（圖 6.92A）。

##### 近端群（Proximal Group）

近端群由兩塊大型骨骼組成：**talus**（拉丁語「踝」）與 **calcaneus**（拉丁語「踵」）：

- **Talus** 是足部最上方的骨骼，位於並支撐於 calcaneus 之上（圖 6.92B）——上方與脛骨及腓骨形成踝關節，並向前突出與足部內側的中間跗骨（**navicular**）形成關節。
- **Calcaneus** 是最大的跗骨——後方構成踵的骨骼框架，前方突出與遠端跗骨群中的一塊（**cuboid**）在外側形成關節。

##### Talus（踝骨）

Talus 從內側或外側觀看呈蝸牛狀（圖 6.93A, B）。具有圓形的頭部，由短而寬的頸部向前內側突出，頸部後方連接擴大的本體。

前方，talus 的頭部呈圓頂狀，與 navicular 骨後表面相應的圓形凹陷形成關節。下方，此圓頂狀關節面與三個關節面連續，以平滑骨脊分隔：

- 前關節面與中關節面與 calcaneus 骨的相鄰面形成關節。
- 另一關節面（位於與 calcaneus 關節面的內側）與一條韌帶——**plantar calcaneonavicular ligament（spring ligament）**——形成關節，此韌帶連接 calcaneus 與位於 talus 頭部下方的 navicular。

Talus 的頸部有一條深溝（**sulcus tali**），從內側到外側斜向前方橫越下方表面，在外側明顯擴大。Sulcus tali 後方有一個大關節面（後跟骨關節面）與 calcaneus 形成關節。

Talus 本體的上方升高，嵌入脛骨與腓骨遠端形成的關節窩，形成踝關節：

- 此升高區域的上（滑車）關節面與脛骨遠端形成關節。
- 內側關節面與脛骨的 **medial malleolus** 形成關節。
- 外側關節面與腓骨的 **lateral malleolus** 形成關節。

由於 lateral malleolus 在踝關節處較 medial malleolus 更大且向下突出更多，相應的 talus 外側關節面也較內側關節面更大且向下突出更多。

Talus 本體外側面的下部（支撐與腓骨關節面的下部）形成一個骨性突起（**lateral process**）。

Talus 本體的下方有一個大的橢圓形凹關節面（**後跟骨關節面**）與 calcaneus 形成關節。

Talus 本體的後面有一個向後並向內側的突起（**posterior process**）。此突起的表面有外側結節與內側結節，兩者之間夾著 **flexor hallucis longus** 肌腱從小腿進入足部的通道。

##### Calcaneus（跟骨）

Calcaneus 位於 talus 下方並支撐它。它是一塊長而不規則的盒狀骨骼，長軸大致沿足部中線，但前端偏向中線外側（圖 6.94）。

Calcaneus 在踝關節後方突出，構成踵的骨骼框架。此踵區的後表面呈圓形，分為上、中、下三部。**Calcaneal tendon（Achilles tendon）** 附著於中部：

- 上部與 calcaneal tendon 之間隔有滑囊。
- 下部向前彎曲，被皮下組織覆蓋，是踵的負重區域，延續至骨骼的蹠側面成為 **calcaneal tuberosity**。

Calcaneal tuberosity 在蹠側面往前突出，分為一個大的內側突與一個小的外側突，兩者之間以 V 形切跡分開（圖 6.94B）。蹠側面前端有一個結節（**calcaneal tubercle**），為足底短韌帶的後側附著處。

Calcaneus 的外側面輪廓平滑，只有兩處輕微突起的區域（圖 6.94C）。其中一處突起——**fibular trochlea（peroneal tubercle）**——位於面中部前方，常有兩條淺溝上下排列斜越其面。**Fibularis brevis** 與 **fibularis longus** 的肌腱在經過 calcaneus 外側面時束縛於此滑車上。

在 fibular trochlea 上後方有第二處突起或結節，為踝關節外側韌帶中 **calcaneofibular part** 的附著處。

Calcaneus 的內側面凹陷，上緣有一個顯著結構（**sustentaculum tali**；圖 6.94A），為一塊向內側突出的骨板，支撐 talus 頭部的後側部分。

Sustentaculum tali 的下方有一條明顯的溝，從後向前，**flexor hallucis longus** 的肌腱沿此溝進入足底。

Sustentaculum tali 的上方有一個關節面（**middle talar articular surface**）與 talus 頭部相應的中關節面形成關節。

Calcaneus 本身上方有前與後 talar 關節面（圖 6.94A）：

- **前talar 關節面**較小，與 talus 頭部相應的前關節面形成關節。
- **後talar 關節面**較大，約位於 calcaneus 上表面的中部。

後talar 關節面（與 talus 本體形成關節）與其他兩個關節面（與 talus 頭部形成關節）之間有一條深溝（**calcaneal sulcus**；圖 6.94A, C）。

Calcaneus 上方的 calcaneal sulcus 與 talus 下方的 sulcus tali 共同構成 **tarsal sinus**，為 calcaneus 與 talus 前端之間的大隙縫，從足部外側觀看可見（圖 6.95）。

##### 中間跗骨（Intermediate Tarsal Bone）

位於足部內側的中間跗骨為 **navicular**（船形；圖 6.92）。此骨後方與 talus 形成關節，前方及外側與遠端跗骨群形成關節。

Navicular 的一個顯著特徵是有一個突出的圓形結節，為 **tibialis posterior** 肌腱的附著處，向下內側突出於骨的蹠側面。

##### 遠端群（Distal Group）

從外側到內側，遠端跗骨群包括（圖 6.92）：

- **Cuboid**（希臘語「立方體」），後方與 calcaneus 形成關節，內側與外側楔骨形成關節，前方與外側兩塊蹠骨基底形成關節——**fibularis longus** 的肌腱位於前側蹠側面上的一條明顯溝中，從外側到內側斜向前穿過此骨。
- **三塊楔骨（cuneiforms）**——外側、中間及內側楔骨，除彼此之間相互關節外，後方與 navicular 形成關節，前方與內側三塊蹠骨基底形成關節。

#### 蹠骨（Metatarsals）

足部有五塊蹠骨，從內側到外側編號 I 至 V（圖 6.96）。與拇指相關的第一蹠骨最短且最粗，第二蹠骨最長。

每塊蹠骨遠端有頭部，中間有細長的骨幹，近端有基底。

每塊蹠骨的頭部與趾的近端趾骨形成關節，基底與一塊或多塊遠端跗骨形成關節。蹠側面有強韌的深橫韌帶連接相鄰蹠骨頭部。

#### 趾骨（Phalanges）

每趾有三節趾骨：近端、中間及遠端趾骨（圖 6.96）。拇指僅有近端與遠端兩節趾骨。

每節趾骨有：

- **基底**，與相鄰趾骨或蹠骨形成關節；
- **本體**；
- **頭部**，遠端與下一趾骨形成關節（拇指除外，其遠端趾骨無遠端關節）。

近端趾骨基底有橢圓形關節面，與蹠骨頭部形成蹠趾關節（metatarsophalangeal joint）。

## Phalanges (趾骨)

The phalanges are the bones of the toes (Fig. 6.96). Each toe has three phalanges (proximal, middle, and distal), except for the great toe, which has only two (proximal and distal).

Each phalanx consists of a **base**, a **shaft**, and a **distal head**:

- The **base** of each proximal phalanx articulates with the head of the related metatarsal.
- The **head** of each distal phalanx is nonarticular and flattened into a crescent-shaped plantar tuberosity under the plantar pad at the end of the digit.

In each toe, the total length of the phalanges combined is much shorter than the length of the associated metatarsal.

> **臨床重點**：骨折 of the talus
>
> The talus is an unusual bone because it ossifies from a single primary ossification center, which initially appears in the neck. The posterior aspect of the talus appears to ossify last, normally after puberty. In up to 50% of people there is a small accessory ossicle (the **os trigonum**) posterior to the lateral tubercle of the posterior process.
>
> Articular cartilage covers approximately 60% of the talar surface and there are no direct tendon or muscle attachments to the bone.
>
> One of the problems with fractures of the talus is that the blood supply to the bone is vulnerable to damage. The main blood supply to the bone enters the talus through the tarsal canal from a branch of the **posterior tibial artery**. This vessel supplies most of the neck and the body of the talus. Branches of the **dorsalis pedis** artery enter the superior aspect of the talar neck and supply the dorsal portion of the head and neck, and branches from the **fibular artery** supply a small portion of the lateral talus.
>
> Fractures of the neck of the talus often interrupt the blood supply to the talus, so making the body and posterior aspect of the talus susceptible to **osteonecrosis**, which may in turn lead to premature osteoarthritis and require extensive surgery.

> **臨床重點**：Midfoot fractures
>
> Fractures of the midfoot are uncommon. They usually occur when heavy weights have been dropped onto the feet or when the feet have been run over by a vehicle. Plain radiographs are usually sufficient to demonstrate dislocations and fractures.

## Ankle Joint (踝關節)

The ankle joint is synovial in type and involves the **talus** of the foot and the **tibia** and **fibula** of the leg (Fig. 6.97).

The ankle joint mainly allows hinge-like **dorsiflexion** and **plantarflexion** of the foot on the leg.

The distal end of the fibula is firmly anchored to the larger distal end of the tibia by strong ligaments. Together, the fibula and tibia create a deep bracket-shaped socket for the upper expanded part of the body of the talus:

- The **roof** of the socket is formed by the inferior surface of the distal end of the **tibia**.
- The **medial side** of the socket is formed by the **medial malleolus** of the tibia.
- The **lateral side** of the socket is formed by the **lateral malleolus** of the fibula.

The articular surfaces are covered by **hyaline cartilage**. The articular part of the talus is shaped like a short half-cylinder tipped onto its flat side with one end facing lateral and the other end facing medial. The curved upper surface of the half-cylinder and the two ends are covered by hyaline cartilage and fit into the bracket-shaped socket formed by the distal ends of the tibia and fibula.

When viewed from above, the articular surface of the talus is much wider anteriorly than it is posteriorly. As a result, the bone fits tighter into its socket when the foot is dorsiflexed and the wider surface of the talus moves into the ankle joint than when the foot is plantarflexed and the narrower part of the talus is in the joint. The joint is therefore **most stable when the foot is dorsiflexed**.

The articular cavity is enclosed by a **synovial membrane**, which attaches around the margins of the articular surfaces, and by a **fibrous membrane**, which covers the synovial membrane and is also attached to the adjacent bones.

The ankle joint is stabilized by **medial (deltoid)** and **lateral ligaments**.

### Medial Ligament (Deltoid Ligament) (內側韌帶/三角韌帶)

The medial (deltoid) ligament is large, strong (Fig. 6.98), and triangular in shape. Its apex is attached above to the **medial malleolus** and its broad base is attached below to a line that extends from the **tuberosity of the navicular bone** in front to the **medial tubercle of the talus** behind.

The medial ligament is subdivided into four parts based on the inferior points of attachment:

- The part that attaches in front to the tuberosity of the navicular and the associated margin of the **plantar calcaneonavicular ligament** (spring ligament), which connects the navicular bone to the sustentaculum tali of the calcaneus bone behind, is the **tibionavicular part** of the medial ligament.
- The **tibiocalcaneal part**, which is more central, attaches to the **sustentaculum tali** of the calcaneus bone.
- The **posterior tibiotalar part** attaches to the medial side and medial tubercle of the talus.
- The fourth part (the **anterior tibiotalar part**) is deep to the tibionavicular and tibiocalcaneal parts of the medial ligament and attaches to the medial surface of the talus.

### Lateral Ligament (外側韌帶)

The lateral ligament of the ankle is composed of three separate ligaments: the **anterior talofibular ligament**, the **posterior talofibular ligament**, and the **calcaneofibular ligament** (Fig. 6.99):

- The **anterior talofibular ligament** is a short ligament, and attaches the anterior margin of the lateral malleolus to the adjacent region of the talus.
- The **posterior talofibular ligament** runs horizontally backward and medially from the malleolar fossa on the medial side of the lateral malleolus to the posterior process of the talus.
- The **calcaneofibular ligament** is attached above to the malleolar fossa on the posteromedial side of the lateral malleolus and passes posteroinferiorly to attach below to a tubercle on the lateral surface of the calcaneus.

## Intertarsal Joints (蹠骨間關節)

The numerous synovial joints between the individual tarsal bones mainly invert, evert, supinate, and pronate the foot:

- **Inversion** and **eversion** is turning the whole sole of the foot inward and outward, respectively.
- **Pronation** is rotating the front of the foot laterally relative to the back of the foot, and **supination** is the reverse movement.

Pronation and supination allow the foot to maintain normal contact with the ground when in different stances or when standing on irregular surfaces.

The major joints at which movements occur include the **subtalar**, **talocalcaneonavicular**, and **calcaneocuboid joints** (Fig. 6.100). The talocalcaneonavicular and calcaneocuboid joints together form what is often referred to as the **transverse tarsal joint**.

Intertarsal joints between the cuneiforms and between the cuneiforms and the navicular allow only limited movement. The joint between the cuboid and navicular is normally fibrous.

> **臨床重點**：Ankle fractures
>
> An appreciation of ankle anatomy is essential to understand the wide variety of fractures that may occur at and around the ankle joint.
>
> The ankle joint and related structures can be regarded as a fibro-osseous ring oriented in the coronal plane:
>
> - The upper part of the ring is formed by the joint between the distal ends of the fibula and tibia and by the ankle joint itself.
> - The sides of the ring are formed by the ligaments that connect the medial malleolus and lateral malleolus to the adjacent tarsal bones.
> - The bottom of the ring is not part of the ankle joint, but consists of the subtalar joint and the associated ligaments.
>
> Visualizing the ankle joint and surrounding structures as a fibro-osseous ring allows the physician to predict the type of damage likely to result from a particular type of injury. For example, an inversion injury may fracture the medial malleolus and tear ligaments anchoring the lateral malleolus to the tarsal bones.
>
> The ring may be disrupted not only by damage to the bones (which produces fractures), but also by damage to the ligaments. Unlike bone fractures, damage to ligaments is unlikely to be appreciated on plain radiographs. When a fracture is noted on a plain radiograph, the physician must always be aware that there may also be appreciable ligamentous disruption.

> **臨床重點**：Ottawa Ankle Rules
>
> The Ottawa ankle rules were developed to assist clinicians in deciding whether patients with acute ankle injuries require investigation with radiographs in order to avoid unnecessary studies. Named after the hospital where they were developed, the rules are highly sensitive and have reduced the utilization of unwarranted ankle radiographs since their implementation.
>
> **An ankle x-ray series is required** if there is ankle pain and any of the following:
>
> - Bone tenderness along the distal 6 cm of the posterior tibia or tip of the medial malleolus
> - Bone tenderness along the distal 6 cm of the posterior fibula or tip of the lateral malleolus
> - Inability to bear weight for four steps both immediately after the injury and in the emergency department
>
> **A foot x-ray series is required** if there is midfoot pain and any of the following:
>
> - Bone tenderness at the base of the fifth metatarsal bone
> - Bone tenderness at the navicular bone
> - Inability to bear weight for four steps both immediately after the injury and in the emergency department

### Subtalar Joint (距下關節)

The subtalar joint is between:

- the large **posterior calcaneal facet** on the inferior surface of the talus, and
- the corresponding **posterior talar facet** on the superior surface of the calcaneus.

The articular cavity is enclosed by **synovial membrane**, which is covered by a **fibrous membrane**.

The subtalar joint allows **gliding and rotation**, which are involved in **inversion and eversion** of the foot. **Lateral**, **medial**, **posterior**, and **interosseous talocalcaneal ligaments** stabilize the joint. The **interosseous talocalcaneal ligament** lies in the tarsal sinus (Fig. 6.101).

### Talocalcaneonavicular Joint (距舟關節)

The talocalcaneonavicular joint is a complex joint in which the **head of the talus** articulates with the calcaneus and **plantar calcaneonavicular ligament** (spring ligament) below and the **navicular** in front (Fig. 6.102A).

The talocalcaneonavicular joint allows **gliding and rotation movements**, which together with similar movements of the subtalar joint are involved with **inversion and eversion** of the foot. It also participates in **pronation and supination**.

The parts of the talocalcaneonavicular joint between the talus and calcaneus are:

- the **anterior and middle calcaneal facets** on the inferior surface of the talar head, and
- the corresponding **anterior and middle talar facets** on the superior surface and sustentaculum tali, respectively, of the calcaneus (Fig. 6.102B).

The part of the joint between the talus and the plantar calcaneonavicular ligament (spring ligament) is between the ligament and the **medial facet** on the inferior surface of the talar head.

The joint between the navicular and talus is the largest part of the talocalcaneonavicular joint and is between the ovoid anterior end of the talar head and the corresponding concave posterior surface of the navicular.

#### Ligaments

The capsule of the talocalcaneonavicular joint, which is a synovial joint, is reinforced:

- **posteriorly** by the **interosseous talocalcaneal ligament**,
- **superiorly** by the **talonavicular ligament**, which passes between the neck of the talus and adjacent regions of the navicular, and
- **inferiorly** by the **plantar calcaneonavicular ligament** (spring ligament) (Fig. 6.102C,D).

The lateral part of the talocalcaneonavicular joint is reinforced by the **calcaneonavicular part of the bifurcate ligament**, which is a Y-shaped ligament superior to the joint. The base of the bifurcate ligament is attached to the anterior aspect of the superior surface of the calcaneus and its arms are attached to:

- the dorsomedial surface of the cuboid (calcaneocuboid ligament), and
- the dorsolateral part of the navicular (calcaneonavicular ligament).

The **plantar calcaneonavicular ligament** (spring ligament) is a broad thick ligament that spans the space between the **sustentaculum tali** behind and the **navicular bone** in front (Fig. 6.102B,C). It supports the head of the talus, takes part in the talocalcaneonavicular joint, and resists depression of the **medial arch** of the foot.

### Calcaneocuboid Joint (跟骰關節)

The calcaneocuboid joint is a synovial joint between:

- the facet on the **anterior surface of the calcaneus**, and
- the corresponding facet on the **posterior surface of the cuboid**.

The calcaneocuboid joint allows **sliding and rotating movements** involved with **inversion and eversion** of the foot, and also contributes to **pronation and supination** of the forefoot on the hindfoot.

#### Ligaments

The calcaneocuboid joint is reinforced by the **bifurcate ligament** (see above) and by the **long plantar ligament** and the **plantar calcaneocuboid ligament** (short plantar ligament).

The **plantar calcaneocuboid ligament** (short plantar ligament) is short, wide, and very strong, and connects the **calcaneal tubercle** to the inferior surface of the cuboid (Fig. 6.103A). It not only supports the calcaneocuboid joint, but also assists the long plantar ligament in resisting depression of the **lateral arch** of the foot.

The **long plantar ligament** is the longest ligament in the sole of the foot and lies inferior to the plantar calcaneocuboid ligament (Fig. 6.103B):

- **Posteriorly**, it attaches to the inferior surface of the calcaneus between the tuberosity and the calcaneal tubercle.
- **Anteriorly**, it attaches to a broad ridge and a tubercle on the inferior surface of the cuboid bone behind the groove for the **fibularis longus tendon**.

More superficial fibers of the long plantar ligament extend to the bases of the metatarsal bones. The long plantar ligament supports the calcaneocuboid joint and is the strongest ligament, resisting depression of the **lateral arch** of the foot.

## Tarsometatarsal Joints (跗蹠關節)

The tarsometatarsal joints between the metatarsal bones and adjacent tarsal bones are **plane joints** and allow limited **sliding movements** (Fig. 6.104).

The range of movement of the tarsometatarsal joint between the metatarsal of the great toe and the **medial cuneiform** is greater than that of the other tarsometatarsal joints and allows **flexion, extension, and rotation**. The tarsometatarsal joints, with the **transverse tarsal joint**, take part in **pronation and supination** of the foot.

---

## Metatarsophalangeal Joints (MTP Joints) (蹠趾關節)

The metatarsophalangeal joints are **ellipsoid synovial joints** between the sphere-shaped heads of the metatarsals and the corresponding bases of the proximal phalanges of the digits.

The metatarsophalangeal joints allow **extension and flexion**, and limited **abduction, adduction, rotation, and circumduction**.

The joint capsules are reinforced by **medial and lateral collateral ligaments**, and by **plantar ligaments**, which have grooves on their plantar surfaces for the long tendons of the digits (Fig. 6.104).

### Deep Transverse Metatarsal Ligaments

Four **deep transverse metatarsal ligaments** link the heads of the metatarsals together and enable the metatarsals to act as a single unified structure (Fig. 6.104). The ligaments blend with the plantar ligaments of the adjacent metatarsophalangeal joints.

The metatarsal of the great toe is oriented in the same plane as the metatarsals of the other toes and is linked to the metatarsal of the second toe by a **deep transverse metatarsal ligament**. In addition, the joint between the metatarsal of the great toe and medial cuneiform has a limited range of motion. The great toe therefore has a very restricted independent function—unlike the thumb in the hand, where the metacarpal is oriented 90° to the metacarpals of the fingers, there is no deep transverse metacarpal ligament between the metacarpals of the thumb and index finger, and the joint between the metacarpal and carpal bone allows a wide range of motion.

> **臨床重點**：Bunions (拇趾外翻)
>
> A **bunion** occurs on the medial aspect of the first metatarsophalangeal joint. This is an extremely important area of the foot because it is crossed by tendons and ligaments, which transmit and distribute the body's weight during movement. It is postulated that abnormal stresses in this region of the joint may produce the bunion deformity.
>
> Clinically, a bunion is a significant protuberance of bone that may include soft tissue around the medial aspect of the first metatarsophalangeal joint. As it progresses, the toe appears to move toward the smaller toes, producing crowding of the digits.
>
> This deformity tends to occur among people who wear high-heeled or pointed shoes, but osteoporosis and a hereditary predisposition are also risk factors.
>
> Typically the patient's symptoms are **pain, swelling, and inflammation**. The bunion tends to enlarge and may cause problems in obtaining appropriate footwear.
>
> Initial treatment is by adding padding to shoes, changing the type of footwear used, and taking anti-inflammatory drugs. Some patients may need surgery to correct the deformity and realign the toe.

## Interphalangeal Joints（指節關節）

**Interphalangeal joints（指節關節）** 是**hinge joints（樞紐關節）**，主要允許**屈曲（flexion）**與**伸展（extension）**。它們由**medial collateral ligament（內側副韌帶）**、**lateral collateral ligament（外側副韌帶）**以及**plantar ligaments（蹠側韌帶）**所加強（圖 6.104）。

## Tarsal Tunnel, Retinacula, and Arrangement of Major Structures at the Ankle（踝隧道、 支持帶與踝部主要結構之排列）

### Tarsal Tunnel（踝隧道）

**Tarsal tunnel（踝隧道）** 形成於踝關節的**後內側（posteromedial side）**，由以下結構組成：

- 由以下骨骼所形成的凹陷：
 - **Tibia（脛骨）** 的 **medial malleolus（內踝）**
 - **Talus（距骨）** 的內側與後表面
 - **Calcaneus（跟骨）** 的內側面
 - **Calcaneus（跟骨）** 的 **sustentaculum tali（載距突）** 下表面
- 上方覆蓋的 **flexor retinaculum（屈肌支持帶）**（圖 6.105）

### Flexor Retinaculum（屈肌支持帶）

**Flexor retinaculum（屈肌支持帶）** 是一片帶狀的**結締組織層**，跨越由以下結構形成的骨性凹陷：
- **Medial malleolus（內踝）**
- **Talus（距骨）** 的內側與後表面
- **Calcaneus（跟骨）** 的內側面
- **Sustentaculum tali（載距突）** 下表面

它附著於上方至**medial malleolus（內踝）**，下方與後方至**calcaneus（跟骨）** 的內下緣。

> **臨床重點**：**Flexor retinaculum（屈肌支持帶）** 上方與**小腿深筋膜（deep fascia of the leg）**相延續，下方與**足部深筋膜（plantar aponeurosis）**相延續。來自屈肌支持帶的**隔膜（septa）**將骨頭上的溝槽轉化為管狀結締組織通道，供屈肌肌腱從小腿後腔隙進入足底時穿過（圖 6.105）。肌腱在通道內的自由活動由圍繞肌腱的**滑膜鞘（synovial sheaths）**促進。

**Tibia（脛骨）** 的支持帶與小腿深筋膜及足底深筋膜相延續。

踝關節後內側面有兩個通道：
- **Tibialis posterior（脛骨後肌）** 與 **flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）** 肌腱的通道。**Tibialis posterior 肌腱**位於 **flexor digitorum longus 肌腱**的內側。
- 在 **tibialis posterior** 與 **flexor digitorum longus** 肌腱的外側，**posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）** 及其伴行靜脈與 **tibial nerve（脛神經）** 通過踝隧道進入足底。**Posterior tibial artery 的脈搏**可在**內踝與跟骨之間中點**、透過屈肌支持帶觸摸到。

> **臨床重點**：**Tibial nerve（脛神經）** 的外側是位於**talus（距骨）**後表面及**sustentaculum tali（載距突）**下表面的通道，供 **flexor hallucis longus（拇長屈肌）** 肌腱通過。

## Extensor Retinacula（伸肌支持帶）

兩個**extensor retinacula（伸肌支持帶）**將伸肌肌腱固定在踝關節區域，並在足部與腳趾伸展時防止肌腱彎曲（圖 6.106）：

- **Superior extensor retinaculum（上伸肌支持帶）**：是小腿遠端、踝關節正上方的深筋膜增厚，附著於**fibula（腓骨）**與**tibia（脛骨）**的前緣。
- **Inferior extensor retinaculum（下伸肌支持帶）**：呈**Y 形**，以其基底部附著於**calcaneus（跟骨）**上表面的外側，越過足部內側，一支附著於**medial malleolus（內踝）**，另一支繞過足部內側並附著於**plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）**的內側。

> **臨床重點**：**Extensor digitorum longus（趾長伸肌）** 與 **fibularis tertius（第三腓骨肌）** 的肌腱通過足部外側近端的通道。這些肌腱的內側是 **dorsalis pedis artery（足背動脈）**（anterior tibial artery 的終支）、**extensor hallucis longus（拇長伸肌）** 肌腱，以及最後通過伸肌支持帶下方的 **tibialis anterior（脛骨前肌）** 肌腱。

---

## Fibular Retinacula（腓骨支持帶）

**Fibular（peroneal）retinacula（腓骨支持帶）**將 **fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）** 與 **fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）** 的肌腱固定於足部外側（圖 6.107）：

- **Superior fibular retinaculum（腓骨上支持帶）**：在**lateral malleolus（外踝）**與**calcaneus（跟骨）**之間延伸。
- **Inferior fibular retinaculum（腓骨下支持帶）**：附著於**calcaneus（跟骨）** 围绕 **fibular trochlea（腓骨滑車）**的外側面，在上方與**inferior extensor retinaculum（下伸肌支持帶）**的纖維交織。

在**fibular trochlea（腓骨滑車）**處，一個隔膜將上方 **fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）** 肌腱的通道與下方 **fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）** 的通道分開。

## Arches of the Foot（足的弓形結構）

足的骨頭並非位於水平面上。相反，它們相對於地面形成**縱弓（longitudinal arch）**與**橫弓（transverse arch）**（圖 6.108），在站立與不同表面上行走時吸收並分散來自身體的向下力量。

### Longitudinal Arch（縱弓）

**縱弓**由**calcaneus（跟骨）**的後端至**metatarsals（蹠骨）**的頭部形成（圖 6.108A）。它在內側最高，形成**內側縱弓（medial longitudinal arch）**，在外側最低，形成**外側縱弓（lateral longitudinal arch）**。

### Transverse Arch（橫弓）

**橫弓**在冠狀面上最高的平面通過**talus（距骨）**的頭部，在**metatarsals（蹠骨）**頭部附近消失，這些骨頭在此由**deep transverse metatarsal ligaments（深側蹠橫韌帶）**固定在一起（圖 6.108B）。

### Ligament and Muscle Support（韌帶與肌肉支撐）

韌帶與肌肉支撐足的弓形結構（圖 6.109）：

> **臨床重點**：支撐弓形結構的韌帶包括 **plantar calcaneonavicular ligament（跟舟蹠側韌帶，又稱彈簧韌帶）**、**plantar calcaneocuboid ligament（跟骰蹠側韌帶，又稱短蹠韌帶）**、**long plantar ligament（長蹠韌帶）** 以及 **plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）**。

動態支撐弓形結構的肌肉包括在行走時的 **tibialis anterior（脛骨前肌）**、**tibialis posterior（脛骨後肌）** 與 **fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）**。

## Plantar Aponeurosis（足底腱膜）

**Plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）** 是足底深筋膜的增厚部分（圖 6.110）。它牢固地錨定於**calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）**的內側突，向前延伸為縱向排列的粗厚結締組織纖維帶。這些纖維在向前通過時分散，並形成**digital bands（趾帶）**，進入腳趾並連接骨頭、韌帶與皮膚真皮層。

在**metatarsophalangeal joints（蹠趾關節）**的遠端，**足底腱膜**的趾帶由橫向纖維相互連接，形成**superficial transverse metatarsal ligaments（淺層蹠橫韌帶）**。

> **臨床重點**：**Plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）**支撐足的縱弓，並保護足底深層結構。

---

## Fibrous Sheaths of Toes（趾的纖維鞘）

**Flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**、**flexor digitorum brevis（趾短屈肌）** 與 **flexor hallucis longus（拇長屈肌）** 的肌腱進入腳趾蹠側的**纖維性趾鞘（fibrous digital sheaths/tunnels）**（圖 6.111）。這些纖維鞘始於**metatarsophalangeal joints（蹠趾關節）**前方，延伸至**遠節趾骨（distal phalanges）**。它們由纖維拱與**cruciate ligaments（十字韌帶）**形成，附著於趾骨的邊緣以及與蹠趾關節和指節關節相關的蹠側韌帶。

> **臨床重點**：這些纖維隧道將肌腱固定於骨平面，並在腳趾屈曲時防止肌腱彎曲。每個隧道內，肌腱被**滑膜鞘（synovial sheath）**包圍。

---

## Extensor Hoods（伸肌罩）

**Extensor digitorum longus（趾長伸肌）**、**extensor digitorum brevis（趾短伸肌）** 與 **extensor hallucis longus（拇長伸肌）** 的肌腱進入腳趾背側，形成複雜的**背側趾擴張部（dorsal digital expansions，又稱伸肌罩）**（圖 6.112）。

每個伸肌罩呈三角形：
- **Apex（頂點）**附著於**遠節趾骨（distal phalanx）**
- **中央區域**附著於**中節趾骨（middle phalanx）**（趾 II 至 V）或**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**（趾 I）
- **Base（底部）**的每個角繞過**metatarsophalangeal joint（蹠趾關節）**的兩側。這些角的附著主要至**deep transverse metatarsal ligaments（深側蹠橫韌帶）**

許多**足的固有肌（intrinsic muscles of the foot）**附著於每個罩的遊離緣。這些肌肉附著於伸肌罩，使這些肌肉的力量能夠分佈到腳趾上，引起**metatarsophalangeal joint（蹠趾關節）**的屈曲，同時伸展**interphalangeal joints（指節關節）**（圖 6.112）。

> **臨床重點**：這些動作的功能在足部尚不確定，但它們可能防止在行走時抬起足跟、腳趾抓住地面時**metatarsophalangeal joints（蹠趾關節）**的過度伸展以及**interphalangeal joints（指節關節）**的屈曲。

---

## Intrinsic Muscles of the Foot（足的固有肌）

足的**固有肌（intrinsic muscles）**起源並附著於足部：

- **背側**：**extensor digitorum brevis（趾短伸肌）** 與 **extensor hallucis brevis（拇短伸肌）**
- **蹠側（足底）**：所有其他固有肌——**dorsal interossei（背側骨間肌）**、**plantar interossei（蹠側骨間肌）**、**flexor digiti minimi brevis（小趾短屈肌）**、**flexor hallucis brevis（拇短屈肌）**、**flexor digitorum brevis（趾短屈肌）**、**quadratus plantae（蹠方肌）**、**abductor digiti minimi（小趾展肌）**、**abductor hallucis（拇展肌）** 與 **lumbricals（蚓狀肌）**——位於足底的蹠側，在此組織成**四層**。

固有肌主要調節長肌腱的動作，並產生腳趾的細微動作。

> **臨床重點**：所有足的固有肌都由 **tibial nerve（脛神經）** 的**內側蹠神經（medial plantar nerve）**與**外側蹠神經（lateral plantar nerve）**分支支配，但 **extensor digitorum brevis（趾短伸肌）** 由 **deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）**支配。**第一背側骨間肌**也可能接受部分來自 **deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）** 的神經支配。

## Dorsal Aspect — Extensor Digitorum Brevis and Extensor Hallucis Brevis（背側——趾短伸肌與拇短伸肌）

### Extensor Digitorum Brevis（趾短伸肌）

**Extensor digitorum brevis（趾短伸肌）**附著於**calcaneus（跟骨）**上外側表面、**tarsal sinus（跗骨竇）**外側的粗糙區域（圖 6.113 與表 6.10）。

扁平的肌腹向前內側越過足部，位於**extensor digitorum longus（趾長伸肌）**肌腱的深層，形成三條肌腱，進入趾 II、III 和 IV。這些肌腱連接至**extensor digitorum longus（趾長伸肌）**肌腱的外側。**Extensor digitorum brevis** 通過對長伸肌肌腱與伸肌罩的附著而伸展中間三趾。它由 **deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）**支配。

### Extensor Hallucis Brevis（拇短伸肌）

**Extensor hallucis brevis（拇短伸肌）**與 **extensor digitorum brevis（趾短伸肌）**一起起始。其肌腱附著於大腳趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**的底部。該肌肉伸展大腳趾的**metatarsophalangeal joint（蹠趾關節）**，由 **deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）**支配。

| **Extensor hallucis brevis** | Calcaneus（跟骨）的上外側表面 | 大腳趾近節趾骨底部 | Deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）（S1, S2） | 伸展大腳趾蹠趾關節 |

## In the Sole — Four Layers of Muscles（足底——肌肉的四層結構）

足底的肌肉組織成**四層**。從淺至深（即從蹠側至背側），這些層次為**第一層、第二層、第三層與第四層**。

---

## First Layer（第一層）

第一層肌肉有三個組成部分，是四層中最淺的，位於**plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）**的正深層（圖 6.114 與表 6.11）。從內側至外側，這些肌肉是 **abductor hallucis（拇展肌）**、**flexor digitorum brevis（趾短屈肌）** 與 **abductor digiti minimi（小趾展肌）**。

### Abductor Hallucis（拇展肌）

**Abductor hallucis（拇展肌）**形成足的內側緣，並有助於足底內側的軟組織隆起（圖 6.114）。它起源於**calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）**的內側突以及**flexor retinaculum（屈肌支持帶）**與**plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）**的相鄰邊緣。它形成一條肌腱，附著於大腳趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**底部的內側以及與 **flexor hallucis brevis（拇短屈肌）** 肌腱相關的**內側籽骨（medial sesamoid bone）**。

**Abductor hallucis** 在**metatarsophalangeal joint（蹠趾關節）**處外展並屈曲大腳趾，由 **tibial nerve（脛神經）** 的**內側蹠神經（medial plantar nerve）**支配。

### Flexor Digitorum Brevis（趾短屈肌）

**Flexor digitorum brevis（趾短屈肌）**位於**plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）**的正上方與足底**flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**肌腱的下方（圖 6.114）。紡錘形的扁平肌腹以肌腱形式起源於**calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）**的內側突以及相鄰的**plantar aponeurosis（足底腱膜）**。

**Flexor digitorum brevis** 的肌纖維向前匯聚形成四條肌腱，每條進入一個外側四趾。在腳趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**底部附近，每條肌腱分叉，繞過**flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**肌腱的兩側，附著於**中節趾骨（middle phalanx）**的邊緣。

**Flexor digitorum brevis** 在**proximal interphalangeal joints（近側指節關節）**處屈曲外側四趾，由 **tibial nerve（脛神經）** 的**內側蹠神經（medial plantar nerve）**支配。

### Abductor Digiti Minimi（小趾展肌）

**Abductor digiti minimi（小趾展肌）**位於足的外側，形成足底外側的大隆起（圖 6.114）。它有廣闊的起源基部，主要來自**calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）**的外側與內側突，以及連接**calcaneus（跟骨）**與**metatarsal V（第五蹠骨）**底部的結締組織帶。

**Abductor digiti minimi** 形成一條肌腱，在**metatarsal V（第五蹠骨）**底部蹠側的淺溝中行走，繼續向前附著於小腳趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**底部的外側。

**Abductor digiti minimi** 在**metatarsophalangeal joint（蹠趾關節）**處外展小腳趾，由 **tibial nerve（脛神經）** 的**外側蹠神經（lateral plantar nerve）**支配。

### 表 6.11 — 足底第一層肌肉

| 肌肉 | 起源 | 附著 | 神經支配 | 功能 |
|------|------|------|----------|------|
| **Abductor hallucis** | Calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）的內側突 | 大腳趾近節趾骨底部的內側 | Medial plantar nerve（內側蹠神經），來自 tibial nerve（S1, S2, S3） | 在蹠趾關節處外展並屈曲大腳趾 |

## Second Layer（第二層）

足底第二肌肉層與通過該層的**flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**肌腱相關，由**quadratus plantae（蹠方肌）**與四條**lumbricals（蚓狀肌）**組成（圖 6.115 與表 6.12）。

### Quadratus Plantae（蹠方肌）

**Quadratus plantae（蹠方肌）**是一塊扁平的四方形的肌肉，有兩個起源頭（圖 6.115）：

- 其中一個頭起源於**sustentaculum tali（載距突）**下方的**calcaneus（跟骨）**內側面。
- 另一個頭起源於**calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）**外側突的前方以及**long plantar ligament（長蹠韌帶）**附著處的**calcaneus（跟骨）**下面。

**Quadratus plantae** 插入於足底近端一半處、**flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**肌腱分叉附近的**該肌腱外側**。

**Quadratus plantae** 協助 **flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**肌腱屈曲腳趾，並可調整該肌腱從內側進入足底時的「拉力線」。該肌肉由**外側蹠神經（lateral plantar nerve）**支配。

### Lumbricals（蚓狀肌）

**Lumbricals（蚓狀肌）**是四條蟲狀肌肉，起源於**flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**的肌腱，向背側行走，附著於外側四趾伸肌罩的遊離內側緣（圖 6.115）。

第一條蚓狀肌起源於與第二趾相關的**flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**肌腱的內側。其餘三條肌肉為**羽狀（bipennate）**，起源於相鄰肌腱的兩側。

蚓狀肌通過伸肌罩作用，抵抗行走時抬起足跟時**metatarsophalangeal joints（蹠趾關節）**的過度伸展以及**interphalangeal joints（指節關節）**的屈曲。

第一條蚓狀肌由**內側蹠神經（medial plantar nerve）**支配，其餘三條由**外側蹠神經（lateral plantar nerve）**支配。

### 表 6.12 — 足底第二層肌肉

| 肌肉 | 起源 | 附著 | 神經支配 | 功能 |
|------|------|------|----------|------|
| **Quadratus plantae** | Calcaneus（跟骨）的內側面以及 calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）的外側突 | 足底近端 **flexor digitorum longus（趾長屈肌）**肌腱的外側 | Lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經），來自 tibial nerve（S1, S2, S3） | 協助 flexor digitorum longus 肌腱屈曲趾 II 至 V |
| **Lumbricals** | 第一條：與趾 II 相關的 flexor digitorum longus 肌腱的內側；第二、三、四條：相鄰的 flexor digitorum longus 肌腱的相鄰表面 | 趾 II 至 V 伸肌罩的遊離內側緣 | 第一條：medial plantar nerve（內側蹠神經）；第二、三、四條：lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）（S2, S3） | 蹠趾關節屈曲與指節關節伸展 |

## Third Layer（第三層）

足底第三層有三塊肌肉（圖 6.116 與表 6.13）：

### Adductor Hallucis（拇收肌）

**Adductor hallucis（拇收肌）**呈三角形，有兩個頭：
- **Oblique head（斜頭）**：起源於 **fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）**肌腱的底部、鄰近的 **fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）**肌腱，以及**cuboid（骰骨）**的下表面。
- **Transverse head（橫頭）**：起源於**deep transverse metatarsal ligaments（深側蹠橫韌帶）**以及外側三趾**metatarsophalangeal joints（蹠趾關節）**的蹠側韌帶。

兩個頭會合形成一個肌腱，附著於大腳趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**底部的外側以及**外側籽骨（lateral sesamoid bone）**。

**Adductor hallucis** 內收並協助屈曲大腳趾的蹠趾關節，由**深支（deep branch）**與**淺支（superficial branch）**的**lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）**支配。

### Flexor Hallucis Brevis（拇短屈肌）

**Flexor hallucis brevis（拇短屈肌）**位於第一蹠骨的蹠側，分為兩個頭：
- **Medial head（內側頭）**：起源於**cuneiform bones（楔骨）**以及**tibialis posterior（脛骨後肌）**的延續部分。
- **Lateral head（外側頭）**：起源於**cuboid（骰骨）**與**lateral cuneiform（外側楔骨）**的鄰近表面。

兩個頭的肌腱包含籽骨，並附著於大腳趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**底部的兩側。內側腱與**abductor hallucis（拇展肌）**相連，外側腱與**adductor hallucis（拇收肌）**相連。

**Flexor hallucis brevis** 屈曲大腳趾的蹠趾關節，由**medial plantar nerve（內側蹠神經）**支配。

### Flexor Digiti Minimi Brevis（小趾短屈肌）

**Flexor digiti minimi brevis（小趾短屈肌）**起源於**fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）**與**fibularis brevis（腓骨短肌）**肌腱管道的底部，以及**metatarsal V（第五蹠骨）**的基底部。它的肌腱與**abductor digiti minimi（小趾展肌）**的肌腱相結合，附著於小腳趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**的底部。

**Flexor digiti minimi brevis** 屈曲小腳趾的蹠趾關節，由**lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）**支配。

### 表 6.13 — 足底第三層肌肉

| 肌肉 | 起源 | 附著 | 神經支配 | 功能 |
|------|------|------|----------|------|
| **Adductor hallucis** | 斜頭：fibularis longus 肌腱底部、fibularis brevis 肌腱及 cuboid 下表面；橫頭：深側蹠橫韌帶及外側三趾蹠趾關節的蹠側韌帶 | 大腳趾近節趾骨底部外側及外側籽骨 | Lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）的深支與淺支（S2, S3） | 內收並協助屈曲大腳趾蹠趾關節 |
| **Flexor hallucis brevis** | 內側頭：楔骨及 tibialis posterior 延續；外側頭：骰骨及外側楔骨 | 大腳趾近節趾骨底部兩側（經由籽骨） | Medial plantar nerve（內側蹠神經）（S2, S3） | 屈曲大腳趾蹠趾關節 |
| **Flexor digiti minimi brevis** | Fibularis longus 與 fibularis brevis 肌腱管道底部及 metatarsal V 底部 | 小腳趾近節趾骨底部（與 abductor digiti minimi 肌腱會合） | Lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）（S2, S3） | 屈曲小腳趾蹠趾關節 |

## Fourth Layer（第四層）

足底第四層由**足底骨間肌（plantar interossei）**與**背側骨間肌（dorsal interossei）**組成（圖 6.117）。

### Plantar Interossei（蹠側骨間肌）

三塊**蹠側骨間肌**位於外側三趾的相鄰蹠骨之間。每一塊肌肉起源於相關**蹠骨（metatarsal）**的內側或全部表面，並附著於同趾**近節趾骨（proximal phalanx）**底部的內側或外側，以及伸肌罩的相應角。

**蹠側骨間肌**的作用是內收相應的腳趾（相對於中線而言），由**lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）**支配。

### Dorsal Interossei（背側骨間肌）

四塊**背側骨間肌**呈羽狀，填充於蹠骨之間的間隙。每一塊肌肉有兩個頭，起源於相鄰蹠骨的相對表面。

背側骨間肌的附著如下：
- 前三塊附著於：
 - 趾 II 的近節趾骨底部（兩側）與伸肌罩的兩角——因此趾 II 可以內收或外展
 - 趾 III 的近節趾骨底部外側與伸肌罩的外側角
 - 趾 IV 的近節趾骨底部外側與伸肌罩的外側角
- 第四塊附著於趾 V 的近節趾骨底部外側與伸肌罩的外側角

**背側骨間肌**的作用是外展相應的腳趾（相對於中線而言），由**lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）**支配（第一、第二背側骨間肌也可能接受**deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）**的部分神經支配）。

| 肌肉 | 起源 | 附著 | 神經支配 | 功能 |
|------|------|------|----------|------|
| **Plantar interossei（蹠側骨間肌）**（三塊） | 外側三趾蹠骨的內側面或全部表面 | 同趾近節趾骨底部內側或外側，以及伸肌罩的相應角 | Lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）（S2, S3） | 內收相應腳趾 |

## Dorsal Venous Arch（背側靜脈弓）與足部靜脈

**背側靜脈弓（dorsal venous arch）**位於足背，收集足部背側的血液，並引流至**大隱靜脈（great saphenous vein）**（經由足內側）與**小隱靜脈（small saphenous vein）**（經由足外側）。

---

## Arteries（動脈）

足部的血液供應來自：
- **Dorsalis pedis artery（足背動脈）**——anterior tibial artery 的終支
- **Posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）**——通過踝隧道
- **Fibular artery（腓骨動脈）**——tibial artery 的分支

### Dorsalis Pedis Artery（足背動脈）

**Dorsalis pedis artery（足背動脈）**是**anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）**的延續，在踝關節前方下降，越過**talus（距骨）**、**navicular（舟骨）**與**medial cuneiform（內側楔骨）**的背面，到達足底內側。它提供分支至足背與鄰近結構。

### Lateral Plantar Artery（外側蹠動脈）與 Medial Plantar Artery（內側蹠動脈）

**Posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）**在踝隧道內分為：
- **Lateral plantar artery（外側蹠動脈）**——較大，穿過足底
- **Medial plantar artery（內側蹠動脈）**——較小

---

## Nerves（神經）

足部由以下神經支配：
- **Tibial nerve（脛神經）**的**內側蹠神經（medial plantar nerve）**與**外側蹠神經（lateral plantar nerve）**
- **Superficial fibular nerve（腓淺神經）**——足背
- **Deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）**——第一趾間隙

### Medial Plantar Nerve（內側蹠神經）

**內側蹠神經**是**tibial nerve（脛神經）**的直接延續，進入足底內側。它支配：
- **Abductor hallucis（拇展肌）**
- **Flexor digitorum brevis（趾短屈肌）**
- **Flexor hallucis brevis（拇短屈肌）**
- **第一蚓狀肌（first lumbrical）**

> **臨床重點**：內側蹠神經支配區域的感覺喪失表明**tibial nerve（脛神經）**在踝部或更近端受壓。

### Lateral Plantar Nerve（外側蹠神經）

**外側蹠神經**是**tibial nerve（脛神經）**的分支，穿過足底，支配：
- **Abductor digiti minimi（小趾展肌）**
- **Flexor digiti minimi brevis（小趾短屈肌）**
- **Quadratus plantae（蹠方肌）**
- **蹠側骨間肌（plantar interossei）**
- **背側骨間肌（dorsal interossei）**（部分）
- **第二、第三、第四蚓狀肌**

### Superficial Fibular Nerve（腓淺神經）

**腓淺神經**支配足背大部分的感覺。

### Deep Fibular Nerve（腓深神經）

**腓深神經**支配：
- **Extensor digitorum brevis（趾短伸肌）**
- **Extensor hallucis brevis（拇短伸肌）**
- **第一、第二背側骨間肌**（部分）

> **臨床重點**：**Deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）**在**tarsal tunnel（踝隧道）**受壓可導致**伸肌無力（extensor weakness）**，表現為**垂足（foot drop）**。

---

## Lymphatic Drainage（淋巴引流）

足部的淺層淋巴引流遵循淺層靜脈，而深層淋巴引流伴隨主要動脈。兩者最終引流至**腹股溝淋巴結（inguinal lymph nodes）**。

---

## Surface Anatomy（表面解剖）

- **Medial malleolus（內踝）**與**sustentaculum tali（載距突）**可在踝關節內側觸及
- **Lateral malleolus（外踝）**可在踝關節外側觸及
- **Calcaneal tuberosity（跟骨結節）**可在足跟觸及
- **Navicular tuberosity（舟骨結節）**可在足內側觸及
- **Dorsalis pedis artery（足背動脈）**的脈搏可在第一趾間隙近端觸及
- **Posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）**的脈搏可在內踝與跟骨之間中點觸及

> **臨床重點**：在評估外周血管疾病時，應觸摸**足背動脈（dorsalis pedis artery）**與**脛後動脈（posterior tibial artery）**的脈搏。兩者的減弱或缺失表明下肢血液供應受損。

## 第三層肌肉（續）

### Flexor Hallucis Brevis（拇趾短屈肌）

**Flexor hallucis brevis** 肌肉有兩個腱性起點（圖 6.116）：

- **Lateral head（外側頭）**：起源於 **cuboid（骰骨）** 表面，**fibularis longus（腓骨長肌）** 溝的後方，以及 **lateral cuneiform（外側楔骨）** 的相鄰表面。
- **Medial head（內側頭）**：起源於 **tibialis posterior muscle（脛後肌）** 的肌腱，當其進入足底時。

內側頭與外側頭會合並產生肌腹，在接近 **metatarsal I（第一蹠骨）** 的足底表面處分為內側部和外側部。肌肉的每一部分都產生一條肌腱，插入到拇趾近端指骨的基部外側或內側。

在 **flexor hallucis brevis** 的每一條肌腱跨越 **metatarsal I（第一蹠骨）** 頭部的足底表面時，會出現一塊 **sesamoid bone（籽骨）**。**Flexor hallucis longus（拇趾長屈肌）** 的肌腱在兩塊籽骨之間通過。

**Flexor hallucis brevis** 可屈曲拇趾的 **metatarsophalangeal joint（蹠趾關節）**，由 **medial plantar nerve（內側蹠神經）** 支配。

### Adductor Hallucis（拇趾內收肌）

**Adductor hallucis** 肌肉通過兩個肌頭（transverse 橫頭和 oblique 斜頭）起源，在接近末端時會合，插入拇趾近端指骨基部的外側（圖 6.116）：

- **Transverse head（橫頭）**：起源於與外側三趾蹠趾關節相關的 **plantar ligaments（足底韌帶）**，以及相關的 **deep transverse metatarsal ligaments（深橫蹠韌帶）**——肌肉從外側到內側橫向穿過足底，在拇趾基部附近與斜頭會合。
- **Oblique head（斜頭）**：比橫頭大，起源於 **metatarsals II to IV（第二至第四蹠骨）** 基部的足底表面，以及覆蓋 **fibularis longus muscle（腓骨長肌）** 的鞘——此頭從後外側向前內側穿過足底，與橫頭會合。

**Adductor hallucis** 的插入腱附著於與 **flexor hallucis brevis** 肌腱相關的 **lateral sesamoid bone（外側籽骨）**，以及近端指骨。

**Adductor hallucis** 可在蹠趾關節處內收拇趾，由 **lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）** 支配。

### Flexor Digiti Minimi Brevis（小趾短屈肌）

**Flexor digiti minimi brevis** 肌肉起源於 **metatarsal V（第五蹠骨）** 基部的足底表面和 **fibularis longus tendon（腓骨長肌腱）** 的相鄰鞘（圖 6.116）。它插入小趾近端指骨基部的外側。

**Flexor digiti minimi brevis** 可屈曲小趾的蹠趾關節，由 **lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）** 支配。

## 第四層肌肉

足底最深層肌肉層有兩組肌肉：**dorsal interossei（骨間背側肌）** 和 **plantar interossei（骨間足底肌）**（圖 6.117 和表 6.14）。

### Dorsal Interossei（骨間背側肌）

四塊 **dorsal interossei** 是足底最上層的肌肉，相對於通過第二趾長軸的外側，可外展第二至第四趾（圖 6.117）。所有四塊肌肉都是雙羽狀，起源於相鄰蹠骨的側面。

**Dorsal interossei** 的肌腱插入趾伸肌罩膜的遊離緣和近端指骨的基部。

第二趾可以沿其長軸的任一側外展，因此有兩塊 **dorsal interossei** 與其相關，每側一塊。第三和第四趾在其外側只有一塊 **dorsal interosseous muscle（骨間背側肌）**。拇趾和小趾有各自的展肌（分別是 **abductor hallucis** 和 **abductor digiti minimi**），位於足底肌肉的第一層。

除了外展，**dorsal interossei** 還通過伸肌罩膜作用，抵抗蹠趾關節的伸展和趾間關節的屈曲。

**Dorsal interossei** 由 **lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）** 支配。第一和第二骨間背側肌還接受來自 **deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）** 其上表面的分支。

### Plantar Interossei（骨間足底肌）

三塊 **plantar interossei** 可將第三、第四和小趾內收朝向通過第二趾的長軸（圖 6.117）。

每塊 **plantar interosseous muscle（骨間足底肌）** 起源於其相關蹠骨的內側側，插入伸肌罩膜的內側遊離緣和近端指骨的基部。

拇趾有自己的內收肌（**adductor hallucis**），位於足底肌肉的第三層；第二趾通過使用其一块 **dorsal interossei** 內收回其縱軸。

除了內收，**plantar interossei** 還通過伸肌罩膜作用，抵抗蹠趾關節的伸展和趾間關節的屈曲。所有都由 **lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）** 支配。

## 足部 arteries（arteries）

足部的血液供應來自 **posterior tibial artery（脛後動脈）** 和 **dorsalis pedis artery（足背動脈）** 的分支。

**Posterior tibial artery** 進入足底，分叉為 **lateral plantar artery（外側蹠動脈）** 和 **medial plantar artery（內側蹠動脈）**。**Lateral plantar artery** 與 **dorsalis pedis artery**（足背动脉）的終末端（**deep plantar artery（足底深弓）**）會合形成 **deep plantar arch（足底深弓）**。來自此弓的分支供應趾部。

**Dorsalis pedis artery** 是 **anterior tibial artery（脛前動脈）** 的延續，越過足部背面，然後作為 **deep plantar artery** 在第一和第二蹠骨之間下行進入足底。

### Posterior Tibial Artery 與 Plantar Arch

**Posterior tibial artery** 穿過踝關節內側的 **tarsal tunnel（跗管）** 進入足部，位於 **medial malleolus（內踝）** 的後方。在內踝和足跟之間的中點處，可以觸摸到 **posterior tibial artery** 的脈搏，因為此處动脉只覆蓋著一層薄薄的 **retinaculum（支持帶）**、淺層結締組織和皮膚。在此位置附近，**posterior tibial artery** 分叉為一支較小的 **medial plantar artery** 和一支較大的 **lateral plantar artery**。

#### Lateral Plantar Artery（外側蹠動脈）

**Lateral plantar artery** 向前外側進入足底，首先位於 **abductor hallucis muscle（拇趾展肌）** 近端的深處，然後在 **quadratus plantae（蹠方肌）** 和 **flexor digitorum brevis（趾短屈肌）** 之間穿行（圖 6.118）。它到達 **metatarsal V（第五蹠骨）** 的基部，位於 **flexor digitorum brevis** 和 **abductor digiti minimi（小趾展肌）** 之間的溝中。從這裡，**lateral plantar artery** 向內側彎曲形成 **deep plantar arch**，穿過蹠骨基部和骨間肌的深層平面。

在第一和第二蹠骨基部之間，**deep plantar arch** 與來自足背的 **dorsalis pedis artery** 的終支（**deep plantar artery**）會合。

**Deep plantar arch** 的主要分支包括：
- 供應小趾外側的趾支
- 四條 **plantar metatarsal arteries（蹠總動脈）**，供應趾 I-V 相鄰側的趾支和拇趾的內側
- 三條 **perforating arteries（穿支）**，穿過第二至第五蹠骨基部，與足背的血管吻合

#### Medial Plantar Artery（內側蹠動脈）

**Medial plantar artery** 通過 **abductor hallucis muscle** 近端深處進入足底（圖 6.118）。它供應一支深支到相鄰肌肉，然後在 **abductor hallucis** 和 **flexor digitorum brevis** 之間的溝中向前走行。它最終與來自 **deep plantar arch** 的趾支會合，供應拇趾的內側。

在第一蹠骨基部附近，**medial plantar artery** 發出一個淺支，分為三支，穿過 **flexor digitorum brevis** 淺層，與來自 **deep plantar arch** 的蹠總動脈連接。

### Dorsalis Pedis Artery（足背動脈）

**Dorsalis pedis artery** 是 **anterior tibial artery** 的延續，開始於 **anterior tibial artery** 越過踝關節處（圖 6.119）。它向前越過 **talus（距骨）**、**navicular（舟骨）** 和 **intermediate cuneiform（中間楔骨）** 的背面，然後作為 **deep plantar artery** 在第一塊 **dorsal interosseous muscle（骨間背側肌）** 的兩個頭之間向下越過，與足底的 **deep plantar arch** 會合。可以通過輕輕觸摸第二趾的 **extensor hallucis longus（拇趾長伸肌）** 和 **extensor digitorum longus（趾長伸肌）** 肌腱之間的蹠骨上方的底層骨，來感受足背的 **dorsalis pedis artery** 脈搏。

**Dorsalis pedis artery** 的分支包括 **lateral tarsal branches（外側跗骨支）**、**medial tarsal branches（內側跗骨支）**、**arcuate artery（弓狀動脈）** 和 **first dorsal metatarsal artery（第一蹠背動脈）**：

- **Tarsal arteries（跗骨动脉）**：內側和外側越過跗骨，供應相鄰結構，並與踝關節周圍形成的血管網吻合
- **Arcuate artery（弓狀动脉）**：越過蹠骨基部附近的外側，發出三條 **dorsal metatarsal arteries（蹠背动脉）**，供應趾 II-V 相鄰側的背側趾动脉，以及供應趾 V 外側的背側趾动脉
- **第一蹠背动脉**（足背动脉進入足底前的最後一個分支）供應拇趾和第二趾相鄰側的背側趾支

蹠背动脉與來自 **deep plantar arch** 的穿支和來自蹠總动脉的類似分支連接。

## 足部 Veins（靜脈）

足部有深層和淺層相互連接的靜脈網。深層靜脈伴隨动脉行走。淺層靜脈引流到蹠骨背面足背的 **dorsal venous arch（背側靜脈弓）**（圖 6.120）：

- **Great saphenous vein（大隱靜脈）**：起源於靜脈弓的內側，在內踝前方越過並進入小腿的內側
- **Small saphenous vein（小隱靜脈）**：起源於靜脈弓的外側，在外踝後方越過並進入小腿的後側

## 足部 Nerves（神經）

足部由 **tibial nerve（脛神經）**、**deep fibular nerve（腓深神經）**、**superficial fibular nerve（腓淺神經）**、**sural nerve（腓腸神經）** 和 **saphenous nerve（隱神經）** 支配：

- 所有五條神經都參與皮質或一般感覺神經支配
- **Tibial nerve** 支配足部所有固有肌肉，除了由 **deep fibular nerve** 支配的 **extensor digitorum brevis（趾短伸肌）**
- **Deep fibular nerve** 通常也參與第一和第二骨間背側肌的神經支配

### Tibial Nerve（脛神經）

**Tibial nerve** 穿過位於踝關節內側的 **tarsal tunnel（跗管）** 進入足部，位於 **medial malleolus（內踝）** 的後方。在隧道中，神經位於 **posterior tibial artery** 的外側，發出穿透 **flexor retinaculum（屈肌支持帶）** 供應足跟的 **medial calcaneal branches（內側足跟支）**。在內踝和足跟之間的中點處，**tibial nerve** 與 **posterior tibial artery** 分叉為：

- 一支較大的 **medial plantar nerve（內側蹠神經）**
- 一支較小的 **lateral plantar nerve（外側蹠神經）**（圖 6.121）

內側和外側蹠神經位於相應动脉之間。

#### Medial Plantar Nerve（內側蹠神經）

**Medial plantar nerve** 是足底主要的感覺神經（圖 6.121）。它支配足底前三分之二大部分區域和相鄰的內側三個半趾表面的皮膚，包括拇趾。除了大面積的足底皮膚外，神經還支配四塊固有肌肉——**abductor hallucis**、**flexor digitorum brevis**、**flexor hallucis brevis** 和第一塊 **lumbrical（蚓狀肌）**。

**Medial plantar nerve** 通過 **abductor hallucis muscle** 深處進入足底，然後在 **abductor hallucis** 和 **flexor digitorum brevis** 之間的溝中向前走行，供應兩塊肌肉的分支。

**Medial plantar nerve** 供應一條趾支（**proper plantar digital nerve**）到拇趾的內側，然後在 **flexor digitorum brevis** 的足底表面分為三條神經（**common plantar digital nerves**），繼續向前供應趾 I-IV 相鄰側的 **proper plantar digital branches**。到第一塊蚓狀肌的神經起源於第一條蹠總神經。

#### Lateral Plantar Nerve（外側蹠神經）

**Lateral plantar nerve** 是足部重要的運動神經，因為它支配足底幾乎所有固有肌肉，除了由內側蹠神經支配的肌肉（**abductor hallucis**、**flexor digitorum brevis**、**flexor hallucis brevis** 和第一塊蚓狀肌）（圖 6.121）。它還支配足底前三分之二外側條帶狀皮膚和相鄰的外側一個半趾的足底表面。

**Lateral plantar nerve** 通過 **abductor hallucis muscle** 近端附著處深處進入足底。它在足底向前外側走行，位於 **flexor digitorum brevis** 和 **quadratus plantae** 之間，供應兩塊肌肉的分支，然後在 **metatarsal V（第五蹠骨）** 頭部附近分為深支和淺支。

**Lateral plantar nerve** 的淺支產生一條到小趾外側皮膚的 **proper plantar digital nerve**，以及一條 **common plantar digital nerve**，分為供應趾 IV 和 V 相鄰側皮膚的 **proper plantar digital nerves**。

到小趾外側的 **proper plantar digital nerve** 也支配 **flexor digiti minimi brevis** 和介於第四和第五蹠骨之間的背側和足底骨間肌。

**Lateral plantar nerve** 的深支是運動支，伴隨 **lateral plantar artery** 在長屈肌腱和 **adductor hallucis** 深處走行。它供應第二至第四塊蚓狀肌、**adductor hallucis** 和除第四和第五蹠骨之間的骨間肌（由淺支支配）外的所有骨間肌。

> **臨床重點**：**Morton's Neuroma（莫頓神經瘤）**
>
> **Morton's neuroma** 是一種增粗的蹠總神經，通常位於第三和第四趾之間的第三蹠間隙。足部的這個區域，**lateral plantar nerve** 通常與 **medial plantar nerve** 會合。當兩條神經會合時，結果神經的直徑通常比其它趾的神經大。此外，它處於相對淺層的位置，就在足部脂肪墊上方，接近动脉和靜脈。神經上方是 **deep transverse metatarsal ligament（深橫蹠韌帶）**，這是一個寬闊堅固的結構，將蹠骨固定在一起。
>
> 通常，當患者進入行走「推進」階段時，趾間神經被夾在地面和 **deep transverse metatarsal ligament** 之間。這些力傾向於擠壓蹠總神經，可能引起刺激，通常伴隨有相關的炎症變化和增厚。
>
> 患者通常在第三蹠間隙感到疼痛，可能是尖銳或鈍痛，且通常因穿鞋和行走而加重。
>
> 治療可能包括注射抗炎藥物，或可能需要手術切除病變。

### Deep Fibular Nerve（腓深神經）

**Deep fibular nerve** 支配 **extensor digitorum brevis**，參與第一和第二骨間背側肌的神經支配，並供應第一和第二趾相鄰背側和它們之間的 web space（拇趾間隙）皮膚的一般感覺分支（圖 6.122）。

**Deep fibular nerve** 在足背位於 **dorsalis pedis artery** 的外側，與 **extensor hallucis longus** 肌腱平行且位於其外側。就在踝關節遠端，神經發出一條外側支，從深面支配 **extensor digitorum brevis**。

**Deep fibular nerve** 繼續在足背向前走行，在接近蹠趾關節處穿過第一和第二蹠骨之間的深筋膜，然後分為兩條 **dorsal digital nerves**，供應趾 I 和 II 相鄰表面向下到指甲床起始處的皮膚。

在穿過深筋膜之前，**deep fibular nerve** 發出的小運動分支參與第一和第二骨間背側肌的支配。

### Superficial Fibular Nerve（腓淺神經）

**Superficial fibular nerve** 是足背大部分皮膚和趾背的感覺神經，但不包括趾 I 和 II 相鄰側（由 **deep fibular nerve** 支配）和足和小趾外側（由 **sural nerve** 支配）的皮膚（圖 6.122）。

**Superficial fibular nerve** 在小腿前外側穿透深筋膜，進入足背的淺筋膜。它沿路發出皮質分支和背側趾神經。

### Sural Nerve（腓腸神經）

**Sural nerve** 是 **tibial nerve** 的皮質分支，起源於小腿高處。它在足跟後方靠近小隱靜脈的淺筋膜中進入足部。終支支配足和小趾背外側表面的皮膚（圖 6.122B）。

### Saphenous Nerve（隱神經）

**Saphenous nerve** 是起源於大腿的 **femoral nerve（股神經）** 的皮質分支。終支在踝關節內側的淺筋膜中進入足部，支配足近端內側的皮膚（圖 6.122B）。
# Surface Anatomy — Lower Limb

## 6.123 Avoiding the Sciatic Nerve

The **sciatic nerve** innervates muscles in the posterior compartment of the thigh, muscles in the leg and foot, and an appreciable area of skin. It enters the lower limb in the gluteal region and passes inferiorly midway between two major palpable bony landmarks, the **greater trochanter** and the **ischial tuberosity**.

- The **greater trochanter** can be easily felt as a hard bony protuberance about one hand's width inferior to the midpoint of the iliac crest.
- The **ischial tuberosity** is palpable just above the gluteal fold.

### Gluteal Quadrant Division

The gluteal region can be divided into quadrants by two lines positioned using palpable bony landmarks:

1. One line descends vertically from the highest point of the **iliac crest**.
2. The other line passes horizontally through the first line midway between the highest point of the iliac crest and the horizontal plane through the **ischial tuberosity**.

> **臨床重點**：The sciatic nerve curves through the upper lateral corner of the lower medial quadrant and descends along the lateral margin of the lower medial quadrant. Injections can be carried out in the anterior corner of the upper lateral quadrant to avoid injury to the sciatic nerve and major vessels in the region.

---

## 6.124 Finding the Femoral Artery in the Femoral Triangle

The **femoral artery** passes into the **femoral triangle** of the lower limb from the abdomen.

The femoral triangle is the depression formed in the anterior thigh between:

- The **medial margin of the adductor longus muscle**
- The **medial margin of the sartorius muscle**
- The **inguinal ligament**

### Key Palpable Structures

- The tendon of the **adductor longus muscle** can be palpated as a cord-like structure that attaches to bone immediately inferior to the **pubic tubercle**.
- The **sartorius muscle** originates from the **anterior superior iliac spine** and crosses anteriorly over the thigh to attach to the medial aspect of the tibia below the knee joint.
- The **inguinal ligament** attaches to the **anterior superior iliac spine** laterally and the **pubic tubercle** medially.

> **臨床重點**：The femoral artery descends into the thigh from the abdomen by passing under the inguinal ligament and into the femoral triangle. In the femoral triangle, its pulse is easily felt just inferior to the inguinal ligament midway between the pubic symphysis and the anterior superior iliac spine. Medial to the artery is the **femoral vein** and medial to the vein is the **femoral canal**, which contains lymphatics and lies immediately lateral to the pubic tubercle. The **femoral nerve** lies lateral to the femoral artery.

---

## 6.125 Identifying Structures around the Knee

The **patella** is a prominent palpable feature at the knee. The **quadriceps femoris tendon** attaches superiorly to it and the **patellar ligament** connects the inferior surface of the patella to the **tibial tuberosity**. The patellar ligament and the tibial tuberosity are easily palpable.

> **臨床重點**：A tap on the patellar ligament (tendon) tests reflex activity mainly at spinal cord levels **L3** and **L4**.

### Additional Knee Landmarks

- The **head of the fibula** is palpable as a protuberance on the lateral surface of the knee just inferior to the lateral condyle of the tibia. It can also be located by following the tendon of the **biceps femoris** inferiorly.

- The **common fibular nerve** passes around the lateral surface of the neck of the fibula just inferior to the head and can often be felt as a cord-like structure in this position.

- Another structure that can usually be located on the lateral side of the knee is the **iliotibial tract**. This flat tendinous structure, which attaches to the lateral tibial condyle, is most prominent when the knee is fully extended. In this position, the anterior edge of the tract raises a sharp vertical fold of skin posterior to the lateral edge of the patella.

---

## 6.126 Visualizing the Contents of the Popliteal Fossa

The **popliteal fossa** is a diamond-shaped depression formed between the **hamstrings** and **gastrocnemius muscle** posterior to the knee.

### Boundaries

- **Inferior margins**: medial and lateral heads of the **gastrocnemius muscle**
- **Superior margins**:
 - Laterally: **biceps femoris muscle**
 - Medially: **semitendinosus** and **semimembranosus muscles**

The tendons of the biceps femoris muscle and the semitendinosus muscle are palpable and often visible.

> **臨床重點**：The **popliteal fossa** contains the **popliteal artery**, the **popliteal vein**, the **tibial nerve**, and the **common fibular nerve**. The popliteal artery is the deepest of the structures in the fossa and descends through the region from the upper medial side. As a consequence of its position, the popliteal artery pulse is difficult to find, but usually can be detected on deep palpation just medial to the midline of the fossa.

The **head of the fibula** is palpable on the lateral side of the knee and can be used as a landmark for identifying the biceps femoris tendon and the common fibular nerve, which curves laterally out of the popliteal fossa and crosses the neck of the fibula just inferior to the head.

> **臨床重點**：The small saphenous vein penetrates deep fascia in the upper part of the posterior leg and joins the popliteal vein.

---

## 6.127 Finding the Tarsal Tunnel — The Gateway to the Foot

The **tarsal tunnel** is formed on the medial side of the foot in the groove between the **medial malleolus** and the **heel (calcaneal tuberosity)** and by the overlying **flexor retinaculum**.

### Structures Passing Through the Tarsal Tunnel

The **posterior tibial artery** and **tibial nerve** enter the foot through the tarsal tunnel. The tendons of the **tibialis posterior**, **flexor digitorum longus**, and **flexor hallucis longus** also pass through the tarsal tunnel in compartments formed by septa of the flexor retinaculum.

> **臨床重點**：The order of structures passing through the tunnel from anteromedial to posterolateral are the tendon of the tibialis posterior, the tendon of the flexor digitorum longus, the posterior tibial artery and associated veins, the tibial nerve, and the tendon of the flexor hallucis longus ("**Tom, Dick, and a very nervous Harry**").

The tibial artery is palpable just posteroinferior to the medial malleolus on the anterior face of the visible groove between the heel and medial malleolus.

---

## 6.128 Identifying Tendons around the Ankle and in the Foot

Numerous tendons can be identified around the ankle and in the foot and can be used as useful landmarks for locating vessels or testing spinal reflexes.

### Medial Side

- The **tibialis anterior tendon** is visible on the medial side of the ankle anterior to the medial malleolus.

### Posterior Aspect

- The **calcaneal tendon** (Achilles tendon) is the largest tendon entering the foot and is prominent on the posterior aspect of the foot as it descends from the leg to the heel.

> **臨床重點**：A tap with a tendon hammer on the calcaneal tendon tests reflex activity of spinal cord levels **S1** and **S2**.

### Lateral Side

- When the foot is everted, the tendons of the **fibularis longus** and **fibularis brevis** raise a linear fold of skin, which descends from the lower leg to the posterior edge of the lateral malleolus.
- The tendon of the **fibularis brevis** is often evident on the lateral surface of the foot descending obliquely to the base of **metatarsal V**.

### Dorsal Aspect

- The tendons of the **fibularis tertius**, **extensor digitorum longus**, and **extensor hallucis longus** are visible on the dorsal aspect of the foot from lateral to medial.

---

## 6.129 Finding the Dorsalis Pedis Artery

The nature of the **dorsalis pedis pulse** is important for assessing peripheral circulation because the dorsalis pedis artery is the farthest palpable vessel from the heart. Also, it is the lowest palpable artery in the body when a person is standing.

> **臨床重點**：The dorsalis pedis artery passes onto the dorsal aspect of the foot and anteriorly over the tarsal bones where it lies between and is parallel to the tendon of the **extensor hallucis longus** and the tendon of the **extensor digitorum longus** to the second toe. It is palpable in this position. The terminal branch of the dorsalis pedis artery passes into the plantar surface of the foot between the two heads of the **first dorsal interosseous muscle**.

---

## 6.130 Approximating the Position of the Plantar Arterial Arch

The blood supply of the foot is provided by branches of the **posterior tibial** and **dorsalis pedis arteries**.

- The **posterior tibial artery** enters the plantar surface of the foot through the tarsal tunnel and divides into a **lateral** and a **medial plantar artery**.
- The **lateral plantar artery** curves laterally across the posterior half of the sole and then curves medially as the **plantar arch** through the anterior sole.
- Between the bases of metatarsals I and II, the plantar arch joins the terminal branch (deep plantar artery) of the dorsalis pedis artery. Most of the foot is supplied by the plantar arch.
- The **medial plantar artery** passes anteriorly through the sole, connects with branches of the plantar arch, and supplies the medial side of the great toe.

---

## 6.131 Major Superficial Veins

**Superficial veins** in the lower limb often become enlarged. Also, because the veins are long, they can be removed and used elsewhere in the body as vascular grafts.

### Great Saphenous Vein

Superficial veins in the lower limb begin as a **dorsal venous arch** in the foot. The medial side of the arch curves superiorly anterior to the **medial malleolus** and passes up the leg and thigh as the **great saphenous vein**.

This vein passes through an aperture in the fascia lata (saphenous ring) to join with the **femoral vein** in the femoral triangle.

### Small Saphenous Vein

The lateral side of the dorsal venous arch in the foot passes posterior to the **lateral malleolus** and up the posterior surface of the leg as the **small saphenous vein**. This vessel passes through the deep fascia in the upper one-third of the leg and connects with the **popliteal vein** in the popliteal fossa posterior to the knee.

---

## 6.132 Pulse Points

**Peripheral pulses** can be felt at four locations in the lower limb:

| Location | Artery | Position |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Femoral triangle | **Femoral artery** | Inferior to the inguinal ligament, midway between the anterior superior iliac spine and the pubic symphysis |
| Popliteal fossa | **Popliteal artery** | Deep in the popliteal fossa near the midline |
| Tarsal tunnel | **Posterior tibial artery** | Posteroinferior to the medial malleolus in the groove between the medial malleolus and the heel |
| Dorsal foot | **Dorsalis pedis artery** | As it passes distally over the tarsal bones between the tendon of the extensor hallucis longus and the tendon of the extensor digitorum longus to the second toe |

## Clinical Cases

### Case 1: Varicose Veins

A young woman came to a vascular surgeon with a series of large dilated tortuous veins in her right leg. A diagnosis of **varicose veins** was made and the surgeon needed to determine the site of valvular incompetence.

> **臨床重點**：Typical points where incompetent valves occur between the superficial and the deep veins:
> - At the **saphena varix** — the **saphenofemoral junction** where the femoral vein is joined by the great saphenous vein
> - In the midthigh perforating vein between the great saphenous vein and the femoral vein
> - In the calf — the three sites where perforators occur, 5, 10, and 15 cm above the medial malleolus between the great saphenous vein and the deep veins of the calf
> - At the junction of the small saphenous vein and the popliteal vein

#### Tourniquet Test

The surgeon asked the patient to lie supine on the bed and elevated the leg. A tourniquet was placed around the upper thigh below the saphenofemoral junction and the patient was asked to stand up. No veins were demonstrated filling on the medial aspect of the thigh and lower limb.

> **臨床重點**：The effect of the tourniquet is to compress the great saphenous vein while permitting blood to flow in the deep venous system of the femoral vein and the deep femoral vein. Because there was no filling of the medial varicose veins below the level of the tourniquet, the surgeon assumed that the valve at the saphenofemoral junction was incompetent and would require surgical treatment.

During the tourniquet maneuver the surgeon also noted some veins around the posterior and posterolateral aspect of the calf. A similar technique was performed by application of a tourniquet just below the level of the knee joint while the leg was elevated. The patient stood up and no veins were demonstrated filling in the posterior and posterolateral aspect of the calf. These findings suggested that there was also incompetence of the valve for the small saphenous system where it anastomoses with the popliteal vein.

#### Surgical Complications

Surgery was planned. A small transverse incision was made below the level of the inguinal ligament where the great saphenous vein passes through the saphenous ring in the deep fascia. The saphenofemoral junction was identified and the great saphenous vein was ligated at its anastomosis with the femoral vein. The great saphenous vein was stripped and removed.

The patient was placed prone for the second part of the operation. A small incision was made transversely at the level of the skin crease in the popliteal fossa. However, the surgeon had difficulty identifying the junction between the small saphenous vein and the popliteal vein. After considerable time the surgeon located what he thought was the small saphenous vein and the structure was ligated and the wound closed.

**Two weeks later**, the patient returned complaining of problems walking. On examination there was absence of dorsiflexion, a sensory disturbance over the lateral aspect of the leg and foot, and obvious wasting of the fibular muscles. As the patient walked, the foot was dragged between steps. A clinical diagnosis of **footdrop** was made and a **common fibular nerve injury** was diagnosed.

> **臨床重點**：Within the popliteal fossa are the popliteal artery, popliteal vein, and sciatic nerve (and its divisions). The popliteal artery is the deepest structure. The popliteal vein is superficial to the artery and the sciatic nerve is superficial to the vein. Importantly, the sciatic nerve divides at the apex of the popliteal fossa. The tibial nerve continues into the lower popliteal fossa. The **common fibular nerve** passes laterally adjacent to the biceps femoris muscle to become superficial and wrap around the fibula neck.

It was concluded that the surgeon had accidentally ligated the common fibular nerve rather than the small saphenous vein, thus producing this patient's symptoms.

---

### Case 2: Knee Joint Injury

A young man was skiing and caught an inner edge of his right ski. He lost his balance and fell. During his tumble he heard an audible "click." After recovering from his spill, he developed tremendous pain in his right knee. He was unable to carry on skiing, and by the time he returned to his chalet, his knee was significantly swollen.

#### Mechanism of Injury

The man was skiing down the slope with both skis in parallel. The ankles were held rigid in the boots and the knees were slightly flexed. A momentary loss of concentration led to the skier catching the inner edge of his right ski. This effect was to force the boot and calf into **external rotation**. Furthermore, the knee was forced into a **valgus position** (bowed laterally away from the midline) and the skier tumbled.

#### Sequence of Structural Damage

As the knee went into external rotation and valgus:

1. The **anterior cruciate ligament** became taut, acting as a fulcrum
2. The **tibial collateral ligament** was stressed and the lateral compartment of the knee compressed
3. As the force increased, the **tibial collateral ligament was torn**
4. The **medial meniscus was torn**
5. Finally, the **anterior cruciate ligament** gave way

> **臨床重點**：Disruption of the anterior cruciate ligament characteristically produces marked joint swelling. The ligament is extrasynovial and intracapsular and has a rich blood supply. As the ligament was torn it ruptured into the joint. Blood from the tear irritates the synovial membrane and also enters the joint. These factors produce gradual swelling of the joint over the ensuing hours with significant fluid accumulation in the joint cavity.

#### Surgical Reconstruction

The patient had a surgical reconstruction of the anterior cruciate ligament. Two of the commonest methods use the **patellar ligament (tendon)** and **hamstrings** to reconstruct the ligament.

The patient had further surgical procedures. The tibial collateral ligament was explored and resutured. Using arthroscopic techniques, the tear in the medial meniscus was debrided to prevent further complications.
# Gray's Anatomy — Upper Limb (Chapter 7)
## Conceptual Overview

---

## General Description

The **upper limb** is associated with the lateral aspect of the lower portion of the neck and with the **thoracic wall**. It is suspended from the trunk by **muscles** and a small skeletal articulation between the clavicle and the sternum — the **sternoclavicular joint**. Based on the position of its major joints and component bones, the upper limb is divided into **shoulder**, **arm**, **forearm**, and **hand** (Fig. 7.1A).

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The shoulder is the area of upper limb attachment to the trunk. The **arm** is the part of the upper limb between the shoulder and the elbow joint; the **forearm** is between the elbow joint and the wrist joint; and the **hand** is distal to the wrist joint.

### Transitional Areas（過渡區域）

The **axilla**, **cubital fossa**, and **carpal tunnel** are significant areas of transition between the different parts of the limb. Important structures pass through, or are related to, each of these areas.

- **Axilla（腋窩）**: An irregularly shaped pyramidal area formed by muscles and bones of the shoulder and the lateral surface of the thoracic wall. The apex or inlet opens directly into the lower portion of the neck. The skin of the armpit forms the floor. All major structures that pass between the neck and arm pass through the axilla.

- **Cubital Fossa（肘窩）**: A triangularly shaped depression formed by muscles anterior to the elbow joint. The major artery, the **brachial artery**, passing from the arm to the forearm passes through this fossa, as does one of the major nerves of the upper limb, the **median nerve**.

- **Carpal Tunnel（腕隧道）**: The gateway to the palm of the hand. Its posterior, lateral, and medial walls form an arch, which is made up of small carpal bones in the proximal region of the hand. A thick band of connective tissue, the **flexor retinaculum**, spans the distance between each side of the arch and forms the anterior wall of the tunnel. The **median nerve** and all the long flexor tendons passing from the forearm to the digits of the hand pass through the carpal tunnel.

---

## Functions

### Positioning the Hand

Unlike the lower limb, which is used for support, stability, and locomotion, the upper limb is highly mobile for positioning the hand in space.

The shoulder is suspended from the trunk predominantly by muscles and can therefore be moved relative to the body. Sliding (protraction and retraction) and rotating the **scapula** on the thoracic wall changes the position of the **glenohumeral joint** (shoulder joint) and extends the reach of the hand (Fig. 7.3). The **glenohumeral joint** allows the arm to move around three axes with a wide range of motion. Movements of the arm at this joint are flexion, extension, abduction, adduction, medial rotation, and lateral rotation.

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The **clavicle** acts as a strut, holding the shoulder region away from the trunk to maximize the range of motion of the arm. This function is emphasized by the fact that the clavicle is the most commonly fractured bone in the body.

### Upper Limb Functions

- **Positioning the hand**: Mobility for reaching and orienting the hand in space
- **Fine movements**: Precise control of hand and finger muscles, primarily originating in the forearm
- **Structural support**: The clavicle and sternoclavicular joint provide a stable base for upper limb movement

---

## Component Regions（組成區域）

The upper limb is organized into the following regions:

### Shoulder（肩部）

The area of upper limb attachment to the trunk, formed by the **clavicle**, **scapula**, and **humerus**, and their associated muscles and ligaments.

### Arm（臂部）

The part of the upper limb between the shoulder and the elbow joint. Contains the **humerus** and associated muscles, arteries, veins, and nerves, including the **biceps brachii**, **triceps brachii**, and **brachial artery**.

### Forearm（前臂）

The part of the upper limb between the elbow joint and the wrist joint. Contains the **radius** and **ulna**, with anterior compartment muscles (flexors) and posterior compartment muscles (extensors).

### Hand（手部）

The part of the upper limb distal to the wrist joint. Contains the **carpal bones**, **metacarpal bones**, **phalanges**, and associated joints, muscles, tendons, arteries, veins, and nerves.

---

## Contents of the Axilla（腋窩內容物）

The axilla is an important transitional region containing:

- **Neurovascular bundle**: Including the **brachial plexus**, **axillary artery**, **axillary vein**, and associated nerves
- **Lymphatic vessels and nodes**: Drainage from the upper limb and lateral thoracic wall
- **Muscles**: Including the **pectoralis major**, **pectoralis minor**, **subscapularis**, **serratus anterior**, and **latissimus dorsi**

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The axilla is a common site for lymph node dissection in breast cancer surgery and for brachial plexus nerve blocks used in anesthesia.

## Arm（臂部）

### Bones

The arm contains the **humerus**, the longest bone of the upper limb.

### Muscles

- **Biceps brachii**: Flexes the elbow and supinates the forearm
- **Triceps brachii**: Extends the elbow
- **Coracobrachialis**: Flexes and adducts the arm
- **Brachialis**: Flexes the elbow

### Arteries and Veins

- **Brachial artery**: Main arterial supply to the arm; continuation of the axillary artery
- **Deep brachial artery (profunda brachii)**: Accompanies the radial nerve
- **Brachial veins**: Accompany the brachial artery

### Nerves

- **Musculocutaneous nerve**: Innervates the flexor muscles of the arm
- **Radial nerve**: Innervates the extensor muscles of the arm
- **Median nerve**: Passes through the arm to the forearm
- **Ulnar nerve**: Passes medially down the arm

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The radial nerve is closely related to the **humerus** and is vulnerable to injury in humeral shaft fractures. Damage causes wrist drop due to paralysis of the wrist extensors.

---

## Elbow Joint（肘關節）

A hinge joint formed by the distal humerus and the proximal radius and ulna. It allows flexion and extension.

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> **Supracondylar fractures** of the humerus are common in children and may damage the brachial artery or median nerve.

---

## Cubital Fossa（肘窩）

A triangular depression anterior to the elbow joint. Contents (from lateral to medial):

- **Tendon of the biceps brachii**
- **Brachial artery** (divides into radial and ulnar arteries)
- **Median nerve**

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The cubital fossa is the site for measuring blood pressure and for venous cannulation. The **median cubital vein** is commonly used for venipuncture.

---

## Forearm（前臂）

### Bones

- **Radius**: Lateral bone; involved in wrist rotation
- **Ulna**: Medial bone; forms the elbow joint with the humerus

### Anterior Compartment of the Forearm（前臂前筋膜室）

**Muscles** (superficial to deep):

- **Pronator teres**
- **Flexor carpi radialis**
- **Palmaris longus**
- **Flexor carpi ulnaris**
- **Flexor digitorum superficialis**
- **Flexor pollicis longus**
- **Flexor digitorum profundus**
- **Pronator quadratus**

**Arteries and Veins**:

- **Radial artery**: Lateral side of the forearm
- **Ulnar artery**: Medial side
- **Anterior and posterior interosseous arteries**

**Nerves**:

- **Median nerve**: Passes between the two heads of pronator teres
- **Ulnar nerve**: Passes through the ulnar side of the forearm
- **Radial nerve**: Divides into superficial and deep branches

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> **Carpal tunnel syndrome** results from compression of the **median nerve** at the wrist. It causes numbness in the thumb, index, middle, and lateral half of the ring finger, with weakness in thumb opposition.

### Posterior Compartment of the Forearm（前臂後筋膜室）

**Muscles**: All extensors of the wrist and fingers

- **Extensor carpi radialis longus**
- **Extensor carpi radialis brevis**
- **Extensor digitorum**
- **Extensor digiti minimi**
- **Extensor carpi ulnaris**
- **Supinator**
- **Abductor pollicis longus**
- **Extensor pollicis brevis**
- **Extensor pollicis longus**
- **Extensor indicis**

**Arteries**: Posterior interosseous artery

**Nerves**: **Radial nerve** and its deep branch (posterior interosseous nerve)

---

## Hand（手部）

### Bones

**Carpal Bones（腕骨）** — Two rows:

- **Proximal row** (lateral to medial): **Scaphoid**, **Lunate**, **Triquetrum**, **Pisiform**
- **Distal row** (lateral to medial): **Trapezium**, **Trapezoid**, **Capitate**, **Hamate**

**Metacarpal Bones（掌骨）**: Five bones numbered I–V from thumb to little finger

**Phalanges（指骨）**: Fingers I–V; thumb has 2 phalanges; fingers have 3 phalanges each

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The **scaphoid** is the most commonly fractured carpal bone and is prone to avascular necrosis due to its limited blood supply. Fractures typically occur from a fall on an outstretched hand (FOOSH injury).

### Joints

- **Wrist joint (radiocarpal joint)**: Between the radius and proximal carpal row
- **Intercarpal joints**: Between carpal bones
- **Carpometacarpal joints**: Between carpal and metacarpal bones
- **Metacarpophalangeal (MCP) joints**: Between metacarpals and proximal phalanges
- **Interphalangeal (IP) joints**: Between phalanges

### Carpal Tunnel and Structures at the Wrist（腕隧道及腕部結構）

The **carpal tunnel** is bounded:

- **Posteriorly, laterally, medially**: Carpal bones (arched)
- **Anteriorly**: **Flexor retinaculum** (transverse carpal ligament)

**Structures passing through the carpal tunnel**:

- **Median nerve**
- **Tendons of flexor digitorum superficialis** (4 tendons)
- **Tendons of flexor digitorum profundus** (4 tendons)
- **Tendon of flexor pollicis longus**

**Structures superficial to the flexor retinaculum**:

- **Tendons of flexor carpi radialis** (in its own groove)
- **Ulnar artery and ulnar nerve** (in Guyon's canal)
- **Palmar cutaneous branches**

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> Compression of the **median nerve** in the carpal tunnel causes carpal tunnel syndrome — the most common peripheral nerve compression syndrome. Risk factors include repetitive hand use, pregnancy, diabetes, and hypothyroidism.

### Palmar Aponeurosis（掌腱膜）

A thick, triangular fibrous sheet on the palm, continuous with the **palmaris longus** tendon. It protects underlying structures and anchors the skin.

### Palmaris Brevis（掌短肌）

A small muscle in the superficial palm that wrinkles the ulnar side of the palm and protects the **ulnar nerve**.

### Anatomical Snuffbox（鼻煙窩）

A triangular depression on the lateral aspect of the wrist, bounded by:

- **Extensor pollicis longus** (ulnar border)
- **Extensor pollicis brevis** and **abductor pollicis longus** (radial border)
- **Styloid process of the radius** (proximal border)

**Contents**:

- **Radial artery**: Crosses the floor
- **Scaphoid bone**: Forms the floor
- **Superficial sensory branch of the radial nerve**

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The anatomical snuffbox is clinically important because the **scaphoid bone** lies at its floor, and scaphoid fractures often present with tenderness in this region.

### Fibrous Digital Sheaths（纖維性指鞘）

Sheaths that hold the flexor tendons to the phalanges. They form synovial sheaths for the tendons and are attached to the phalanges at the joints.

### Extensor Hoods（伸肌腱帽）

Extensor expansions over the dorsum of the fingers. They distribute the pull of the extensor tendons evenly over the phalanges.

### Muscles of the Hand（手部肌肉）

**Intrinsic muscles** (originate and insert in the hand):

- **Thenar muscles** (thumb): **Abductor pollicis brevis**, **flexor pollicis brevis**, **opponens pollicis**, **adductor pollicis**
- **Hypothenar muscles** (little finger): **Abductor digiti minimi**, **flexor digiti minimi brevis**, **opponens digiti minimi**
- **Lumbricals** (4 muscles): Flex the MCP joints and extend the IP joints
- **Interossei** (7 muscles): **Dorsal interossei** (abduct fingers) and **palmar interossei** (adduct fingers)
- **Adductor pollicis**

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> **High median nerve palsy** (Ainbridge syndrome) causes loss of thumb opposition and weakness of lateral finger flexion. **Ulnar nerve palsy** causes claw hand deformity due to loss of the interossei and lumbricals.

### Arteries and Veins of the Hand（手部動靜脈）

**Arteries**:

- **Superficial palmar arch**: Continuation of the **ulnar artery**; gives off digital arteries
- **Deep palmar arch**: Continuation of the **radial artery**; lies deep to the palmar aponeurosis
- **Digital arteries**: Supply the fingers

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The **superficial palmar arch** is incomplete in approximately 20% of individuals. The **deep palmar arch** is complete in most people and provides important collateral circulation.

**Veins**:

- **Dorsal venous network**: Drains into the **cephalic vein** (lateral) and **basilic vein** (medial)
- **Palmar venous arches**: Accompany the palmar arches

### Nerves of the Hand（手部神經）

- **Median nerve**: Supplies the thenar muscles and first two lumbricals; sensory to palmar surface of lateral 3.5 fingers
- **Ulnar nerve**: Supplies the hypothenar muscles, interossei, adductor pollicis, and last two lumbricals; sensory to medial 1.5 fingers
- **Radial nerve**: Sensory to the dorsum of the hand and fingers (lateral 3.5 dorsally)

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> The **median nerve** is most vulnerable at the wrist (carpal tunnel) and elbow (pronator teres syndrome). The **ulnar nerve** is most vulnerable at the elbow (cubital tunnel) and wrist (Guyon's canal).

The axilla can be palpated by abducting the arm and feeling the depression between the pectoralis major and latissimus dorsi.

The **brachial artery** can be palpated medial to the **biceps brachii** tendon in the arm.

### The Triceps Brachii Tendon and Position of the Radial Nerve（肱三頭肌腱和橈神經位置）

The **triceps brachii tendon** is palpable posterior to the elbow. The **radial nerve** passes deep to the triceps brachii in the radial groove of the humerus.

### Cubital Fossa (Anterior View)（肘窩前面觀）

The cubital fossa is bounded laterally by the **brachioradialis** and medially by the **pronator teres**.

### Identifying Tendons and Locating Major Vessels and Nerves in the Distal Forearm（前臂遠端肌腱及主要血管神經定位）

- **Radial artery**: Palpable at the wrist, lateral to the flexor carpi radialis tendon
- **Ulnar artery**: Palpable medial to the flexor carpi ulnaris tendon
- **Median nerve**: Between the flexor digitorum superficialis tendons
- **Radial nerve (superficial branch)**: Subcutaneous on the lateral forearm

### Normal Appearance of the Hand（手部正常外觀）

The hand should be examined for symmetry, swelling, deformity, and muscle wasting.

### Position of the Flexor Retinaculum and the Recurrent Branch of the Median Nerve（屈肌支持帶和正中神經返支的位置）

The **flexor retinaculum** spans from the **pisiform** and **hook of hamate** (medially) to the **scaphoid** and **trapezium** (laterally). The **recurrent branch of the median nerve** supplies the thenar muscles.

### Motor Function of the Median and Ulnar Nerves in the Hand（正中神經和尺神經在手部的運動功能）

- **Median nerve**: Thumb opposition, lateral finger flexion
- **Ulnar nerve**: Finger abduction and adduction (interossei), MCP flexion, finger extension at IP joints

### Visualizing the Positions of the Superficial and Deep Palmar Arches（淺深掌弓位置）

- **Superficial palmar arch**: Approximately at the level of the fully flexed fingers
- **Deep palmar arch**: Approximately 1–2 cm proximal to the superficial arch

### Pulse Points（脈搏觸診點）

- **Brachial artery**: Medial arm
- **Radial artery**: Lateral wrist
- **Ulnar artery**: Medial wrist

## Clinical Cases — Upper Limb（臨床案例）

### Winged Scapula（翼狀肩胛）

Due to **long thoracic nerve** injury → **serratus anterior** paralysis. The scapula "wings" posteriorly when pushing against resistance.

### Brachial Plexus Nerve Block（臂神經叢神經阻斷）

Used for upper limb surgery. Approaches include interscalene, supraclavicular, infraclavicular, and axillary routes.

### Complication of a Fractured First Rib（第一肋骨骨折併發症）

The **subclavian vessels** and **brachial plexus** pass over the first rib and can be damaged in fractures.

### Carpal Tunnel Syndrome（腕隧道症候群）

Compression of the **median nerve** at the wrist. Symptoms: numbness in the lateral 3.5 fingers, especially at night; weakness in thumb opposition.

> **Clinical Focus（臨床重點）**
> Carpal tunnel syndrome is the most common peripheral neuropathy. Treatment includes wrist splinting, corticosteroid injections, and surgical release of the flexor retinaculum.

### High Median Nerve Palsy（Ainbridge Syndrome / 高位正中神經麻痺）

Loss of median nerve function proximal to the forearm. Causes weakness in thumb opposition and lateral finger flexion.

### Extensor Tendon Injury of the Hand（手部伸肌腱損傷）

Mallet finger (disruption of extensor digitorum tendon at DIP joint) and Boutonnière deformity (disruption at PIP joint).

### Hand Infection（手部感染）

Common sites include the **pulp space** (felon) and **flexor tendon sheath** (pyogenic flexor tenosynovitis), which requires urgent surgical drainage.

### Clavicular Fracture（鎖骨骨折）

The most commonly fractured bone in the body. The **subclavian vessels** and **brachial plexus** are at risk with displaced fractures.

### Pancoast Tumor（肺尖腫瘤 / Pancoast tumor）

A tumor at the apex of the lung that can invade the **brachial plexus**, causing shoulder pain and arm symptoms along the C8–T1 distribution.

# Upper Limb — Segment 11

## Movements（運動總覽）
### Arm Movements（肩部運動）
上肢的主要運動包括：

- **flexion（屈曲）**
- **extension（伸展）**
- **abduction（外展）**
- **adduction（內收）**
- **medial rotation（內旋）**（internal rotation）
- **lateral rotation（外旋）**（external rotation）
- **circumduction（環繞運動）**（見 Fig. 7.4）

> **臨床重點**：肩部的環繞運動結合多軸關節特性，使手臂可進行全方位活動，但同時也使 Glenohumeral joint（盂肱關節）成為人體最常脫臼的關節之一。

### Elbow Joint Movements（肘部運動）
肘關節的主要運動為前臂的**屈曲（flexion）**與**伸展（extension）**（見 Fig. 7.5A）。

### Forearm Movements（前臂運動）
前臂遠端的外側骨——**radius（橈骨）**，可翻越相鄰的內側骨——**ulna（尺骨）**的頭部。由於手與橈骨相連，只需將橈骨遠端交叉跨過尺骨，即可有效地將手從掌心向前（palm-anterior）位置翻轉至掌心向後（palm-posterior）位置（見 Fig. 7.5B）。

此運動稱為**pronation（旋前）**，僅發生在前臂。**Supination（旋後）**則將手恢復至解剖姿勢。

> **臨床重點**：Pronation 與 supination 的動作幅度因人而異，臨床上常以旋前/旋後活動度評估前臂功能。橈骨頭骨折或 proximal radioulnar joint（近端橈尺關節）損傷時，旋前/旋後會受到明顯限制。

### Wrist Joint Movements（腕部運動）
腕關節處，手可進行**abduction（外展）**、**adduction（內收）**、**flexion（屈曲）**、**extension（伸展）**及**circumduction（環繞運動）**（見 Fig. 7.6）。這些動作結合肩、臂、前臂的運動，使手能相對於身體達到極大範圍的姿勢。

---

## The Hand as a Mechanical Tool（手作為機械工具）
手的主要功能之一是抓握（gripping）與操作物體。抓握物體通常涉及手指對拇指的屈曲。根據不同類型的握法，手部肌肉的作用包括：

- **調整來自前臂並插入手指的長肌腱（long tendons）的動作**
- **產生手指內各關節的組合運動**，這是單靠來自前臂的長屈伸肌腱無法單獨完成的

---

## The Hand as a Sensory Tool（手作為感覺工具）
手可用於透過觸覺辨別物體。手指掌側的墊狀區域（pads）含有高密度的**軀體感覺受器（somatic sensory receptors）**。此外，大腦皮質中專門用於解讀手部資訊——特別是拇指——的感覺皮質區域，相對於許多其他皮膚區域而言，所占比例異常地大。

> **臨床重點**：手部感覺皮質的高度錐定表現臨床上可用於評估神經可塑性變化，例如中風康復或截肢後的皮質重組。

## Component Parts（結構組成）
### Bones and Joints（骨骼與關節）
#### 肩帶骨骼（Shoulder Girdle）

肩部的骨骼包括**scapula（肩胛骨）**、**clavicle（鎖骨）**及**humerus（肱骨）**近端（見 Fig. 7.7）。

- **Clavicle（鎖骨）**：內側與胸骨柄（manubrium of sternum）形成關節；外側與肩胛骨的**acromion（肩峰）**形成關節，肩峰拱蓋於肩胛骨的**glenoid cavity（關節盂）**與**humerus（肱骨）**頭部之間的關節（即 Glenohumeral joint）之上。
- **Humerus（肱骨）**：上臂骨頭。遠端與前臂骨形成肘關節（elbow joint），屬於**hinge joint（樞紐關節）**，允許前臂屈伸。

#### 前臂骨骼（Forearm Bones）

前臂包含兩塊骨頭：

- **Radius（橈骨）**：位於外側。
- **Ulna（尺骨）**：位於內側（見 Fig. 7.7）。

在肘關節處，radius 與 ulna 的近端不僅與彼此形成關節，也與 humerus 形成關節。除了屈伸前臂外，肘關節還允許 radius 在 pronation 與 supination 期間於 humerus 上旋轉，同時在 ulna 的頭部上滑動。

Radius 與 ulna 的遠端也相互形成關節，此關節允許 radius 在 hand pronation 時從 ulna 的外側翻轉至內側。

> **臨床重點**：Radius 遠端骨折（Colles' fracture / Smith's fracture）是臨床上常見的損傷類型，常因跌倒時伸手撐地所致，可影響手腕的功能及前臂的旋前/旋後活動度。

#### 腕關節（Wrist Joint）

腕關節由**radius**與腕骨（carpal bones）之間、以及尺骨遠端遠側的關節盤（articular disc）與腕骨之間形成。

#### 手部骨骼（Bones of the Hand）

手部骨骼包括：

- **腕骨（Carpal bones）**：8塊小骨骼，關節間活動度有限，幾乎作為一個整體單位運作。
- **掌骨（Metacarpals）**：5塊，每指一根，是手掌的主要骨骼基礎（見 Fig. 7.7）。
 - 拇指掌骨（Metacarpal I）與腕骨之間的關節為**鞍狀關節（saddle joint）**，賦予拇指比其他手指更大的活動度。
 - 拇指掌骨與食指掌骨之間缺乏強韌的韌帶連接，加上此特殊的鞍狀關節，使拇指能相對於其他手指自由移動。
- **指骨（Phalanges）**：拇指有2節指骨，其餘各指有3節（見 Fig. 7.7）。

#### 手部關節（Joints of the Hand）

- **掌指關節（Metacarpophalangeal joints, MCP joints）**：屬於雙軸**condylar joint（髁狀關節）**（橢圓關節），可進行外展、內收、屈曲、伸展及環繞運動（見 Fig. 7.8）。手指的外展與內收以中指為中心軸定義。
- **指間關節（Interphalangeal joints, IP joints）**：主要是**hinge joint（樞紐關節）**，僅允許屈曲與伸展。

> **臨床重點**：MCP 關節的活動度下降（如關節炎）會嚴重影響握力與手部功能。IP 關節的僵硬則直接影響手指的精細運動能力。

### Muscles（肌肉）
#### 肩部肌肉（Shoulder Muscles）

部分肩部肌肉，如**trapezius（斜方肌）**、**levator scapulae（提肩胛肌）**及**rhomboids（菱形肌）**，連接肩胛骨與鎖骨至軀幹。其他肌肉連接鎖骨、肩胛骨及體壁至肱骨近端，包括**pectoralis major（胸大肌）**、**pectoralis minor（胸小肌）**、**latissimus dorsi（背闊肌）**、**teres major（大圓肌）**及**deltoid（三角肌）**（見 Fig. 7.9A, B）。

其中最重要的是四塊**旋轉袖肌肉（Rotator cuff muscles）**——連接肩胛骨與肱骨並支撑 Glenohumeral joint 的：

- **Subscapularis（肩胛下肌）**
- **Supraspinatus（棘上肌）**
- **Infraspinatus（棘下肌）**
- **Teres minor（小圓肌）**（見 Fig. 7.9C）

> **臨床重點**：Rotator cuff 損傷是肩部疼痛與功能障礙的最常見原因之一，其中 Supraspinatus 肌腱撕裂最為常見，常見於重複性 overhead 活動或外傷。

#### 臂與前臂的肌肉隔間（Muscles of the Arm and Forearm）

臂與前臂的肌肉由筋膜層、骨骼與韌帶分隔為**前間隔（anterior compartment, 屈肌群）**與**後間隔（posterior compartment, 伸肌群）**（見 Fig. 7.10）。

**臂部（Arm）**：
- 前間隔位於前方，由**humerus**及內外側肌間隔（medial and lateral intermuscular septa）與後間隔分開。
- 這些肌間隔與包裹臂部的深層筋膜相連，並附著於 humerus 的兩側。

**前臂（Forearm）**：
- 前後間隔由外側肌間隔、**radius**、**ulna**及**骨間膜（interosseous membrane）**分開，骨間膜連接 radius 與 ulna 的相鄰面（見 Fig. 7.10）。

臂部肌肉主要作用於肘關節，前臂肌肉則主要作用於腕關節及手指。

#### 手部固有肌（Intrinsic Muscles of the Hand）

完全存在於手部的肌肉為**固有肌（intrinsic muscles）**，可產生手指的精細運動，並調節來自前臂肌腱進入手指與拇指的力量。

手部固有肌包括三塊小的**魚際肌（thenar muscles）**，在手掌掌側形成一塊軟組織隆起，稱為**thenar eminence（魚際）**，覆蓋於第一掌骨（Metacarpal I）之上。Thenar muscles 使拇指能相對於其他手指自由移動。

> **臨床重點**：Thenar eminence 肌肉的癱瘓（如 median nerve 損傷）會導致拇指對掌功能喪失，顯著影響握物與捏取能力，臨床上稱為「猿手（ape hand）」畸形。

## Relationship to Other Regions（與其他區域的關係）
### Neck（頸部）
上肢與頸部直接相連。**胸廓上口（superior thoracic aperture）**兩側各有 一個**腋窩入口（axillary inlet）**，由以下結構形成（見 Fig. 7.11）：

- **第一肋骨（Rib I）**的外側緣
- **鎖骨（Clavicle）**的後面
- **肩胛骨（Scapula）**的上緣
- **肩胛骨喙突（coracoid process）**的內側面

上肢的主要**artery（动脉）**與**vein（靜脈）**經由 Rib I 上方及腋窩入口在胸廓與上肢之間通過。主要來自脊髓頸段的**nerves（神經）**也穿過腋窩入口與腋窩供應上肢。

### Back and Thoracic Wall（背部與胸壁）
連接肩部骨骼與軀幹的肌肉與背部及胸壁相關，包括**trapezius（斜方肌）**、**levator scapulae（提肩胛肌）**、**rhomboid major（大菱形肌）**、**rhomboid minor（小菱形肌）**及**latissimus dorsi（背闊肌）**（見 Fig. 7.12）。

胸前壁的**乳房（breast）**與腋窩及上肢有重要關係：

- 乳房覆蓋於**pectoralis major（胸大肌）**之上，該肌肉構成腋窩前壁的大部分，並將肱骨附著於胸壁（見 Fig. 7.13）。
- 乳房的腋側部（axillary process / axillary tail）常延伸繞過 pectoralis major 的外側緣進入腋窩。
- 乳房外側與上部的淋巴主要引流至腋窩淋巴結。供應或引流乳房的數條 arteries 與 veins 也起源於或引流至主要的腋血管。

> **臨床重點**：乳房的腋窩淋巴結引流臨床上非常重要，乳腺癌手術時需評估腋窩淋巴結狀態。腋窩淋巴結腫大也可能反映上肢或乳房的病理變化。

## Key Points（重要考點）
### Innervation by Cervical and Upper Thoracic Nerves（頸椎與上胸椎神經支配）
上肢的神經支配來自**臂神經叢（Brachial plexus）**，由**C5至T1**脊神經的前支形成（見 Fig. 7.14）。此神經叢最初形成於頸部，隨後穿過腋窩入口進入腋窩。最終支配臂、前臂與手的主要神經起源於腋窩處的臂神經叢。

因此，臨床上透過檢查上肢的**dermatomes（皮節）**、**myotomes（肌節）**及**肌腱反射（tendon reflexes）**來測試下位頸椎與T1神經的功能。另一個後果是，下位頸椎神經相關問題的臨床表現——疼痛、針刺感（感覺異常/paresthesia）及肌肉顫抖——會出現在上肢。

#### 皮節（Dermatomes）

上肢皮節（見 Fig. 7.15A）的測試區域（皮節重疊最少的區域）包括：

- **上臂外側區**（Upper lateral region of arm）→ C5
- **拇指掌側墊**（Palmar pad of thumb）→ C6
- **食指墊**（Pad of index finger）→ C7
- **小指墊**（Pad of little finger）→ C8
- **肘部內側皮膚**（Skin on medial aspect of elbow）→ T1

#### 肌節（Myotomes）

選定的關節運動可用於測試肌節（見 Fig. 7.15B）：

| 運動 | 主要控制神經根 |
|------|--------------|
| 肩部外展（Glenohumeral abduction） | C5 |
| 前臂屈肘（Elbow flexion） | C6 |
| 前臂伸肘（Elbow extension） | C7 |
| 手指屈曲（Flexion of digits） | C8 |
| 食指、中指、無名指的外展與內收 | T1 |

#### 肌腱反射（Tendon Reflexes）

在意識喪失的患者中，可使用肌腱反射測試脊神經 levels 的感覺與運動功能：

- **Biceps tendon**（在肘窩內）→ 主要測試 C6
- **Triceps tendon**（肘後方）→ 主要測試 C7

> **臨床重點**：橫膈膜神經支配的主要脊髓 level（C4）恰好位於上肢支配神經 levels 之上。評估上肢皮節與肌節可提供關於C4以下脊髓損傷可能發生的呼吸併發症的重要資訊。

### Major Nerves（主要神經）
臂與前臂的主要肌肉隔間，以及手部固有肌，主要由起源於腋窩處臂神經叢的主要神經之一支配（見 Fig. 7.16A）：

#### Musculocutaneous Nerve（C5–C7）
- 支配**臂部前間隔所有肌肉**

#### Median Nerve（C6–C8, T1）
- 支配**前臂大多數屈肌**
- 支配**手部 thenar muscles**

#### Radial Nerve（C5–C8, T1）
- 支配**臂部與前臂後間隔所有肌肉**

#### Ulnar Nerve（C[7], C8, T1）
- 支配**手部大多數固有肌肉**
- 支配前臂的**flexor carpi ulnaris**及**flexor digitorum profundus 的內側半部**

> **臨床重點**：Median nerve 在腕管（carpal tunnel）內受壓迫導致的腕管症候群（carpal tunnel syndrome）是最常見的上肢神經壓迫病變，表現為拇指、食指、中指及無名指橈側的麻木與刺痛，夜間癥狀加重。

#### 皮節感覺分布（Sensory Distribution of Major Nerves）

各主要神經也攜帶特定皮膚區域的感覺資訊（見 Fig. 7.16B），可用於測試周邊神經病變：

| 神經 | 感覺分布區域 |
|------|------------|
| **Musculocutaneous nerve** | 前臂前外側皮膚 |
| **Median nerve** | 橈側三指半（拇指、食指、中指、無名指）的掌側 |
| **Ulnar nerve** | 尺側一指半（小指及無名指尺側）的掌側 |
| **Radial nerve** | 前臂後表面及手背橈側的皮膚 |

### Nerves Related to Bone（與骨骼相關的神經）
三條重要神經與肱骨（humerus）的特定部分直接相鄰（見 Fig. 7.17）：

- **Axillary nerve**：支配**deltoid muscle**（肩部主要外展肌），從肱骨上部後方（外科頸，surgical neck）繞過。
- **Radial nerve**：支配上肢所有伸肌，從肱骨中部後方斜行通過，位於**radial groove（橈神經溝）**內。
- **Ulnar nerve**：最終目的地為手部，從肱骨遠端內側的骨性突起——**medial epicondyle（內上髁）**後方通過。

> **臨床重點**：這三個部位的肱骨骨折均可危及相鄰的神經。外科頸骨折可能損傷 axillary nerve；橈神經溝區域骨折可能損傷 radial nerve；內上髁骨折或肘關節脫臼可能損傷 ulnar nerve，導致特有的臨床表現（如 "Claw hand" 爪形手）。

### Superficial Veins（淺層靜脈）
位於上肢淺層筋膜中的大型靜脈常用於進入患者血管系統及採血。最重要的包括**cephalic vein（頭靜脈）**、**basilic vein（貴要靜脈）**及**median cubital vein（肘正中靜脈）**（見 Fig. 7.18）。

#### Cephalic Vein（頭靜脈）

- 起源於手背的**背側靜脈網（dorsal venous network）**
- 在拇指底部的**鼻煙窩（anatomical snuffbox）**上方形成
- 繞過前臂遠端外側，到達肢體的前外側面
- 繼續上行，穿過肘部，沿臂部上行
- 進入**鎖骨胸三角（clavipectoral triangle / deltopectoral triangle）**——介於 pectoralis major、deltoid 及 clavicle 之間的三角形凹陷
- 在此凹陷處，於鎖骨下方穿透深層筋膜進入腋窩

#### Basilic Vein（貴要靜脈）

- 起源於手背靜脈網的內側
- 沿前臂後內側上行
- 在肘部下方繞至肢體前面
- 繼續上行，約於臂部中點處穿透深層筋膜

#### Median Cubital Vein（肘正中靜脈）

- 在肘部連接 cephalic vein 與 basilic vein
- 跨越**肘窩（cubital fossa）**的頂部

> **臨床重點**：肘窩處的 median cubital vein 是最常用的靜脈穿刺部位，臨床上常用於採血及靜脈注射。操作時應注意避免損傷鄰近的 **median nerve** 及 **brachial artery**，兩者均通過肘窩深層。
